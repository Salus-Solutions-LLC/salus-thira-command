# Salus Solutions — Chat Startup and Handoff Protocol

## New-chat startup prompt

Paste this into any new Salus chat when needed:

> Continue the Salus Solutions Master Program. Before changing anything, read the Project Instructions and the authoritative records: `SALUS_PROGRAM_CHARTER.md`, `SALUS_DECISION_REGISTER.md`, `CURRENT_HANDOFF.md`, `APPROVED_ARTIFACTS.md`, and `SALUS_CHAT_STARTUP_PROTOCOL.md`. For technical work, inspect the authoritative GitHub repository `Salus-Solutions-LLC/salus-thira-command` and the relevant approved artifacts. GitHub is authoritative for code; approved artifacts are authoritative for design and deliverable intent. Do not reconstruct requirements from assumptions or redesign approved work. First report the current objective, records inspected, deployed state, completed work, open defects, acceptance criteria, exact next action, and any required authorization. Then continue the unfinished work and update the handoff before stopping.

## Mandatory startup checklist

Before modifying anything, the chat must:

1. Read all five continuity records, treating the Charter and the approved Decision Register as one governing program authority under SAL-D036; apply exact decision wording and supersession from the register.
2. Inspect the relevant approved artifacts directly.
3. Inspect current repository and deployment state when the task is technical.
4. Identify conflicts or stale facts.
5. For facility, personnel or other organizational assessment, apply SAL-D030: screen leadership, command, trusted access and insider-risk controls, crisis communications, five mission areas, all 32 FEMA core capabilities and added sector capabilities, plus evidence-linked local-to-global dependencies. Preserve individual privacy and the distinction between no evidence found and not assessed.
6. Inspect the approved Salus system presentation and three-level service framework when scope, packages, proposals, reports or product design are involved. Check requested, authorized and completed integrated package and product level, prerequisites and same-level SalINTEL → SalTHIRA → SalOPS handoffs, evidence/stop gates and report labels under SAL-D031–SAL-D032. Every customer starts with Level 1; Level 2/3 require the completed prior package. Confirm methods and standards before a level-labeled SalINTEL report, SalTHIRA report, SalOPS report and final SalCAPA aggregate under SAL-D033. Keep SalTHIRA phases distinct from service levels and Protected Membership entitlements.
8. For SalINTEL or SalTHIRA work, inspect `SALUS_SALINTEL_SALTHIRA_METHOD_STANDARD.md` and the exact registered Word template version under SAL-D044. Treat the method and member-neutral report structure as approved; treat each populated Protected Member finding and report release as a separate qualification. Check active drafts for method impact without copying member evidence into an enterprise template.
8. Identify current report confidentiality/cover, blank-second-page and customer-education standards under SAL-D034–SAL-D035; confirm all role families, vendors and contractors are considered where relevant. Identify the current approved methodology/template version and relevant reusable cross-domain or cross-sector lessons under SAL-D025; test their applicability, restrictions and conflicts against this engagement rather than copying conclusions.
9. During active authorized SalINTEL work, apply SAL-D038: proactively search relevant public sources; compare them with member files and prior findings; record material contradictions, current source dates, eight evidence dimensions and a dedicated compliance section. Distinguish binding duties from guidance and missing records from violations.
10. Before sharing methods, templates, samples, reports or member materials, apply SAL-D037: verify source/contract rights, intended audience, handling tier, permitted use, disclosed detail and release record. Keep nonpublic implementation separate from the client decision aid, and preserve necessary safety sharing.
11. State the objective, current state, unfinished work, next action, and authorization boundary.
12. Obtain clarification before acting if sources materially conflict.

## Chat-record reconciliation and report update check

At startup, inspect the current candidate-record queue and recently approved decisions, methods, templates and lessons relevant to the objective. A chat, summary or memory note is a lead to verify against source turns and governing artifacts, never self-validating authority. Record source IDs, conflicts, dispositions and review owner for material proposals. Preserve member-specific source and access boundaries.

Before creating or materially revising a report, identify its governed record ID and current version, approved method/template versions, and applicable improvements since the last release. Document the impact decision for active drafts and issued versions. Apply approved changes to the working report; revise or supplement an issued version through controlled review and release, retaining its predecessor. For every report version, complete and retain the SAL-D043 Charter conformance matrix: met, pending with release gate, or not applicable with reason. Check approved name, level/status, product content and handoff, provenance, contradictions, Protected Member handling, confidentiality, Arial/Times New Roman style, cover and blank second page where applicable, page X of Y, accessibility, rendered pages, and DOCX/PDF/print parity where offered. Correct defects before delivery and clearly label any working document with open release gates. Verify DOCX/PDF/print substance and rendered-page quality. Update SalCAPA's report impact and delivery status when that workflow exists; until then, record the same evidence in the handoff and governed records.

## Required learning check

For every material assessment, service or methodology change, record what prior knowledge was used, what new evidence confirmed or challenged it, what member-neutral lesson may transfer to other sectors or domains, and what restriction or human approval controls reuse. Preserve customer originals in their engagement boundary. Do not claim every possible source was reviewed; record the actual collection boundary and unresolved unknowns. See SAL-D025 and the Program Charter.

## Mandatory closeout checklist

After material work, the chat must update:

- `CURRENT_HANDOFF.md` with exact current status and next action.
- `SALUS_DECISION_REGISTER.md` if Marc made a material decision.
- `APPROVED_ARTIFACTS.md` if an artifact was approved, replaced, or superseded.
- GitHub release, issue, or continuity records when technical work changed the product.
- The governed candidate-record and lesson/methodology queue when chats, evidence or outcomes may improve work across Protected Members, sectors or domains; record provenance, conflicts, reviewer, disposition, restrictions and affected reports. No unapproved lesson becomes doctrine or cross-member disclosure.
- The report-impact record when an approved improvement may affect an active draft or issued report; record the update, controlled revision/supplement decision, validation and delivery state.

The final handoff must identify:

- What changed.
- What was validated and how.
- Current production version and relevant commits.
- Known defects or uncertainties.
- Exact artifact and storage locations.
- Any user action or authorization still required.
- The single best next action.

## Recovery rule

If a new chat lacks a file, repository, account connection, or permission needed to verify current state, it must say so. It must not fill the gap by guessing, silently redesigning, or treating old chat memory as authoritative.
