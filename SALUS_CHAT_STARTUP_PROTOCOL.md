# Salus Solutions — Chat Startup and Handoff Protocol

## New-chat startup prompt

Paste this into any new Salus chat when needed:

> Continue the Salus Solutions Master Program. Before changing anything, read the Project Instructions and the authoritative records: `SALUS_PROGRAM_CHARTER.md`, `SALUS_DECISION_REGISTER.md`, `CURRENT_HANDOFF.md`, `APPROVED_ARTIFACTS.md`, and `SALUS_CHAT_STARTUP_PROTOCOL.md`. For technical work, inspect the authoritative GitHub repository `Salus-Solutions-LLC/salus-thira-command` and the relevant approved artifacts. GitHub is authoritative for code; approved artifacts are authoritative for design and deliverable intent. Do not reconstruct requirements from assumptions or redesign approved work. First report the current objective, records inspected, deployed state, completed work, open defects, acceptance criteria, exact next action, and any required authorization. Then continue the unfinished work and update the handoff before stopping.

## Mandatory startup checklist

Before modifying anything, the chat must:

1. Read all five continuity records, treating the Charter and the approved Decision Register as one governing program authority under SAL-D036; apply exact decision wording and supersession from the register.
2. Inspect the relevant approved artifacts directly.
3. Inspect current repository and deployment state when the task is technical.
4. Identify conflicts or stale facts.
5. For facility, personnel or other organizational assessment, apply SAL-D030: screen leadership, command, trusted access and insider-risk controls, crisis communications, five mission areas, all 32 FEMA core capabilities and added sector capabilities, plus evidence-linked local-to-global dependencies. Preserve individual privacy and the distinction between no evidence found and not assessed.
6. Inspect the approved Salus system presentation and three-level service framework when scope, packages, proposals, reports or product design are involved. Check requested, authorized and completed integrated package and product level, prerequisites and same-level SalINTEL → SalTHIRA → SalOPS handoffs, evidence/stop gates and report labels under SAL-D031–SAL-D032. Every customer starts with Level 1; Level 2/3 require the completed prior package. Confirm methods and standards before a level-labeled SalINTEL report, SalTHIRA report, SalOPS report and final SalCAPA aggregate under SAL-D033. Keep SalTHIRA phases distinct from service levels and Protected Membership entitlements.
7. For SalINTEL or SalTHIRA work, inspect `SALUS_SALINTEL_SALTHIRA_METHOD_STANDARD.md` and the exact registered Word template version under SAL-D044. Treat the method and member-neutral report structure as approved; treat each populated Protected Member finding and report release as a separate qualification. Check active drafts for method impact without copying member evidence into an enterprise template.
8. For SalOPS work, inspect `SALUS_SALOPS_METHOD_REPORT_STANDARD.md` under SAL-D045; separate options and report recommendations from authorized, performed and verified operations. For SalCAPA integration and report work, inspect `SALUS_SALCAPA_INTEGRATED_REPORT_STANDARD.md` under SAL-D046; use only approved same-level source reports for issue, reconcile exact versions, and trace decisions to evidence and outcomes. Apply SAL-D047 in both SalOPS and SalCAPA work: connect relevant additional Salus services to specific Member needs, verify current service authority/availability, provide voluntary scoped follow-up and distinguish referral from authorized work. Working Word designs and member examples are not issued.
9. Identify current report confidentiality/cover, blank-second-page and customer-education standards under SAL-D034–SAL-D035; confirm all role families, vendors and contractors are considered where relevant. Identify the current approved methodology/template version and relevant reusable cross-domain or cross-sector lessons under SAL-D025; test their applicability, restrictions and conflicts against this engagement rather than copying conclusions.
10. During active authorized SalINTEL work, apply SAL-D038: proactively search relevant public sources; compare them with member files and prior findings; record material contradictions, current source dates, eight evidence dimensions and a dedicated compliance section. Distinguish binding duties from guidance and missing records from violations.
11. Before sharing methods, templates, samples, reports or member materials, apply SAL-D037: verify source/contract rights, intended audience, handling tier, permitted use, disclosed detail and release record. Keep nonpublic implementation separate from the client decision aid, and preserve necessary safety sharing.
12. State the objective, current state, unfinished work, next action, and authorization boundary.
13. Obtain clarification before acting if sources materially conflict.

## Chat-record reconciliation and report update check

At startup, inspect the current candidate-record queue and recently approved decisions, methods, templates and lessons relevant to the objective. A chat, summary or memory note is a lead to verify against source turns and governing artifacts, never self-validating authority. Record source IDs, conflicts, dispositions and review owner for material proposals. Preserve member-specific source and access boundaries.

Before creating or materially revising a report, identify its governed record ID and current version, approved method/template versions, and applicable improvements since the last release. Document the impact decision for active drafts and issued versions. Apply approved changes to the working report; revise or supplement an issued version through controlled review and release, retaining its predecessor. For every report version, complete and retain the SAL-D043 Charter conformance matrix: met, pending with release gate, or not applicable with reason. Check approved name, level/status, product content and handoff, provenance, contradictions, Protected Member handling, confidentiality, Arial/Times New Roman style, cover and blank second page where applicable, page X of Y, accessibility, rendered pages, and DOCX/PDF/print parity where offered. Correct defects before delivery and clearly label any working document with open release gates. Verify DOCX/PDF/print substance and rendered-page quality. Update SalCAPA's report impact and delivery status when that workflow exists; until then, record the same evidence in the handoff and governed records.

Apply SAL-D059 when closing that matrix: inspect informative-image alternative text, table headers, headings and reading order, relevant navigation/links and every rendered page at full size; compare each offered DOCX/PDF/print form. Record the exact hash/version, tested scope, defects and reviewer. Contact sheets, automated checks and sampled pages alone do not establish release readiness.

## Required learning check

Apply SAL-D063 whenever material research, a new lesson, changed questionnaire/service/report or a product handoff is in scope. Inspect `SALUS_PORTFOLIO_COHERENCE_AND_HANDOFF_STANDARD.md` with the governing records; name exact current versions and affected active/issued work, then check source meaning, level, rating, authority, privacy, plain language and same-ID receiver disposition. Test favorable, adverse, unknown and conflicting cases and ask for a nonexpert readback before closure. Record corrections, unreconciled conflicts, release gates and search boundaries in the handoff. A repository method change does not prove an updated template, issued Member report or deployed workflow.

Apply SAL-D065 when starting or expanding material research. Open `SALUS_RESEARCH_COVERAGE_AND_GAP_CLOSURE_STANDARD.md`; name the decision, level, applicable topics and source classes, dates, Protected Member boundary and reviewer. Search proactively for primary and contrary evidence, including relevant geographic and external dependencies, then maintain the source coverage and stable missing-information registers. Before handoff or report issue, challenge the excluded domains and give each decision-changing gap an owner, proof request, priority and receiver disposition. State the actual bounded search and any unsatisfied gate; do not call a source inventory or a broad map screen a complete assessment.

Apply SAL-D066–SAL-D068 at the item and Member level. Inspect the restricted engagement execution ledger before claiming prior work was performed; trace each material source, claim and decision through original content, linked/contrary evidence, analysis, specific action/proof or reasoned no-change, and same-level receiver dispositions. Distinguish the approved requirement from its demonstrated execution for the named Member and exact report versions. If a domain or source remains unassessed, state its bounded status and next proof, then continue the authorized work rather than declaring customer-level completion.

For a material Member assessment or report change, also apply SAL-D064. Locate the complete actual engagement source location and ledger exact files, versions, restrictions and reviewed/excluded status; do not infer coverage from a synced mirror, source count or prior summary. Reconcile changed operational instructions with the current authority, and keep issued, delivered, understood and acted-on communications distinct. Use historic directories only as protected contact leads, with aggregate reporting and no unnecessary personal or access details. Render and inspect the exact output under SAL-D059, recording the renderer and any native Word/print gap. Preserve historical artifact and model lineage; do not regenerate an older result with newer logic without a qualified new version. Name any source, access, parity or historical-retrieval gate in the handoff.

For every material assessment, service or methodology change, record what prior knowledge was used, what new evidence confirmed or challenged it, what member-neutral lesson may transfer to other sectors or domains, and what restriction or human approval controls reuse. Preserve customer originals in their engagement boundary. Do not claim every possible source was reviewed; record the actual collection boundary and unresolved unknowns. See SAL-D025 and the Program Charter.

Apply SAL-D060 before material Salus decisions and deliverables: consult the controlled thought-leader source registry, conduct a fresh dated search of relevant primary work and credible opposing views, record the selected/excluded lenses and search limits, and reconcile decision-changing ideas against current Salus authority, evidence and Member context. Route any proposed improvement through the governed candidate, human approval and report-impact process. A supplied list is a research lead, not verified doctrine; if it is unavailable, record the access gap and continue with otherwise available primary sources.

For material capability or recovery work, use SAL-D061 to screen actual and emergent roles, upward reporting, expertise, accessible message understanding, minimum-function restoration, system interactions and verified corrective-action closure. The 28 September 2026 reconciliation is a bounded research companion, not a verified roster or Member finding; recheck relevant original works and Member evidence for the present decision.

Apply SAL-D062 for material Salus or Protected Member work: state the decision and authority, source/contrary evidence and falsifier, approved product/level path, specific recommendation and checklist, separate service authorization, observed proof and residual risk. Use the shared operating standard's record fields and preserve one ID across same-level products. Check report and live-system impact separately; method adoption does not establish deployment or a released Member outcome.

## Mandatory closeout checklist

After material work, the chat must update:

- `CURRENT_HANDOFF.md` with exact current status and next action.
- `SALUS_DECISION_REGISTER.md` if Marc made a material decision.
- `APPROVED_ARTIFACTS.md` if an artifact was approved, replaced, or superseded.
- GitHub release, issue, or continuity records when technical work changed the product.
- The governed candidate-record and lesson/methodology queue when chats, evidence or outcomes may improve work across Protected Members, sectors or domains; record provenance, conflicts, reviewer, disposition, restrictions and affected reports. No unapproved lesson becomes doctrine or cross-member disclosure.
- The report-impact record when an approved improvement may affect an active draft or issued report; record the update, controlled revision/supplement decision, validation and delivery state.

The final handoff must identify:

- What changed.
- What was validated and how.
- Current production version and relevant commits.
- Known defects or uncertainties.
- Exact artifact and storage locations.
- Any user action or authorization still required.
- The single best next action.

## Recovery rule

If a new chat lacks a file, repository, account connection, or permission needed to verify current state, it must say so. It must not fill the gap by guessing, silently redesigning, or treating old chat memory as authoritative.

Identify the exact Member output and critical input, ordinary versus stressed operating minimum, source/date and confidence, potential common-mode dependencies, proposed time-to-survive/time-to-recover test, same-level four-product chain ID and controlled action/proof. Do not treat a market feed as an individual supplier assurance, a candidate Word revision as an issued report, or a proposed monitoring cadence as a live service. ## SAL-D069 source-index continuity

## SAL-D071 completion and continuation check

At startup, recover the exact authorized objective, acceptance gates, restricted open queue and last verified source/report versions. Continue the next available action; do not treat the prior chat ending, a draft, or a status reply as task completion. During work, communicate progress without implying release. Before any completion statement, verify every in-scope material item's disposition, exact artifact and report checks, and required human approval. When a real boundary prevents one step, record alternatives attempted, custodian and exact next proof, continue unaffected work, and hand off with the task explicitly in progress or gated. Preserve SAL-D068 limits on privacy, expense and indefinite monitoring.

## SAL-D072 enterprise applicability check

For every Salus task and Protected Member engagement, identify the current approved Program Charter, Decision Register and applicable method/artifact versions before work. Screen the requirements across the task's sector, level, product, service, channel, data handling, output and release path; record the owner and proof for each material application. When a new decision affects existing questionnaires, methods, reports, workflows, services or customer projects, open an SAL-D027 impact review and SAL-D063/SAL-D064 coherence and source-to-release check. Document reasoned not-applicable determinations or governed exceptions with authority, scope, duration and review date; never omit a rule silently. Retain member-neutral learning with provenance and review while keeping each Protected Member's evidence isolated. Do not claim that this startup check by itself has remediated every artifact or completed any Member engagement.

## SAL-D073 individual report baseline and exception check

For every Protected Member report, start from the complete current enterprise baseline applicable to its product and level. List and disposition required sections, evidence, confidentiality/style, cross-product trace and release checks before considering any reduction. Do not infer a request to shorten or waive from price, timing, stakeholder role or a past exception. A specific request may be processed only after the full baseline is prepared, reviewed and retained; verify requester authority, qualified/authorized decision, exact effect, compensating controls and review trigger. Preserve full and reduced versions with labels and lineage. Never waive binding law, safety, privacy, truthful disclosure, professional qualification or human release authority. [SAL-D073]

## SAL-D074 premature-stop prevention

At resumption, read the original authorized objective and restricted open queue before choosing work. If a prior response marked a supporting change complete while the main deliverable remained open, correct that status plainly and perform the next available main-task action. Before any final answer or handoff, compare the entire objective to exact source, artifact, QA and human-release gates. Continue available work; if a real interruption remains, say work in progress with the precise completed portion, open items, owner and next executable action, and persist the same in `CURRENT_HANDOFF.md` and the engagement ledger. Never make a partial-method update sound like delivery of the Member assessment. [SAL-D074]

## SAL-D077 automatic continuation check

At startup, inspect any active same-chat automation for the accepted open objective and compare its prompt, cadence and stop condition with the governed queue. Do not create a duplicate. During an active turn, perform the next available source, analysis, report or QA work. Before closing a work period while material work remains, arrange or update an available authorized follow-up, capture the exact next action and owner in CURRENT_HANDOFF.md, and verify its active target and cadence. On each scheduled run distinguish work actually completed from time between runs; report missed, failed or unavailable runs plainly. Disable the follow-up only after the objective's defined gates, including human release where required, are met. Do not infer 24-hour continuous work, unapproved access or expense from a schedule. [SAL-D077]

## SAL-D078 uninterrupted-processing acceptance check

When the accepted objective requires uninterrupted processing, do not treat an active chat or periodic automation as compliance. Inspect the durable queue, actual run/idle history, last successful source/report update, retry failures, host availability and verified resumption path. Continue useful work in the present turn and keep the scheduled fallback where available, but mark uninterrupted execution not implemented until a persistent, monitored mechanism and its acceptance evidence exist. Record the gap and owner in CURRENT_HANDOFF.md and the controlled capability queue; do not claim continuous work across idle intervals or request that Marc reauthorize the original task. [SAL-D078]

## SAL-D079 enterprise continuation default

At startup, treat uninterrupted processing as required for every accepted Salus work package, including internal and Protected Member work, without a fresh request. Record the specific objective, durable queue/next item, accountable owner, actual persistent-worker state, last successful action and active/idle/failure interval, and exact completion or qualified/human gate. Continue independent authorized items when one task is gated. Only Marc's specific recorded instruction for the identified work and period may change cadence, pause or stop the default. If a persistent mechanism has not been implemented and verified for this workflow, say **SAL-D079 unmet**; the scheduled same-chat follow-up is an interim fallback and must not be called uninterrupted processing. Preserve the Charter's Member isolation, source rights, cost and release controls. [SAL-D078; SAL-D079]
