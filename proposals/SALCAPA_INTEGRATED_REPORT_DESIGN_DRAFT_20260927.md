## Purpose and controlling relationship

The SalCAPA™ report is the decision summary for a completed level. It reconciles approved, same-level SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, and SalOPS™ reports into a useful account of what is known, what matters most, what the Protected Member can decide now, who would act, and what must be learned or authorized next. It links the exact source versions and preserves the source reports as the detailed evidence and analysis records. It must never imply that a working component is issued or that an unapproved option is an operational directive.

The report answers five questions in plain language:
1. What did Salus learn, and how strong is the evidence?
2. What risks, capability gaps, strengths, and time constraints matter most?
3. What response or improvement options are feasible, with ownership, prerequisites, resources, and decision timing?
4. What decisions does the Protected Member need to make now?
5. What specific uncertainty or implementation need would Level 2 or Level 3 work resolve?

## Proposed report architecture

**Front matter.** A Salus Confidential cover identifies Protected Member, exact report name and level, version, date, status, approved recipients, handling and redistribution limits. Page 2 is entirely blank. The report begins on page 3. Nonblank pages use “page X of Y.” The executive summary visibly states the level, that level’s evidence boundary, the top decisions, and any proposed next-level value.

**Executive decision view.** A Bottom Line Up Front section has titled tables subordinate to the BLUF heading: (A) decision priorities; (B) mission-area and capability posture; and (C) immediate, near-term and longer-term action choices. Each table includes evidence strength and source lineage. Narrative explains tradeoffs, dependencies, and material uncertainty.

**Body.**
1. How to use the integrated report and its relationship to the three component reports.
2. Scope, service level, date boundary, and methods.
3. Exact source report versions, approval status, reconciliation, contradictions, and open disputes.
4. Integrated findings: external context, hazards, human and organizational factors, continuity, governance, finance, infrastructure and supply dependencies, strengths and vulnerabilities.
5. SalTHIRA priorities across prevention, protection, response, recovery, and mitigation; ranked core capabilities and targets where established; residual uncertainty.
6. SalOPS action options with objective, decision owner, executor, partners, prerequisites, resources, time, success measures, and authorization status.
7. Sequencing and competing-resource choices, including dependencies on external services and mutual aid.
8. Member decisions and Salus follow-through: urgent matters, bounded actions, accountable owner, target date, and evidence needed to close each item.
9. Next-level opportunity: each proposed task is mapped to a current uncertainty, the decision it enables, deliverable, prerequisite, and separate scope/authorization. The Member may stop after a completed level; any urgent action proceeds on its own merits.
10. Evidence, limitations, document control, and an annex index pointing to details in the source reports rather than repeating controlled material.

A decision/action row should carry stable identifiers linking SalINTEL evidence, SalTHIRA risk/capability assessment, SalOPS option, SalCAPA decision, owner, status, and supersession. This prevents a recommendation from losing provenance as reports evolve.

## Levels and scope

| Level | Integrated SalCAPA™ output | Next-level invitation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Qualified baseline, clearly identified estimates and evidence gaps, ranked priorities, feasible options, and actionable immediate decisions. It does not assert field validation. | Specify which interviews, records, site observations, target measures, or partner confirmations would change a named decision in Level 2. |
| 2 | Validated operational picture and tested/confirmed capability targets, resource and cost comparisons, adopted tasks and owners, and exceptions still requiring Level 3. | Specify exercises, deeper assurance, implementation support, or longitudinal measures needed for Level 3. |
| 3 | Sustained, tested, and governed operating picture, performance evidence, improvement cycle, and residual-risk decisions. | Identify continuing SalOPS™ services or reassessment only when tied to a measurable need. |

Every Protected Member starts with Level 1. Advancement is sequential and separately authorized. A completed level remains a useful decision product even if the Member does not advance. Marketing and pricing may describe the distinct work and value at each level, but a report must not manufacture urgency, guarantee outcomes, or conceal a material risk pending a sale.

## Member education and service presentation

Each major finding explains the concern in ordinary terms: the pathway to harm or disruption, what the current evidence shows, what remains unverified, how the proposed action reduces or clarifies risk, and what it will require from the Member. Show Salus services as scoped solutions to named decisions or implementation needs. Distinguish urgent operational recommendations from optional assessment depth and recurring service options. Do not put price or a contractual commitment into a report unless separately approved and sourced.

## Governance and release gates

Before issue: confirm all three component reports are approved at the same level; verify source version links and contradictions; obtain analyst and authorized human review; check Protected Member isolation, necessary PII minimization, evidence restrictions, legal/compliance qualifications, and action authority; reconcile numbers and ratings; render every page and verify links, tables, blank page, confidentiality treatment, page X of Y, Arial headings/table heads, Times New Roman body/table body/footers, accessibility and print output. Record release in the Decision Register and Approved Artifacts as applicable, preserve immutable version and rollback path, and update handoff. Do not infer approval from a filename or a rendered document.
