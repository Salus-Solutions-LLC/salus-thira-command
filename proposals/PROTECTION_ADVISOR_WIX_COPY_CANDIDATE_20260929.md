## Hero

**Protection Advisor™**

Learn what may matter before you buy an assessment.

Part 1 is free and anonymous. It asks short questions about your people, operations, plans and ability to handle disruption. You receive a preliminary summary of what you reported, what remains uncertain and practical subjects to discuss. Part 1 is not a completed Salus assessment.

If you choose Part 2, create an account to describe the specific site or operation you want Salus to consider. Part 2 gives you a preliminary scope and readiness profile, a possible Level 1 package path and a short list of records to prepare. Salus confirms the scope and price in writing before any paid work begins.

**Action:** Start free Part 1.

## What happens next

1. **Learn:** Complete anonymous Part 1 and save your preliminary summary.
2. **Prepare:** Choose whether to complete the confidential Part 2 scope and readiness profile. You can say “not sure” where you lack information.
3. **Confirm the boundary:** Tell Salus which site, operation or group you want covered. Other locations stay outside the proposed scope unless named in the written agreement.
4. **Decide:** Salus reviews the proposed scope, confirms eligibility, deliverables and a fixed fee, and gives you a choice to accept or decline.
5. **Act:** A paid Level 1 SalCAPA™ package produces four connected, separate reports: SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™ and SalCAPA™. They explain the evidence, likely priorities, practical options, decision owner, next step and proof of completion.

## Two choices shape the paid package

**How broad is the decision?** Band A usually covers one site or operating function and one combined decision. Band B compares two named units or materially different operations. Band C helps leadership set priorities across a defined group. Part 2 may suggest a band. Salus confirms it after reviewing the actual work required.

**How much proof is needed?** Level 1 uses authorized records, suitable public sources and remote clarification to establish a useful baseline. Level 2 adds targeted visits, interviews and validation of decision-changing questions after Level 1 is complete. Level 3 adds separately approved specialist or test work after Level 2 is complete. A wider band does not automatically mean a deeper level.

An eligible, simple **Level 1A Access** package costs **$1,500** after Salus confirms the written scope. Other bands and Level 1A Standard receive a written quote. A Member may act on Level 1 recommendations without buying the next level.

## What you can prepare for a Level 1 discussion

- A simple organization chart showing who reports to whom, who may decide, and each role's responsibilities.
- If available, a site or floor map, emergency or continuity plan, and recent incident summary.
- If available, a list of critical utilities and suppliers. Do not send passwords, access codes, personal directories or sensitive security details through the questionnaire.

Salus requests any further files through an approved, secure intake after the proposed boundary is clear. Missing records are identified as unknowns; they are not treated as proof of a violation.

## Clear limits

Protection Advisor answers are self-reported. Its summary and provisional path are educational decision support, not a verified risk finding, inspection, legal or insurance opinion, certification, emergency response service or promise of safety. A separately scoped SalOPS™ service may be offered when a finding supports it; such work requires its own review and authorization. If there is an immediate threat to life or safety, contact local emergency services.
