# Salus multidisciplinary assurance and assessment review

**Status:** Proposed method and report enhancement for human review, 28 September 2026. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential. **Applies to:** SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™ and SalCAPA™ at Levels 1, 2 and 3. This is a cross-sector research record, not a finding about any Protected Member, a legal opinion, an insurance opinion, a certification, or an authorization to operate.

## Decision from the review

The four approved methods already require organizational governance, five mission areas and 32 core capabilities, evidence quality, eight SalINTEL dimensions, SalTHIRA targets/gaps, SalOPS options and SalCAPA decisions. The gaps are chiefly **precision and traceability**: financial survival, insurability, anti-corruption controls, the exact applicability and edition of legal/engineering duties, accessible and trusted warning, energy endurance, operational behavior, and independent challenge should be explicit in the question set and report outputs. A long questionnaire is not the solution. Level 1 screens these themes with existing evidence and marks unknowns; Level 2 validates selected decision-changing issues; Level 3 examines approved complex interdependencies and tests.

A source's recommendation is not automatically a legal duty. For each potential requirement, SalINTEL must record jurisdiction, regulated entity/activity, threshold, effective date, adopted edition and local amendment, exemption, obligation, evidence, owner, reviewer and recheck trigger. A qualified specialist must assess disputed applicability. Missing evidence is an unknown, not proof of noncompliance.

## Multidisciplinary review matrix

| Perspective | Decision-changing questions and evidence to consider | Product handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise risk managers and board advisers | What objectives and risk appetite/tolerance control decisions? Who owns each material risk, aggregate exposure and risk acceptance? Are correlated hazards, opportunity costs, assumptions, leading indicators, controls and residual risk visible? Test whether risk reporting changes strategic or capital choices. | SalINTEL strategy/governance evidence; SalTHIRA scenario portfolio and sensitivity; SalOPS treatment alternatives; SalCAPA board decision and monitoring. |
| Compliance, anti-bribery and investigations counsel | Which actual jurisdictions and activities create duties? Examine gifts, donations, public-official and procurement contact, third-party diligence, conflicts, beneficial ownership where relevant, records, payment approval, reporting channels, nonretaliation, evidence preservation and independence of investigations. Separate lawful preventive review from person-specific allegations and counsel-controlled work. | SalINTEL applicability and restricted-case register; SalTHIRA fraud/corruption scenarios and consequence; SalOPS approved controls/referrals; SalCAPA authority, privilege/access and corrective-action proof. |
| CFOs, CPAs and audit leaders | Model interruption cost by duration and season, cash runway, debt covenants, payroll, receivables, liquidity, capital maintenance backlog, reserves, contingency funding, fraud segregation and approval authority. Distinguish gross loss, net loss, funded recovery, and uncertainty. | SalINTEL financial/dependency profile; SalTHIRA loss and recovery assumptions; SalOPS option cost, cash timing and funding; SalCAPA capital allocation and audit trail. |
| Insurance brokers, underwriters and claims specialists | Map insured entities, locations, assets and activities to actual policy periods, limits, sublimits, deductibles, exclusions, waiting periods, named perils, business interruption, contingent interruption, flood/wildfire/cyber, liability, animal/aviation and contractor coverage as applicable. Compare replacement cost, exposure, claims documentation, notice duties and restoration funding gaps. Do not infer coverage from policy title. | SalINTEL policy/claim evidence and restricted terms; SalTHIRA uninsured and timing scenarios; SalOPS mitigation and claim-ready records; SalCAPA risk-transfer decision. |
| Engineers, architects and construction experts | Verify site, asset age, condition, occupancy/use, load path, drainage, flood/heat/wind/wildfire exposures, utility interfaces, maintenance and repair backlog, design drawings, permits, inspections, change of use, code adoption and amendments. Technical adequacy requires licensed specialist and field review where needed. | SalINTEL asset/design and code evidence; SalTHIRA failure modes and service consequences; SalOPS inspection/retrofit options; SalCAPA capital project and permit gate. |
| Federal, state, tribal and local regulators | Determine actual regulated role, competent authority, jurisdiction, threshold and effective rule. Consider worker safety, fire/building, environmental/chemical, water, land use, accessibility, privacy, sector licensing, reporting, recordkeeping, inspection and grants/contract conditions. Maintain a duty-to-evidence crosswalk and specialist validation. | Dedicated SalINTEL compliance section; SalTHIRA consequence of credible noncompliance; SalOPS corrective option; SalCAPA owner, due date and verified closure. |
| Emergency management and public safety | Reconcile private command with NIMS/ICS and public agency authority. Establish notification, evacuation/shelter, resource typing, mutual-aid terms, credentialing, inaccessible roads, medical transport, competing incidents, cost/reimbursement, exercises and after-action improvement. Do not assume requested mutual aid will arrive. | SalINTEL partner/resource facts; SalTHIRA surge and shortfall; SalOPS coordination and exercise; SalCAPA decision and proof. |
| Homeland security and critical infrastructure | Identify specific assets and credible intentional threats without converting generic threat narratives into a local finding. Include cyber/physical/insider convergence, essential lifelines, upstream/downstream and foreign supply dependencies, protective intelligence source reliability and safe information sharing. | SalINTEL bounded threat/dependency screen; SalTHIRA intentional and compound scenarios; SalOPS proportional protective options; SalCAPA governance and restriction. |
| FBI and behavioral threat professionals | Provide a way to receive and triage concerning behaviors, protect reporting and evidence, use multidisciplinary assessment and lawful intervention, and refer imminent danger appropriately. Avoid profiling, labeling a person dangerous from protected traits, or treating a behavioral concern as a criminal finding. | Restricted SalINTEL case intake; SalTHIRA scenario without unsupported attribution; SalOPS qualified threat-management referral; SalCAPA protected decision record. |
| Energy assurance and utilities | Map grid, fuel, gas, water, communications, OT and transport interdependencies. Record critical loads, generator/battery capacity, fuel logistics, transfer testing, outage endurance, maintenance, restart sequence, supplier priority and common-mode failure. | SalINTEL energy/dependency register; SalTHIRA outage-duration targets/gaps; SalOPS backup and fuel options; SalCAPA investment and proof. |
| Sociologists and community researchers | Map stakeholder groups, trust, informal networks, service access, social vulnerability and local conflict without stereotyping individuals. Test whether governance and outreach reach workers, residents, visitors, vulnerable groups and neighboring communities; use aggregate data and lived-experience interviews with safeguards. | SalINTEL member/community context; SalTHIRA distribution of consequences; SalOPS engagement and warning; SalCAPA stakeholder decisions. |
| Behavioral scientists and human-factors experts | Test whether people understand warnings and can perform required actions under stress, fatigue, low literacy, poor connectivity, language and conflicting cues. Examine alert source trust, message timing, accessible modes, confirmation, drills, role overload and workarounds. | SalINTEL actual communication/performance evidence; SalTHIRA behavioral capacity; SalOPS message/testing design; SalCAPA proof of reach and action. |
| Business continuity and recovery experts | Define essential functions, minimum viable service, maximum tolerable disruption, recovery time/data objectives, alternate people/sites/processes, suppliers, manual modes, succession, restoration dependencies and validation exercises. Consider reputation, market share and long-term recovery. | SalINTEL BIA and dependencies; SalTHIRA targets/current capability; SalOPS restoration options; SalCAPA recovery priorities and funding. |
| Cybersecurity, data privacy and AI assurance | Identify sensitive information, systems/OT, identity and access, backups, third-party/cloud dependencies, incident response, data integrity, information sharing, AI-assisted decisions and human oversight. Apply exact legal/privacy regimes only after applicability review. | SalINTEL source/security limits; SalTHIRA cyber/data scenarios; SalOPS control and recovery options; SalCAPA access, audit and release. |
| Public health, medical and accessibility experts | Consider medical access, surge, worker/public health, well-water and environmental exposure, accessible warning/evacuation/transport/shelter, service animals and confidentiality. Use aggregate planning data before person-level records. | SalINTEL population/health context; SalTHIRA life-safety targets; SalOPS accessible response; SalCAPA responsible partner and evidence. |
| Environmental, climate and animal-health experts | Examine current and future hazard conditions, cumulative exposure, ecological effects, chemical and waste duties, water quality, animal disease/biosecurity, animal evacuation/care and trade/continuity impacts where applicable. | SalINTEL environmental/animal interface; SalTHIRA sector scenarios; SalOPS mitigation/response; SalCAPA investment and residual exposure. |
| Procurement, logistics and supply-chain experts | Test critical vendor concentration, geographic correlation, alternate capacity, lead times, counterfeit/integrity risk, sanctions/export applicability, emergency purchasing authority, contract step-in rights and delivery during regional demand. | SalINTEL vendor/contract lineage; SalTHIRA shortage targets; SalOPS alternatives; SalCAPA purchase authority and outcome. |
| Independent reviewers, ethicists and affected stakeholders | Challenge source selection, favorable evidence, model/rating bias, confidence, data rights, conflicts, restricted disclosure and who bears the risk of an option. Record dissent and unresolved uncertainty without manufacturing consensus. | All four products: analyst challenge, limits, dissent and review trail. |

These are **applicability screens**, not a claim that every Protected Member needs every specialist or full Level 3 analysis.

## Level and report rule

| Level | Required treatment of every applicable lens | Report expression |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Initial Pass | Screen relevance using existing authorized material and open sources; identify owner, high-consequence unknown and specialist trigger. | One concise multidisciplinary applicability table; qualified top findings; bounded missing-information and Level 2 collection questions. |
| 2 Focused Assessment | Validate the selected decision-changing lens through scoped records, interviews, partner confirmation or qualified observation; reconcile contradictions. | Evidence-to-scenario-to-option trace, specialist opinion and residual uncertainty for selected themes. |
| 3 Deep Dive | Examine approved correlated/compound failure, distributional effects, stress/sensitivity, capital and operational alternatives; test or independently challenge as scoped. | Integrated decision record, measurable performance, tradeoffs, residual risk and monitoring trigger. |

Every report must distinguish **already required/covered by existing method**, **newly required screen**, **not applicable**, **not assessed**, **reported**, **inferred**, **verified**, and **disputed**. Do not turn a candidate concern into a client-specific finding without source and review. Confidential/person-level or tactical evidence remains in a restricted annex or separate governed record.

## Required Salus product changes for review

1. **SalINTEL™:** Add a multidisciplinary applicability and authority register with lens, objective, source boundary, law/standard status, scope, owner, evidence, contradiction, specialist trigger and SalTHIRA input. Expand finance/insurance, anti-corruption, engineering/code, human behavior/accessibility and energy endurance prompts in the existing organizational, eight-dimension and compliance sections.
2. **SalTHIRA™:** Add portfolio correlation, distribution of consequences, funding/recovery feasibility, common-mode lifeline failure and behavioral performance to scenario/target/gap/option analysis. Preserve all six approved steps and all 32 capability applicability decisions; no unsupported numeric precision.
3. **SalOPS™:** Require proposed options to state operating authority, competent/licensed owner where needed, legal/permit/insurance gate, capital and cash timing, public-resource competition, accessible communications, safe stopping condition and proof under stress. No report authorizes action.
4. **SalCAPA™:** Reconcile the four product outputs in a board-ready decision and assurance register: decision, authority, consequence, finance/insurance treatment, cross-domain dependency, chosen or deferred option, owner, evidence, cost basis, due date, verification and residual uncertainty. Keep professional review and Member decision distinct.
5. **Protection Advisor:** Do not add these disciplines as a long public questionnaire. Use existing answers to trigger a few educational statements and a post-commitment evidence request. Reassess form burden before adding any field.
6. **Active reports/templates:** Perform SAL-D027 impact review. Add only applicable member-specific analysis through controlled revision of the existing report identity; do not copy this source matrix as a finding or create duplicate reports.

## Research boundary and open work

This is a broad U.S.-oriented cross-sector review of selected authoritative primary sources, **not a search of the entire internet or a complete census of every law, standard, insurance form or local rule**. It does not validate any Protected Member's permits, policies, cash, engineering, employees, threats or legal compliance. Member-specific source discovery must add the correct state/local/tribal authorities, sector rules, adopted code editions, policy forms and dates, actual counterparties, competent specialists and contrary evidence. The source register and method should be revisited on material law, hazard, service or operational changes.
