# SalOPS Method and Report Design — Proposed v0.1

**Date:** 27 September 2026. **Status:** Candidate design for Marc's review; not approved enterprise doctrine, operational authority, price, deployed software or issued member report. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential.

## Design decision

SalOPS™ converts a same-level SalTHIRA™ priority into a bounded option. It becomes a task only after the Protected Member and Salus record the purpose, authority, scope, owner, resources, safety conditions, cost, information access, success measure and stop rule. The report must distinguish **offered**, **authorized**, **performed**, **verified**, **sustained**, **deferred** and **rejected** states. It must state what was learned and what remains.

The approved Salus presentation supplies four operating domains: Readiness and Preparedness; Protective Operations; Incident and Crisis Response; and Recovery and Sustainment. These organize service delivery. The five mission areas and all 32 core capabilities remain the crosscutting assessment screen, with sector-specific capabilities added when relevant.

## Three sequential levels

| Level | SalOPS report and service depth | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Initial Pass | Ranked options and immediate decision needs; bounded readiness check, single plan or advisory task when separately authorized; proof and Level 2 collection register. | Qualified same-level SalINTEL/SalTHIRA inputs and recorded authorization for any execution. |
| 2 Focused Assessment | Targeted plan, training, exercise, protective or incident-support package; validated partners, targets, costs and corrective actions. | Qualified integrated Level 1 package, named Level 2 scope and separate activity authority. |
| 3 Deep Dive | Coordinated complex or multi-site work, sustained program, recovery sequence, exercise program and measured revalidation. | Qualified integrated Level 2 package, specialist and resource approval as scoped. |

An urgent competent-authority life-safety action is not delayed to sell a higher level. SalOPS report recommendations at any level do not silently authorize live response, staffing, field work, purchases or vendor awards.

## Inputs and handoff

Use exact versions and IDs from SalINTEL and SalTHIRA: source, contradiction, scenario, asset, essential function, approved target, current demonstrated capacity, gap, treatment, priority, uncertainty and residual risk. Add the member's mission/authority, existing plans, people, facilities, partner/contract terms, funding, insurance, permits, accessibility and privacy restrictions. Conditional options must name the missing proof and decision effect. Return task states, actual performance, costs, after-action findings and lessons through SalCAPA for SalINTEL/SalTHIRA retest.

## Option-to-action states

1. **Proposed:** linked gap, outcome, no-action comparison, evidence and uncertainty.
2. **Screened:** safety, legal/privacy, feasibility, resources, cost range, dependencies, partner capacity and competing demand.
3. **Offered:** ranked choice with owner, expected change, tradeoffs, funding/approval gate and verification plan.
4. **Authorized:** accountable decision maker, scope, period, resources, cost ceiling, conditions and stop authority.
5. **Planned:** tasks, command/partner interface, communications, safety, milestones and acceptance.
6. **Performed:** actual activities, dates, deviations, incidents, costs and evidence.
7. **Verified:** measured result against approved acceptance and SalTHIRA target; residual gap and corrective action.
8. **Sustained or closed:** maintenance, retest owner/date, final proof, lessons and immutable record.
9. **Deferred or rejected:** decision authority, reason, residual exposure, interim control and recheck trigger.

Rank options by consequence and time to harm, essential-function effect, expected capability gain, confidence, cost and lead time, accessibility, partner availability, side effects, reversibility and sequence. Explain the judgment; do not imply a validated quantitative score.

## Operating controls

Document member policy authority, Salus lead, incident command or unified-command interface where applicable, alternates, vendor authority, escalation and stop-work. For crisis communications identify trigger, approver, channel, accessible reach, confirmation, correction, media voice and offline fallback. Match work to relevant permits, licenses, contracts, insurance and worker safety. Person-specific, resident, medical, vendor credential and tactical detail remains restricted. A missing record is an unknown, not a proved capability failure or violation.

Use a baseline, approved target, measure, exercise or incident observation, reviewer, due date, acceptance and retest. Corrective actions require owner and closure proof. Feed reviewed member-neutral lessons to SalCAPA without copying Protected Member facts across engagements.

## Proposed stand-alone report structure

Salus Confidential cover identifying approved viewers, use, version, status and authority; **entirely blank page 2**; body from page 3. BLUF with (1) ranked decision and option states and (2) four-domain posture; executive summary; level/scope and predecessor lineage; outcome and decision standard; four-domain analysis; crisis communications; command, people, vendors and safety; ranked option/action register; all 32 capabilities and sector additions; resources, finance and competing demand; schedule and verification; residual exposure/next-level needs; SalCAPA handoff; sources and restricted annexes. The title/front matter and executive summary state the represented Level 1, 2 or 3. Use Arial headings/table headings, Times New Roman body/footers and `page X of Y` on nonblank pages. Perform the SAL-D043 report-specific conformance check and render every page.

## Guidance reviewed as design input

FEMA [NIMS doctrine and guidance](https://preptoolkit.fema.gov/web/nims-toolkit/doctrine-guidance) informs command, coordination, resource and communication interfaces; FEMA [HSEEP](https://training.fema.gov/programs/nsec/hseep/) informs exercises, evaluation and corrective action; FEMA [Continuity Guidance Circular](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/2020-10/continuity-guidance-circular-2018.pdf) informs essential functions, succession and delegated authority; [NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/34/r1/upd1/final) informs information-system contingency and recovery. These sources do not by themselves make the SalOPS method a legal mandate, certification or claim of member compliance.
