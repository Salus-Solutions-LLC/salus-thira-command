## Universal vocabulary and offer rule

Protection Advisor™ Part 1 and Part 2 are free educational preparation. They are not a paid assessment. A paid SalCAPA™ package has four separate, connected reports: SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™ and SalCAPA™. The **level** describes how much evidence and validation Salus performs; the **band** describes the agreed decision boundary and comparison work. Level 1 precedes Level 2, and Level 2 precedes Level 3. Band A generally covers one named site or function; B compares two named units or materially different operations; C addresses a defined group. Salus confirms scope, eligibility, deliverables and fee in writing before paid work.

Only eligible, simple **Level 1A Access** may display **$1,500**, conditional on Salus confirming the written scope. All other packages require a written quote. Do not imply that a wider band automatically entails a deeper level, that Level 3 is a certification, that SalOPS implementation is included in the assessment, or that any insurer will give a discount.

## Page 3: SalCAPA

**Public page:** https://www.salus.solutions/salcapa

**Add near the SalCAPA process description:**

> SalCAPA™ is the connected assessment package and decision summary. Its four separate reports retain the same agreed level, band, customer and scope. The summary identifies what is known, what remains uncertain, the ranked priorities, accountable decisions and evidence needed to show progress. A Level 1 result can support immediate action and identify the questions that justify Level 2. Level 2 can identify work that justifies Level 3. ## Page 4: SalINTEL

**Public page:** https://www.salus.solutions/salintel

**Add near the description of research and evidence:**

> At Level 1, SalINTEL™ uses the agreed records, suitable public sources and remote clarification to give a qualified picture of the named operation. It labels evidence, reasonable assumptions, contradictions and unanswered questions. Level 2 adds targeted firsthand validation where it could change a decision. Level 3 incorporates separately approved specialist findings where the customer needs that depth. The band determines which sites or functions are covered and whether separate comparisons or group priorities are required.

## Page 5: SalTHIRA

**Public page:** https://www.salus.solutions/salthira

**Add near the method explanation:**

> SalTHIRA™ uses the evidence and uncertainty recorded by SalINTEL™ to compare threats, hazards, capability needs, gaps and treatment priorities across prevention, protection, mitigation, response and recovery. Level 1 provides a qualified decision baseline for the agreed boundary. Level 2 tests the decisions that require field validation. Level 3 uses separately approved specialist or test evidence. Each report states its level and band and shows which findings may warrant the next level.

**Public page:** https://www.salus.solutions/salops

**Add near the service explanation:**

> The SalOPS™ report turns the assessment priorities into specific recommendations: what to do, who owns the decision, what resources may be needed and how to verify completion.
