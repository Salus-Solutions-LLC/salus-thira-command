# SalINTEL™ reusable module scope: Facilities, POI and personal protection

**Status:** Internal detailed-design candidate, 2 October 2026. SAL-D096 approves the three module requirements and names, including Facilities Assessment (FA), Personal Protection Assessment (PPA) and Person of Interest (POI); this proposal does not approve a particular case investigation, report template, customer offer or software release. The current Protection Advisor release takes priority. Actual James Avery and Tom Fox case records were not inspected for this proposal; any reusable lesson requires source review, de-identification, provenance and qualified approval under SAL-D025. Marc clarified that the Tom Fox work examined threats **to** a person; it must not be described as a POI insider-threat inquiry.

**Existing authority to preserve:** Salus Program Charter and Decision Register; SAL-D029–SAL-D033 and SAL-D080–SAL-D090 for progressive levels and A/B/C decision boundaries; approved SalINTEL™/SalTHIRA™ method, geospatial, organizational-health, supply-chain, assumptions-to-action and required-information standards. A module is a repeatable research and analysis component of the existing SalINTEL™ report, not a fifth stand-alone product or a second Member report identity. Findings pass through same-level SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™ and SalCAPA™ with stable IDs.

## Common module contract

1. Record the Protected Member, authorized level/band, exact included and excluded site/function/person decision boundary, purpose, decision owner, competent analyst, evidence permissions and handling class.
2. State the Member decision the module supports. Gather only decision-relevant inputs; keep an explicit source search boundary, source date/version, provenance, restrictions, currency, contradictions and coverage gaps. Distinguish reported, observed, tested, assumed and unknown conditions.
3. Explain each material concern in plain language, including why it matters, strengths, alternative explanations, limits and what evidence could reverse the assessment.
4. Carry each material issue through `source → finding/assumption → scenario and mission-area/core-capability effect → option → authority/owner → proof and review` using stable IDs. Pair the recommendation narrative with a specific action checklist. No module output itself authorizes an operational action, adverse personnel decision, purchase or higher-level engagement.
5. Preserve restricted detail in a segregated case/asset annex. The Member-facing SalINTEL™ section presents what decision is supported, the bounded conclusion, confidence, options and next-level validation need. SalCAPA™ reconciles the three product reports without duplicating them.

## Facilities Assessment (FA) module

**Decision unit:** One named site or function, two or more distinct operating profiles, or a defined group needing a shared resource decision. Address count, revenue and headcount are routing signals, not automatic band or risk scores. Do not infer enterprise-wide coverage from one office, warehouse, store or other unit.

**Minimum analysis domains, screened for applicability:**

| Domain | Decision-relevant questions and evidence examples |
|---|---|
| People and operations | Who uses the facility, when, and for what essential functions? How do accessibility, visitors, animals, contractors and vulnerable populations change safe minimum operation? |
| Leadership and personnel | Who owns routine and incident decisions, authority delegation, reporting, crisis communication and recovery? Which staffing, vendor and insider-risk controls are evidenced? |
| Site and physical systems | Boundaries, structures, occupancy, fire/life safety, security, access/egress, drainage and critical equipment; condition, inspection, maintenance and single points of failure. |
| Lifelines and dependencies | Power, water, wastewater, fuel, communications, IT, transport, medical access, key suppliers and alternate arrangements; common-mode and competing-resource failure. |
| Context and hazards | Current mapped terrain, waterways, wildfire, severe weather, nearby activity, hazardous materials, crime and traffic where material; record scale/date and confirm private boundaries before asset-specific conclusions. |
| Governance, finance and compliance | Applicable permits, licenses, standards, insurance terms, repair/recovery funding, contracts, authority and evidence of actual practice; do not infer noncompliance from an absent file. |
| Performance and recovery | Incident history, near misses, exercises, protective controls, response partners, continuity thresholds, restoration time, capital constraints and tested versus planned performance. |

**Level × band design:**

| Path | Facilities evidence and Member decision |
|---|---|
| Level 1A Access/Standard | Source-bound screen of one integrated unit; identify priority scenarios, strengths, unknowns and proportionate options within the signed allowance. Access is deliberately bounded; deeper records/complexity use A Standard or a written scope change. |
| Level 1B | Distinct findings for named sites or operating profiles plus a shared-versus-local controls and dependency comparison. |
| Level 1C | Defined site/function group, common-mode exposure, scarce resources and group investment sequence. |
| Level 2A/B/C | Validate the named Level 1 decision-changing uncertainties through authorized site observation, interviews, record inspection and partner checks for the same qualified boundary. |
| Level 3A/B/C | Commission only approved specialist engineering, legal, laboratory, inspection, exercise or technical tests needed for a consequential question; record professional authority, method, result and residual uncertainty. |

**SalINTEL™ output:** A titled Facilities Assessment section and, when needed, restricted maps/asset annex, with a site/function register, evidence and contradiction table, strengths, priority concerns, plain-language explanations, level/band limits, missing proof and exact next-level questions. **SalTHIRA™ handoff:** each selected issue supplies a causal scenario, affected people/functions, five mission-area and applicable core-capability implications, current capacity evidence, target-setting inputs, dependencies and uncertainty. SalOPS™ receives options and separate service opportunities; SalCAPA™ records Member decisions and proof.

**Intake burden:** Protection Advisor Part 1 stays short and anonymous. Part 2 may capture only the proposed unit, activity, decision and basic records available. After a signed Level 1 scope, request a staged minimum packet such as site/floor map, organization chart with roles/responsibilities, plans, recent incidents, critical utility/supplier list and relevant available permit/insurance summaries; allow “not held/not known,” secure equivalents and justified sector additions. Sensitive plans, maps and personnel details use restricted intake.

## Person of Interest module

**Primary use:** An authorized, case-specific inquiry into a person who may create insider risk, with attention to prevention, protection, response, recovery and mitigation. Also support other authorized case types through separate purpose rules: employment/personnel; vendor/contractor; executive/partner; external-threat; and incident-related. A reported concern or “POI” label is not a misconduct finding or prediction of violence.

**Mandatory case opening:** Exact question and legitimate Member decision; subject identity-match threshold; Salus investigator/license and jurisdiction; permissible purpose, consent/notice and source permissions as applicable; necessity/proportionality; excluded source classes; restricted audience, retention and qualified reviewer. Apply purpose-specific legal review, including employment/consumer-report and nondiscrimination rules where relevant. Marc reports a Texas PI license; its number, class, status and responsible investigator must be verified before a sellable or live case workflow.

**Analysis:** Preserve original evidence and source-to-claim trace; test mistaken identity, recency, credibility, contrary facts and alternative explanations; examine documented behavior and actual opportunity/access in context, protective/supportive factors and control weaknesses. A multidisciplinary qualified reviewer separates a valid safety action from an employment, criminal or other adverse decision reserved to competent authorities. No automated person-level threat score, open-ended bulk dossier, default social-media trawl or unreviewed allegation in a Member report.

**Outputs:** A restricted SalINTEL™ case file with factual chronology, evidence matrix, limits, reviewer determination and lawful options. The ordinary Member report and SalTHIRA™ handoff receive the minimum necessary decision-relevant risk mechanism, scenario assumptions, affected capabilities and control gaps; personal identifiers and sensitive allegations remain restricted unless specifically necessary and approved. SalOPS™ may recommend preventive controls, referral, training, access review, support or incident planning according to the case; a recommendation is not authorization to intervene.

## Personal Protection Assessment (PPA) module

**Distinct purpose:** Assess hazards, threats, vulnerabilities, exposure and protective capacity **affecting a named person**. The prior Tom Fox work belongs conceptually here, subject to actual case-record review before method reuse. “Person Protector” was considered but could be mistaken for an assigned protective service or guaranteed outcome; Marc approved **Personal Protection Assessment** as the module name under SAL-D096. This module may inform separate, authorized SalOPS™ personnel-protection services.

**Case boundary:** Name the protected person or role, why the assessment is authorized, affected work and personal contexts, decision owner, consent/authority, time horizon, geography, family/associate scope if specifically justified, handling restrictions and the safety decisions to support. Do not automatically investigate family, associates or alleged threat actors. Coordinate any source-of-risk person investigation through a separately authorized POI case with its own purpose and controls.

**Screen and analyze:** Travel, residence/workplace and public exposure; predictable routines; physical and digital contact routes; medical/emergency access; communications; transportation; relevant known incidents and credible threat information; protective relationships and existing controls; evacuation and recovery dependencies. Consider natural, accidental and intentional hazards. Distinguish credible evidence from rumor, establish source/date/location and contrary facts, assess exposure and consequence without promising a numerical danger prediction, and identify proportionate protective options. Put exploitable routes, schedules, contacts and security details in a restricted annex.

**Level and handoff:** Level 1 gives a bounded source-qualified safety picture for the signed person/context. Level 2 validates selected decision-changing conditions through authorized interviews, site/route review or partner contact. Level 3 adds specifically approved specialist testing, protective planning, exercise or implementation proof. A/B/C applies to the covered decision units, not a person's prominence or income. SalTHIRA™ receives scenarios and capability implications for prevention, protection, response, recovery and mitigation; SalOPS™ receives options requiring separate authorization; SalCAPA™ tracks the decision and proof.

| Module | Primary question | Subject of concern | Typical output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facilities Assessment | What could disrupt or harm people and functions at a defined site or group? | Site, operation and dependencies | Site-specific evidence, vulnerabilities, strengths and scenarios |
| POI | Could documented conduct, access and context make a person a source of insider or other authorized case risk? | Person as a possible source of risk | Restricted, reviewed case finding with alternatives and lawful options |
| Personal Protection Assessment | What could harm this person, and what proportionate protection would improve safety? | Person as the one to protect | Exposure and protective-capacity picture with options |
