## Customer-facing package explanation

**Level 1 — Initial Pass.** Every new Protected Member begins here. Salus reviews a bounded set of records and public information, identifies what is known and uncertain, evaluates the most material scenarios and capabilities, and presents specific options. The four connected work products are SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™ recommendations and a SalCAPA™ decision summary. For each priority recommendation, Salus explains the evidence and working assumption in plain language, names a credible alternative, and gives a practical checklist showing the decision owner, next step and proof to seek. A Level 1 result is qualified by its source and field-work limits; it is useful now and states precisely what deeper validation would change.

**Level 2 — Focused Assessment.** After a completed Level 1 package, the Protected Member may authorize a focused scope to test selected assumptions with agreed records, interviews, observation, exercises, partner confirmation or qualified specialist work. The four Level 2 products revise the same decision chain and explain what changed, why, and which actions warrant approval.

**Level 3 — Deep Dive.** After a completed Level 2 package, the Protected Member may authorize investigation of approved compound conditions, sustained performance, cascading dependencies and major investment alternatives. The Level 3 products compare tradeoffs and residual risk and define a monitoring and recheck plan. This is a separately scoped engagement, not an automatic upgrade.

**Additional services.** A Member may discuss separately scoped planning, training and exercises, security, investigations, crisis communications, continuity, incident support or other listed SalOPS™ services when a specific finding or Member objective supports them. A recommended service is an option, not a commitment, authorization, response-time guarantee or prerequisite for urgent safety action. Salus confirms personnel, licensing, jurisdiction, partner capacity, schedule and price before offering a service.
