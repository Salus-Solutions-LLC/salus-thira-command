# Salus research coverage and gap-closure standard

**Authority:** SAL-D065–SAL-D072, Marc's 28 September 2026 directions. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential. **Scope:** every authorized Salus research, assessment, methodology, questionnaire, service, report, pricing, marketing and improvement decision at Levels 1–3. This is an approved member-neutral method, not proof that a particular search, Member report, live feature or recurring monitor has been completed.

## Purpose

Salus adds value by actively finding decision-changing information the Protected Member did not know to request or could not readily locate. Analysts shall search across relevant disciplines and geographic scales, reconcile discoveries with Member records and Salus knowledge, explain what changed, and leave a usable route to resolve what cannot yet be established. This is a duty of diligent discovery within the authorized scope, not a claim to have searched the entire internet or found every possible fact.

## Research design and coverage record

Before searching, name the Member or enterprise decision, level, essential functions, time horizon, affected people in aggregate, jurisdiction, sensitivity, responsible analyst and reviewer. Build a topic-by-source coverage matrix from the applicable Salus professional lenses, five mission areas, 32 core capabilities, sector capabilities and Member priorities. Include geographic layers and local-to-global dependencies when location or external systems could change the decision. Do not assume a source class is irrelevant without recording why.

For each material topic, seek and compare as applicable: Member and owner records; current and historical incident, exercise and repair records; competent federal, state and local primary sources; statutes, regulations, permits and adopted standards; maps, imagery and technical datasets; public-service and infrastructure operators; insurance, finance and market records; professional research and opposing views; authorized interviews and field evidence; and approved Salus tools and prior qualified lessons. Search both the expected condition and plausible contrary or failure conditions. Recheck volatile facts before a decision or report issue. Record which sources require a specialist or owner to interpret.

Each source or unsuccessful search records the exact query or source class, publisher/custodian, locator, retrieved and effective dates, version, geographic and population coverage, access/license restriction, evidence type, extraction or inspection method, analyst, result and limitation. Preserve an exact Member source inventory and restricted evidence. A search-result count, prior chat summary, file count or citation list does not prove substantive review.

Inspect the content-bearing parts of each format. Email may carry the operative notice in HTML or an attached document; PDF pages may be image-only; a spreadsheet may contain restricted personal fields. Record which parts were actually parsed, visually reviewed or deliberately withheld. Use automated topic hits to prioritize human reading, never to establish that a domain was assessed or absent. Keep personal records segregated and extract only aggregate, decision-relevant facts for reports.

## Item-level source chain and application

Treat every material claim, concept, requirement, questionnaire item, capability, recommendation, service or customer-facing statement as a research item with a stable locator. Pursue its original authority and useful links: source page, parent record, relevant subpages and folders, underlying dataset or map, applicable law or technical note, earlier and current versions, and credible contrary or adjacent evidence. Search local, regional, national and global dependencies when they could change the decision. Do not stop at a search snippet, an index, a summary, one favorable page, or the content already uploaded. Read and record the operative text, table, map scale, image or attachment. Distinguish a source's assertion from independent verification and from Salus inference.

For each item record the question, source trail and searched boundary, precise finding, contrary evidence, date/currency, confidence, Member applicability, decision effect, alternative interpretations, and what would falsify the conclusion. Reconcile the item with approved Salus authority and the exact Protected Member record. Produce a concrete action or a reasoned no-change disposition with owner, timing and observable proof. Check whether the item changes the Protection Advisor, SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™, SalCAPA™, Levels 1–3, operations, services, pricing, marketing, dashboard or governed learning; mark each material receiver accepted, qualified, returned or inapplicable with reason. A cross-product implication is a review trigger, not authorization for an automatic edit or release.

Before closing an item, the reviewer challenges unvisited links, primary and contrary sources, historical/current change, affected jurisdictions, compound effects, owner/field proof and the consequence of error. If access, rights, competence, privacy or authorization blocks pursuit, record what was attempted, the alternate route, exact missing proof, owner and decision gate. Effort alone never establishes completeness. Convenience, a large source list, a keyword count, absence from the uploads or a generic claim that research is impossible does not close a material item. The accountable analyst must label a bounded conclusion and carry it forward when the evidence remains incomplete.

## Gap discovery and reconciliation

Convert every material unknown into a stable gap ID linked to the decision and same-level SalINTEL™ → SalTHIRA™ → SalOPS™ → SalCAPA™ record. Mark **not found**, **not accessible**, **not collected**, **outdated**, **conflicting**, **too coarse**, **not validated**, **outside approved scope** or **not applicable with reason**. Record what was sought, why it matters, likely custodian, alternate source, handling, collector, due/trigger, decision effect, whether Salus can collect it independently, and what observation would confirm or overturn the working assumption. Distinguish source absence from absence of a capability, duty, asset or hazard.

Compare new information with the exact existing Member record and approved Salus method. Separate genuine contradiction, later change in condition, different geographic scope, different definitions and uncertain inference. Keep source and contrary evidence together. Do not allege noncompliance, misconduct, coverage or physical overlap on a coarse map, outdated record or missing file alone. Escalate a decision-changing legal, engineering, medical, insurance or security question to the competent reviewer.

## Diligence and stopping rule

During an active authorized Member assessment, an interim answer, saved draft, chat-turn ending or handoff is a checkpoint. Continue original and linked source pursuit, contrary checks and cross-product application across work periods without waiting for the Member to repeat the instruction. If a real access, rights, specialist, Member-record or authorization boundary prevents one step, pursue lawful alternatives and unaffected work; carry the exact open item and next action in the restricted execution ledger and current handoff. Do not relabel a bounded checkpoint as completed Member research. This does not create an unapproved background monitor or waive privacy, cost or release authority. [SAL-D068]

Do not close a material search merely because the first plausible source supports the initial hypothesis. Search primary and contrary leads, adjacent jurisdictions and dependencies, historical and current periods, and a second independent source when the consequence warrants it. Continue until the defined decision is supported at its level, available leads have been followed or explicitly triaged, contradictions have a disposition, and additional research is unlikely to change the present decision relative to its cost, delay and authorization. Record that judgment and reviewer. If time, access, privacy, budget or competence stops the work first, label the output **bounded** and retain the open gap, owner and next action. A life-safety concern may require immediate separately authorized protective action while evidence collection continues.

## Levels, products and reporting

- **Protection Advisor:** seek low-burden signals and educate; do not require detailed security maps, personal records or an exhaustive evidence packet before Level 1.
- **Level 1 SalINTEL:** perform a broad, documented source screen, identify material findings and the ranked missing-information register; collect lawful public information proactively and ask the Member only for decision-changing restricted records.
- **Level 1 SalTHIRA:** carry qualified facts and unknowns through all six steps, five mission areas and applicable capabilities; show how missing information affects provisional target, gap and priority.
- **Level 1 SalOPS:** turn high-value gaps into specific collection, validation, protective and service options with owner, authority, timing and proof; distinguish proposals from approved work.
- **Level 1 SalCAPA:** show the Member what is known, what remains unresolved, why it matters now, the decision available at Level 1 and the bounded Level 2 case. Retain one stable ID and receiver disposition.
- **Levels 2 and 3:** validate selected gaps and compound questions under their separately authorized scope; never silently upgrade an unverified Level 1 assertion.

Each stand-alone report's scope and executive summary state the actual search boundary and material blind spots. A dedicated evidence and missing-information section provides concise Member education, source-linked findings, contradictions, ranked collection questions and the next-level or urgent action that each answer enables. Keep detailed tactics and sensitive coordinates in the restricted record. Report impact review applies to active and issued versions whenever a new source changes a finding, ranking, recommendation or customer choice.

## Protected Member execution and completion record

Use the SAL-D069 source index alongside this ledger across every relevant Salus research and service domain. The index stores reusable retrieval metadata: stable ID, locator, date/version/hash, actual reading scope, rights/restriction, linked claims/gaps/products, confidence, contradictions/supersession and next step. The execution ledger records analytical disposition and release proof. Keep a restricted index per Protected Member and a separate member-neutral method/public-source index; promotion requires provenance and human review, not copying another Member's content. Update and improve indexing after each source pass and engagement. A URL, catalog entry or thought-leader name is a lead until the relevant original is checked.

Maintain one restricted execution ledger per engagement and level. It must enumerate the exact source inventory and content-bearing parts actually reviewed, each material item/claim and source trail, domain and five-mission-area/32-core-capability applicability, sector additions, contradicting and unavailable evidence, legal/specialist questions, decision effect, owner/proof, and same-level dispositions into all four reports. Mark every entry **substantively assessed**, **bounded with named gate**, **excluded with reason**, or **superseded with lineage**. Neither automated indexing nor a broad domain label earns substantive-assessment status. Recheck the ledger when new material arrives or conditions change.

Apply SAL-D070 to every material indexed source: read the relevant original pages or records, pursue consequential linked and opposing material, separate evidence from author opinion and Salus inference, and capture transferable concepts, strategies, methods, results and limits. Record the exact source-to-method and source-to-Member decision, including reasoned no-change or exclusion. Priority determines order, not permission to call the remaining queue complete. The source index holds navigation and status; the execution ledger holds analytical proof and same-level product effects. Revisit both when a source changes or a new Member context challenges transfer.

A completion statement names the actual engagement, level, report versions, source boundary, open gates and qualified reviewer. It requires original-source sampling and challenged exclusions, evidence-to-recommendation trace, exact report versions and rendered/native/accessibility checks, and governed human release. State a narrower completed question when other domains remain bounded. Never equate a Charter amendment, a count of files or findings, a draft report, or a large amount of effort with completion of the Protected Member application.

## Quality and learning gate

Before handoff or issue, a human reviewer checks the coverage matrix against the authorized scope, samples original sources, challenges important assumptions and excluded domains, confirms volatile dates, verifies privacy and permissions, and checks that every material gap has an owner or documented reason for deferral. Run the SAL-D063 coherence check and SAL-D064 source-to-release integrity check; then record the exact artifact/version, search boundary, gaps, reviewer, corrections and release status. Feed reviewed member-neutral discoveries into SAL-D025 governed learning while keeping Protected Member evidence segregated. A methodology rule does not establish that a particular Member's research is complete.


## SAL-D071 completion gate

Maintain one explicit in-scope queue of original sources, linked and contrary leads, unresolved contradictions, Member questions, method changes, same-level report effects and release checks. Close an item only with an evidenced finding, reasoned exclusion or no-change, or a documented outside-scope decision made by an authorized reviewer. A named blocker remains open with attempted alternatives, decision effect, owner and next proof. Do the remaining lawful work while that blocker exists. Before saying a task is 100% complete, compare the queue with the agreed deliverable and acceptance gates, validate exact versions, and obtain any required qualified review and human release. State interim status plainly, even when a chat turn or work period ends. Do not describe every possible source on the internet as searched; make the search boundary and remaining leads auditable.

## SAL-D072 use across the enterprise and every Member

Use this standard for every authorized Salus research or assessment workstream and every Protected Member, including changes to existing work. At intake and after each material method or evidence change, record the controlling version, applicable research and report requirements, named owner, source and decision trace, verification and release gate. Record a governed, time-bounded exception or reasoned not-applicable decision if a requirement truly does not govern a specific task. Transfer only approved member-neutral learning with provenance, qualified review and Protected Member isolation. An enterprise method approval does not show that a particular Member's source queue or reports passed the completion gate.
## SAL-D075 four-stream reporting and verification gate

Every research checkpoint, Member report and internal release review shall display a four-row source-coverage account: **public/open-source**, **approved Salus knowledge base**, **thought-leader materials**, and **that Protected Member's own uploads and attachments**. For each row identify the search and original-reading boundary, significant discoveries and contradictions, what changed in the Member or Salus analysis, unreviewed or unavailable material, exact next retrieval, owner and version/date. A zero-result or restricted-access row stays visible with its search trail and decision effect. Separate lead/index count from substantive original review and from findings corroborated or applied. Do not turn inherited method text, an indexed link or a cited upload into proof that its original was reviewed in the current engagement.

The restricted index and execution ledger retain each source's full status, version, pages/sections and receiver decisions. Client front matter and executive summary summarize all four streams, and the evidence/coverage section or restricted annex supplies decision-bearing citations and the unresolved queue without disclosing unnecessary personal data, sensitive coordinates, tactical detail or proprietary method internals. SalINTEL™ originates or reconciles the source account; SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™ and SalCAPA™ accept, qualify or return each material handoff by stable ID and update their own report statements. On new evidence, inspect all affected same-level reports and any issued version under report-impact controls. A qualified reviewer compares the four-row statement with the restricted index, sampled originals, unresolved queue and exact rendered report before human release. [SAL-D075]

## SAL-D076 mandatory source-to-report closure

At completion of the authorized level's source and analytical disposition, reconcile the four-stream index and execution ledger against every existing same-level Protected Member report. For each material finding or conclusion, record source and contrary IDs, qualification and date; its SalINTEL™ evidence/gap treatment, SalTHIRA™ scenario/capability/priority effect, SalOPS™ action/proof option, and SalCAPA™ decision/funding/residual-exposure effect; exact report section/version; and any reviewed no-change, superseded or excluded disposition. Verify that the front matter, executive summary and substantive narrative communicate the decision consequence in plain language and that tables/annexes do not carry the only explanation. Compare recommendations for consistency, conflicts, Member authority and resource competition. Maintain one report identity per product and level.

The qualified reviewer shall sample originals and challenge missing source classes, unresolved linked or contrary material, unsupported certainty, currentness, cross-product omissions, personal-data leakage and conclusions that rest only on an index. Check exact-version native document, rendered pages, accessibility, required fonts, confidentiality, blank second page and page-X-of-Y footer before human release. Mark the Member work in progress while material source or report gates remain open. When new evidence arrives after issue, invoke controlled impact review and revision/supplement lineage. A clearly labeled interim safety finding can be communicated through separately authorized channels without falsely closing the assessment. [SAL-D076]

## SAL-D077 automatic research continuation

For an accepted assessment still open after an active work period, persist the exact source/analysis/report/QA queue and arrange an available same-chat scheduled continuation when authorized. Each run resumes the highest-consequence available item, records actual source and report work, and carries unresolved items forward; it does not merely restate the Charter or announce an intention to research. Track last successful run, missed/failed runs, source boundary and next proof. A schedule is a sequence of bounded executions, not uninterrupted research or completion evidence. Stop the schedule only after the defined assessment and human-release gates are met; preserve privacy, expense, access and professional controls. [SAL-D077]

## SAL-D078 sustained-processing evidence

An uninterrupted research requirement is satisfied only by observed sustained queue processing and automatic recovery, not by the existence of a recurring reminder. Capture checkpoint IDs, last successful original-source/report actions, active and idle intervals, restart/retry events, unresolved queue, owner and stop-gate evidence. A persistent worker or equivalent controlled mechanism must preserve Member segregation, single-writer document updates, bounded retries and qualified release. Where this capability is absent, label the workflow not implemented, use the available scheduled continuation as a fallback, continue active-turn work and keep the capability gap open. [SAL-D078]

## SAL-D079 default across every Salus work package

Apply the sustained-processing and evidence rules above by default to every accepted Salus work package, not only to an assessment for which Marc repeats the instruction. Only his specific recorded direction for the identified work and period changes its cadence or pauses/stops it. Keep working independent authorized source, analysis, report and QA items when another item waits for expertise, rights or human decision. If persistent execution is not implemented and verified for a workflow, label SAL-D079 unmet and report actual idle time; continue available interim work without implying the default has been satisfied.
