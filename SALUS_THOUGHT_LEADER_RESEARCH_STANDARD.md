# Salus Thought Leader Research and Reconciliation Standard

**Authority:** SAL-D060, 28 September 2026. **Scope:** Salus strategy, methods, services, pricing, operations, marketing, Protection Advisor, SalCAPA™, SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™, and Protected Member work at Levels 1–3. This is a member-neutral research method, not a claim that any listed person or work has been verified or adopted.

## Purpose and trigger

Start a new, dated review of relevant thought-leader work for each material Salus research, analysis, design, assessment, recommendation, report, service or program decision. Do not rely solely on a prior Salus summary. Select relevant disciplines and leaders from the controlled source registry, seek additional and opposing perspectives where the decision warrants, and record why other lenses were excluded or deferred. A mechanical formatting or administrative step can reference the last applicable review without repeating an unrelated literature search; its underlying material decision must have a current review. Set depth by consequence, uncertainty, recency, service level, Protected Member scope, lawful access and the decision to be made. A Level 1 review screens and prioritizes; Level 2 validates selected decision-changing claims; Level 3 tests competing models, compound dependencies and sensitivity where authorized.

The Word lists supplied by Marc are **candidate research leads**, not proof of an individual's current role, expertise, position, a work's accuracy, or Salus approval. Register each source file and list entry with provenance, version/hash and rights. Deduplicate cumulative versions without deleting originals. Verify each material person, institution, publication and claimed view against a primary work or directly attributable record before relying on it. When a named leader cannot be verified or a work is inaccessible, mark the limit and continue with other qualified sources. No list is closed or exhaustive.

## Review sequence

1. **Frame the decision.** State the Salus or Member question, product, level, jurisdiction, time horizon, affected people/functions, decision owner and consequence if wrong. Identify current Charter, decision, approved artifact and Member evidence versions before searching.
2. **Map lenses and sources.** Select relevant listed thinkers and adjacent disciplines; include credible critics, different schools of thought, practitioners and governing bodies where relevant. Search anew for the most current and seminal primary works, methods, direct statements and revisions. Record exact queries, repositories, access/retrieval dates, publication dates, editions, paywalls, exclusions and stopping boundary. Use secondary summaries to locate originals, not as unquestioned attribution.
3. **Extract a source-bound claim.** Record each leader's actual problem framing, assumptions, causal model, evidence and limits; recommended strategy, method, intervention or action; stated context; and later correction or disagreement. Separate the leader's words from Salus interpretation. Citation, copyright, license and contractual terms govern quotation, storage and reuse.
4. **Test the claim.** Compare independent empirical evidence, current law and standards, operational experience, competing explanations and known failures. Evaluate applicability to the specific sector, geography, Protected Member, resources and level. Influence or reputation does not establish truth, legal duty, technical fitness or a Member finding. Seek qualified specialist review where required.
5. **Reconcile with Salus.** Link the claim to current Charter/decision IDs, approved methods and artifacts, prior Salus lessons, Protection Advisor, the four product handoffs and any affected reports. Record agreement, conflict, new question, candidate improvement, rejected idea or unresolved uncertainty. State the proposed change, tradeoffs, cost/resource implications, measurable benefit, validation plan, and whether it would alter an active or issued report under SAL-D027.
6. **Govern adoption and reuse.** Create a candidate record with source locators, analyst, reviewer, confidence, restrictions, Member boundary, decision and version. A human approves consequential method, recommendation, marketing, price, service or publication changes. Update the Decision Register, Charter and approved-artifact register as applicable; preserve superseded wording and report lineage. Generalize Member-neutral lessons only after review. Retest adopted ideas against outcomes and fresh research at the next material use.

## Research record and deliverable rule

Retain a dated research ledger with task/decision ID; source-list version; selected and excluded lenses/leaders; search boundary; primary works and exact locators; publication/retrieval dates; claim and contrary evidence; rights; applicability; uncertainty; Charter comparison; recommendation; reviewer/disposition; affected products, services, pricing, marketing, reports and Protected Member records; next refresh trigger. Record missing sources and access limits. A deep review is bounded and documented; Salus shall not assert that it found every work on the internet or understood everything a person believes.

Client-facing work explains the decision-relevant concept in plain language and cites the supporting source where useful. Keep detailed nonpublic Salus synthesis, rubrics, implementation and competitive know-how in controlled internal records under SAL-D037; do not reproduce third-party protected expression or expose one Member's records to another. The lawful competitive advantage is Salus's original, tested integration and measured service performance, not appropriation of a thought leader's ideas or an unsupported uniqueness claim.
