**Authority:** SAL-D120–SAL-D124. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential. **Status:** governed member-neutral navigation artifact; every endpoint, linked record and derivative remains **Unvetted Candidate** until qualified under SAL-D121. **Initial discovery:** 2 November 2026 Central. **Latest candidate discovery:** 9 November 2026 Central. **Weekly maintenance:** active Codex heartbeat `weekly-salus-osint-source-expansion`, Mondays at 09:00 America/Chicago. **Work-triggered maintenance:** every new white paper, inquiry, Part 1, Part 2, assessment and material explicit or inferred topic. **Scope:** worldwide public-source discovery for Protection Advisor, SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™, SalCAPA™, research, strategy, methodology, governed learning and authorized Protected Member work.

## Coverage boundary

Use the [United Nations Member States](https://www.un.org/about-us/member-states) list and [UN M49 countries or areas](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/) as the jurisdiction baseline. The baseline includes the 193 UN Member States, the two UN observer States where represented by the controlling directory, and relevant countries, areas and territories for which competent sources publish data. Names and boundaries follow the source being cited; they do not express a Salus position on recognition, sovereignty or disputed boundaries.

This register is a deep, dated discovery map, not a claim that every website, page or file on the Internet has been found or reviewed. For every country or area relevant to a material decision, screen the applicable global directories below and follow their country entry to the competent national or subnational authority. Record **found**, **no listing located**, **search pending**, **access blocked**, **excluded with reason** or **not applicable with reason**. Absence from one directory is not evidence that an authority, hazard, capability or record does not exist.

## Required endpoint and artifact record

For each useful website, page, API, feed, dataset or file discovered through this register, record a stable endpoint ID; country/area and jurisdiction level; publisher/custodian; exact canonical address and redirect target; source family; purpose; available languages; HTML/API/RSS/Atom/CAP/JSON/XML/CSV/XLSX/PDF/DOC/DOCX/PPT/PPTX/image/map/GIS/archive formats; access, authentication, cost, rate, robots, license and reuse limits; update cadence; first and last observed dates; query and filters; linked originals and contrary sources; Salus-product applicability; personal, security and Protected Member sensitivity; collection method; transformations; hash when lawfully downloaded; and candidate/qualified/superseded disposition.

Treat pages and linked artifacts as separate records when they contain material evidence. For PDFs, Word documents and presentations, preserve the exact download address, title, publisher, date/version, page or section used, file size and hash when downloaded. For photographs, satellite imagery, diagrams and maps, preserve the exact asset and landing-page addresses, creator/custodian, capture/publication time, location/scale, caption, license, manipulation limits and material visual observation. Do not download or mirror a binary merely because it is public; retain only what the authorized purpose, rights, privacy, security and storage boundary permit.

## Required worldwide country-resolution sequence

For each in-scope country or area, begin with UN M49 and then screen, as applicable: UN/WHO country pages; the national statistical office; national disaster-risk authority or Sendai focal point; national meteorological/hydrological service; national mapping/geospatial authority; national CERT/CSIRT; central bank; health and humanitarian profiles; legal and official-gazette sources; sanctions and competent-authority records; business-registration authority; energy, food, communications, transport and civil-aviation sources; trade, logistics and development profiles; and relevant methods, reports, PDFs, datasets, maps and imagery. Follow material content-bearing subpages and files; do not crawl unrelated navigation, accounts, advertisements or prohibited areas.
