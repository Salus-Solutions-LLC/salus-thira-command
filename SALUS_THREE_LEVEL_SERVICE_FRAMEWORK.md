# Salus Three-Level Service Framework

**Status:** Approved program structure, 27 September 2026 (SAL-D029); level methodology and reporting architecture refined under SAL-D031 and sequential package rule under SAL-D032 and staged product/final-report sequence under SAL-D033. Commercial prices, service-specific scope sheets, entitlements and software deployment remain subject to their separate approvals.

## Governing distinction

The approved `Salus System and Methodolgy.pptx` (SHA-256 `7FB44AF954ADFE6AF0C29C90EB577F09F46C5A72EADB96FBBED4FDFF567D0768`) supplies the Salus system and methodology. Protected Members retain decision authority. SalCAPA™ coordinates Collect, Analyze, Prioritize, Act; SalINTEL™ qualifies intelligence; SalTHIRA™ applies its six-step assessment method; SalOPS™ implements, sustains and improves approved capability. Operational results and lessons return to SalCAPA. The deck's three SalTHIRA **phases** group six method steps; they are not the three commercial service levels.

Use **Level 1 — Initial Pass**, **Level 2 — Focused Assessment**, and **Level 3 — Deep Dive** as the common service-depth language. In SalINTEL investigations, these correspond to the existing Tier 1 Initial/Baseline, Tier 2 Enhanced, and Tier 3 Deep-Dive/Comprehensive records in #148. “Level” describes authorized scope, depth and professional effort; it does not describe threat severity, readiness, quality of care, or entitlement to an emergency response. All levels retain source provenance, truth about unavailable information, analyst qualification, human review, member isolation and applicable safety standards.

**Protected Membership plan** is a different commercial dimension. A membership may include, discount or separately quote any level of any service. Do not present the membership plan as an investigation tier, and do not let entitlement silently expand service level, scope, cost or disclosure.

## Three-level service architecture

| Dimension | Level 1 — Initial Pass | Level 2 — Focused Assessment | Level 3 — Deep Dive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer decision | Establish a credible baseline and priority questions | Resolve selected material gaps and validate options | Support a consequential, complex or multi-entity decision with expanded corroboration |
| Scope | One bounded subject/site/system and readily available authorized sources | Named priority issues, selected field/human collection and wider source set | Explicitly bounded multi-domain, historical, dependency and scenario scope |
| Evidence | Source inventory, core checks, contradictions and known unknowns | Targeted verification and stronger corroboration | Broadest lawful approved sources, deeper corroboration, counterexamples and residual-unknown register |
| Salus effort | Short, defined analyst/reviewer allocation | Defined specialist and field allocation as scoped | Multidisciplinary allocation, independent challenge where warranted |
| Deliverable | Concise baseline findings, urgent flags, collection plan and next-level option | Validated findings, prioritized gaps/options, cost and dependency implications | Comprehensive decision record, alternatives, implementation roadmap and limitations |
| Stop rule | Approved checklist completed or item marked unavailable/not applicable | Approved follow-up questions completed or dispositioned | Approved scope and review gates completed; no claim of exhaustive knowledge |
| Change | New need creates a request/estimate | Further work requires approval | New scope or sustained support requires approval |

Every customer begins with a Level 1 package. Level 2 requires a qualified Level 1 package; Level 3 requires a qualified Level 2 package. Earlier authorized and qualified evidence may satisfy applicable lower-level checks through review and documented acceptance, but the lower-level package and handoffs still must be completed and recorded. The level purchased must be recorded for the integrated package and each scoped work product, not inferred from member size or perceived danger. Salus may recommend a higher level when evidence, uncertainty, stakes or consequence warrants it; the recommendation itself is not an upgrade.

| Service | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Common requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SalINTEL™ | Baseline open/authorized source review, member context, major gaps | Targeted human/field/partner requests and discrepancy resolution | Expanded multi-source, historical, network, competing-resource and cross-domain analysis | Use all applicable source channels from the approved graphic; record accessed versus unavailable sources and analyst qualification/validation |
| SalTHIRA™ | Run all six steps at screening depth with provisional targets and gaps | Run all six steps with validated scenarios, capability evidence and prioritized options | Run all six steps with complex scenarios, interdependencies, consequences, alternatives and sensitivity review | Three method phases remain distinct; no step is omitted merely because a lower level was purchased |
| SalOPS™ | Bounded readiness review, action plan or single activity | Targeted plan, training, exercise, protective or incident-support package | Coordinated multi-site/complex operations, exercise program or sustained improvement | Activities remain within Readiness and Preparedness, Protective Operations, Incident and Crisis Response, Recovery and Sustainment; live response and staffing are separately authorized and safety qualified |

Level 1 can reveal an immediate life-safety concern. Escalate that concern through the applicable urgent procedure even when deeper paid analysis has not been authorized. Do not defer required protection or imply a response guarantee from a service-level label.

## Methodology depth and completion gates

Every product has a **level profile** that states its covered entities, time/geography, questions and decisions, sources and exclusions, interviews/fieldwork, specialist needs, expected outputs, hours/capacity, schedule, reviewer, completion checklist and price treatment. The package level is selected and authorized for the integrated SalINTEL → SalTHIRA → SalOPS sequence, with each component's actual scope, deliverable and status recorded. A Level 1 SalINTEL product supports a Level 1 SalTHIRA assessment under all six steps and then Level 1 SalOPS recommendations and options. The same same-level sequence applies at Levels 2 and 3. A product handoff does not silently authorize execution, staffing, fieldwork or live response; the package agreement specifies those commitments. Completion of a component alone is not completion of the integrated package.

| Gate | Level 1 Initial Pass | Level 2 Focused Assessment | Level 3 Deep Dive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | Signed baseline package, covered entity and source boundary | Qualified Level 1 package and approved Level 2 priority questions, controls/scenarios and scope | Qualified Level 2 package and approved Level 3 consequential decision, complex scope, specialist plan and budget |
| Collection | Existing member records, readily accessible authorized official/open sources, limited clarification | Targeted interviews, record requests, selective site observation/tests and wider source diversity as authorized | Broadest lawful approved source set, multi-role/partner collection, technical inspection/testing and historical or cross-domain research in scope |
| Organizational lens | Map roles, command, trusted access, crisis-message authority and essential functions; mark unknowns | Validate selected delegations, staffing, screening/access controls and exercises | Challenge succession, simultaneous failures, insider-control effectiveness, interorganizational dependencies and recovery capacity |
| Capability lens | Screen all 32 FEMA capabilities plus sector-specific ones for applicability and owner; no unsupported ratings | Establish outcome/target and performance evidence for the selected high-priority applicable capabilities; disposition the remainder | Evaluate all applicable capabilities within the approved scope, including cross-capability dependencies, competing resources and measured performance |
| External context | Identify material local-to-global drivers and suspected dependencies | Verify named supplier, public-service, legal/regulatory and economic effects on priority functions | Model cascading and compound disruptions, substitutes, lead times, financial sensitivity and regional/national/global competition |
| Analytical confidence | Source-bound baseline, contradictions and explicit unknowns | Corroborated priority findings and documented residual gaps | Deeper corroboration, counterexamples, specialist challenge and sensitivity analysis |
| Stop | All baseline checks completed, unavailable, excluded or deferred with reason | Every authorized priority question resolved or explicitly dispositioned | All approved modules and review gates complete with residual unknowns and future-monitoring triggers |
| Human gate | Analyst qualification and authorized release | Analyst plus relevant specialist/owner validation and release | Multidisciplinary or independent challenge when specified, accountable decision and release |

A high-consequence finding at any level triggers the appropriate safety or alert route. It does not silently purchase the next level. The same standards for lawfulness, privacy, evidence lineage, protected-member isolation, human review, confidentiality and urgent escalation apply at every level.

### Sequential package workflow and level handoffs

**Level 1:** SalINTEL qualifies the bounded baseline, flags and missing information. SalTHIRA then performs all six steps at Level 1 depth, establishing provisional capability targets, gap assessments, treatments and priorities with visible assumptions. SalOPS supplies Level 1 recommendations and options, and any separately approved urgent or enabling actions. The integrated Level 1 package concludes with an executive decision, an evidence/request register for Level 2, and an analyst/reviewer release. It is an actual level-specific package, not merely preparation for later analysis.

**Level 2:** The client and Salus authorize the named questions and resources surfaced by Level 1. SalINTEL gathers, validates, analyzes and prioritizes that information. SalTHIRA reruns all six steps at Level 2 depth with validated targets and selected performance evidence. SalOPS develops or performs authorized Level 2 activities and records corrective actions. The completed Level 2 package states which gaps remain for Level 3.

**Level 3:** The approved Level 2 record supplies the baseline for complex, specialist and compound-scenario work. SalINTEL, SalTHIRA and SalOPS produce their respective Level 3 analyses, decisions, options and approved operations, with independent challenge as scoped.

Refine and approve the applicable methodologies and report standards first. Then issue the level-labeled SalINTEL report, the corresponding SalTHIRA report, and the SalOPS report in that order. Finally issue the level-labeled SalCAPA report aggregating the three approved product reports into a decision summary. SalCAPA links exact versions, source boundaries, unresolved differences, priorities, action status and next-level needs; aggregation does not erase the underlying product analyses or create a duplicate of a product report. At every level, the SalINTEL report, SalTHIRA report, SalOPS report and final SalCAPA report display their represented level in the heading or front matter and executive summary. Within each material section, state whether a finding is adequate for the current level, requires collection or validation at the next level, or calls for an approved SalOPS action now or as an enabling condition. SalCAPA records the chain of evidence, same-level handoffs, authorization, prices, decisions and status. A higher level reuses and updates qualified earlier work without duplicating a governed report.

### Report architecture and disclosure

Every report begins with: Protected Member and engagement; product and **represented, requested, authorized and completed level**; scope/profile version; decision question; as-of period; completed and excluded work; source boundary; status (working, qualified, approved or issued); reviewer; and handling. If the authorized level is unknown, print **“Level authorization pending”** and do not claim completion. Separate service level from severity/readiness/color status and from Protected Membership plan.

All four reports use a consistent sequence: executive decision that states represented level and prerequisite status; scope and method; facts and source quality; organization and external context; scenarios and capability applicability; level-specific findings and uncertainty; options and costs where authorized; decisions and handoffs; collection needs; source/annex register. The body emphasizes findings material to the purchased level; the source and 32-capability disposition register remain traceable in the appropriate annex/record. Level 1 delivers a concise screening and questions; Level 2 shows validated priority analyses and alternatives; Level 3 shows comprehensive scoped analysis, technical inputs, sensitivity, implementation and residual risk. Length is driven by evidence and decision need, never used as a proxy for level.

Each finding states **observed/verified, reported, inferred, hypothesis, unknown, not assessed or no evidence found within a defined search**, with source/date, confidence, contrary evidence and decision effect. A level report explicitly distinguishes (a) work completed inside scope, (b) material facts not yet validated, (c) work outside scope, and (d) recommended next-level work. A recommended next level is a proposal, not an unfinished current level or automatic commitment. Do not imply that a Level 1 screen proves the absence of insider, security or other hazards.

### Upgrade and quality decision

Recommend Level 2 when a material decision depends on unverified people, infrastructure, command, warning, supply or capability facts, or when conflicting sources can reasonably be resolved by targeted collection. Recommend Level 3 when compound failures, multiple entities, high-consequence engineering/financial/operational choices, materially contested facts, specialist methods or long-term investment require deeper proof. A qualified analyst records the reason, incremental questions, exact new work, expected decision benefit, estimate and who may approve it. Unavailable sources may instead be recorded as residual limits when deeper work would not change the decision.

An engagement may stop after any completed authorized level. A customer may not skip the Level 1 package or enter Level 3 without a completed Level 2 package. When reliable prior evidence already exists, qualify and incorporate it into the required lower-level package; avoid duplicative collection while retaining its review and handoff gates. An urgent life-safety action may proceed under appropriate authority at any time and is not delayed by package sequence.

## Selling and pricing rules

1. Lead every new customer with an actual Level 1 package and explain the sequenced pathway to Level 2 and Level 3. Show each level's outcome, exact covered entities, SalINTEL/SalTHIRA/SalOPS work products, source/field limits, professional hours or capacity, delivery cadence, exclusions, assumptions, customer responsibilities, prerequisites and next decision. Do not market Level 2 or 3 as a stand-alone first purchase.
2. Price the actual work using versioned #154 rate-card/estimate assumptions: human labor, AI/tools, partner/direct expense, contingency, margin and capacity. The issue's seed rates and hours are planning inputs, not published prices.
3. Offer transactional, Protected Membership and hybrid buying paths under #159 while requiring Level 1 as the first service package. A membership may include Level 1 capacity or later-level discounts; Level 2 and Level 3 remain sequential, separately scoped and authorized. Each entitlement and overage rule is explicit.
4. Scale to organization size and complexity under #235. Use site/entity counts, geography, dependencies, risk, desired outcomes and budget; avoid one-size-fits-all bundles or “unlimited” work.
5. Salus personnel approve final scope, price, discount, contract, external partner, service-level change and operational commitment. SalCAPA may propose options but must not auto-charge or silently authorize work.
6. Preserve prior estimate, SOW, report and service-level versions. A change request records why scope changed, affected price/capacity, approver and effective date.

## Required proposal, report and dashboard fields

Every proposal/SOW, work order, work product, report and marketing example displays: Protected Member/engagement and covered entities where appropriate; integrated package level and product-level represented status; prerequisite package and handoff; authorized service level and profile version; question/decision; scope and exclusions; source/collection boundaries; deliverable and review criteria; staffing/field/partner assumptions; schedule/cadence; customer dependencies; price basis and approved customer-facing price where applicable; change trigger; approver; and report/method version. Reports also show actual work performed, evidence and uncertainty, findings, decisions, authorized SalOPS actions and residual questions. Internal cost/margin stay Salus-only.

The SalCAPA dashboard should show requested, authorized and completed integrated package level; prerequisite qualification; the same-level SalINTEL, SalTHIRA and SalOPS work products and handoff state; membership entitlement separately; estimate/SOW reference; available/underway/completed status; checks and gaps; reviewer/approval; enabling actions; change requests; report version and where-used/learning links. Never imply a feature, analysis, response capacity or customer entitlement is deployed because this framework is approved.

## Implementation and review

Apply this framework to new work and run an impact check for active drafts and issued reports under SAL-D027. Update existing governed report records through controlled versions; never create a duplicate report. Service-specific profiles must define measurable checklists and capacity, then receive Salus approval. Marketing and sales material must describe Level 1 as the required first package, Level 2 as dependent on a qualified Level 1 package, and Level 3 as dependent on a qualified Level 2 package, with the same-level SalINTEL → SalTHIRA → SalOPS outputs at each stage. Any active marketing or price material inconsistent with this rule requires review before reuse. Marketing may describe the levels only with truthful scope and availability. Review outcomes, economics, customer comprehension and lessons at renewal/closure and feed approved changes through SAL-D025/026.
