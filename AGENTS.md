# Salus agent execution requirements

These instructions apply to every task and every type of work in this program, including brief replies, status checks, administration, research, writing, code and document edits. No size, materiality or repeat-task exemption.

1. Read `SALUS_PROGRAM_CHARTER.md`, `SALUS_DECISION_REGISTER.md`, `CURRENT_HANDOFF.md`, `APPROVED_ARTIFACTS.md` and `SALUS_CHAT_STARTUP_PROTOCOL.md`; inspect the actual applicable authorities/artifacts. Current governed records control memory.
2. Apply `SALUS_TASK_COMPLIANCE_ASSURANCE_STANDARD.md` / SALUS-TASK-COMPLIANCE-001. The assistant owns applicability, execution, evidence checking, corrections and routine compliance verification. Do not ask Marc to attest that you followed requirements.
3. Verify/reconcile `governance/charter_task_requirements_register.json` with `tools/charter_task_compliance.py`; retain one task record and its evidence outside the source repository. Every source unit must receive a justified, evidence-backed applicability/supersession/completion disposition; decompose multi-obligation units into acceptance checks. Pending is the default.
4. Account for current Knowledge and OSINT inventories and actual internet/website searches, linked-source pursuits, full citations, contrary evidence and useful new ideas. Queries, source-family counts or downloads are not proof of content review. Record current task-specific coverage, blocked content and where used. Preserve source rights and Member isolation.
5. Preserve explicit holds. Compass remains paused pending requested existing records and further authorized resumption; its reader remains disabled. Do not restart assessment research, reader access, ingestion recovery or operations as a side effect of compliance work.
6. Run preflight, progress/handoff and closeout verification; recheck current authority and exact evidence after interruption or change. No complete-compliance/research/release claim on an incomplete receipt. If the tool is unavailable, record that gap and use the same manual obligations; do not fabricate or silently waive checks.
7. Retain all actual output tests, substantive preservation and full exact-file page review where applicable. Preserve controlled source/predecessor bytes. Use current Charter visual standards for every offered report format.
8. Record missed requirements, cause, impact, correction and retest in the same task's corrective-action history. Correct and verify routine deficiencies yourself. Keep source qualification, competent human judgment, risk acceptance, report issue, disclosures, purchases, exact-head merges, deployments and operational actions behind their separate required human authority.
9. Before final response, verify what you actually did, expose any blocked requirement and record durable handoff/capture. Do not claim universal automatic enforcement, production deployment or corpus completeness from a local checker or passing CI.

10. Apply SAL-D139 / SALUS-MODEL-REMINDER-001 before every task, including brief replies and status checks. Visibly remind Marc to switch to GPT-6.2 Astra at the highest available reasoning setting for assessment work, or GPT-6.1 Sol at Medium reasoning for other work. Include assessment-specific research/analysis/recommendations/report work; remind again at mixed-task category changes and when returning to other work. Retain the actual reminder as evidence, classify the current task segment and verify reminder compliance yourself. No acknowledgement gate, automatic-switch claim, silent substitution or assumption of selected model; disclose unavailable settings. Keep Compass paused and the reader disabled.

11. Apply SAL-D143 / SALUS-ANALYTICAL-REVIEW-001 to every task, including assessments, planning, research, facilities/personnel assessments, white papers, speaker proposals, code, administration, status checks and brief answers. Challenge assumptions and strategy weaknesses; compare methodologies/applicability; reconcile conflicting evidence and examine unintended consequences; develop recommendations with clear reasoning and limitations. Retain four task-correlated review records and supporting proof, proportionate to the actual task. Document bounded no-conflict/no-applicable-alternative results honestly; never fabricate one or use blanket not-applicable/empty fields to skip a duty. Verify routine analysis yourself, retaining all human qualification/release gates. SAL-D142 revises only assessment reminder content to GPT-6.2 Astra at highest available reasoning; other work stays GPT-6.1 Sol Medium. Compass remains paused; reader disabled.

12. Apply SAL-D144 / SALUS-TASK-REVIEW-PASSES-001 on every task. After the initial work, perform at least eight distinct substantive re-passes, then at least one separate editorial pass on the final output or communication. Retain the evidence, finding and disposition for each pass. Recheck affected work after material changes and verify the ordered record yourself before claiming completion.

13. Apply SAL-D145 / SALUS-ASSESSMENT-RESEARCH-ENTRY-001 before the first substantive judgment in each Protected Member assessment, including a bounded reassessment. Follow the authorized request and named originals, identify the Member and responsible jurisdictions, and record the four-stream review, actual restricted evidence and research entry before analysis. Reopen affected findings and repeat the checkpoint after material new evidence. Report unresolved candidate status accurately.

Record breaches and bounded evidence limits, never silently reset time, waive review, enable a reader or send. The local record checker is not proof of actual delivery or unattended execution.

17. Use confirmed-minimum/unknown counts when evidence is incomplete. No invented human review or retrospective approval.
