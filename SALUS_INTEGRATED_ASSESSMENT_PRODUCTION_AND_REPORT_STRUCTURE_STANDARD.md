# Salus integrated assessment production and report structure standard

## Single integrated assessment report requirement [SAL-D154]

Owner direction received 27 November 2026. SALUS-ASSESSMENT-SINGLE-REPORT-001 amends SALUS-ASSESSMENT-STRUCTURE-001. Every new assessment and material reassessment shall deliver one integrated SalCAPA™ assessment report as the normal Protected Member report. SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™ and SalOPS™ remain mandatory, distinct analytical functions within the assessment and its controlled evidence record. They are not three additional compulsory client files. Preserve the approved six-step SalTHIRA method, five mission areas, all required capability and professional-lens screens, same-level qualification and the evidence-to-decision chain. An expressly commissioned separate component report remains possible within its approved scope; it is not the default delivery requirement.

Use one principal home for each finding. Bring its source and locator, evidence class, contrary explanation, significance, recommendation, alternatives, proposed owner, authority, resource basis, operational/legal/financial implications, verification, residual uncertainty and reconsideration trigger together. Summaries may point to that home; they must not reproduce the full analysis. Preserve stable finding, scenario, target, option, decision and question identities. SalCAPA integrates leadership decisions; SalINTEL establishes evidence; SalTHIRA examines consequences and priorities; SalOPS develops feasible action and proof. Combining delivery does not collapse these responsibilities or turn instructions into observed performance.

The standard report sequence is: leadership brief; scope and documented strengths; coordination or decision journey relevant to the Member; prioritized findings and recommendations; implementation and dependencies; client financial worksheet where applicable; open questions and limitations; and supporting method, capability, source and assurance appendices. Adapt headings to the decision unit. Keep the main report usable without reading internal audit history. Preserve material limitations beside affected claims and use precise section/ID links to supporting detail. Distinguish source-review status, priority, implementation and approval in every color cue.

Retain historical research batches, administrative checkpoints and superseded editions in the restricted evidence record. Consolidate their current substantive implications in the report and preserve a block-level relocation/disposition account; do not concatenate predecessor reports or delete unique material analysis. Maintain one source account and one blank editable financial worksheet. Preserve source-use rights, exact qualification limits, original documents, approved formatting, graphics with accessible explanations, native navigation and required personnel-review counts. Protected Member document change history compares Member editions, not Salus report editions.

For packaging only, this requirement supersedes conflicting four-file/three-separately-issued-source-report requirements in SAL-D033, SAL-D044–SAL-D046, SAL-D136, SAL-D146 and SAL-D150–SAL-D152 and their method, template, startup and acceptance instructions. Their substantive analysis, distinct functions, sequential assessment levels, required reviews and human qualification/release gates remain. The integrated report's controlled internal SalINTEL, SalTHIRA and SalOPS records must pass the same applicable analytical and qualification gates that previously governed the separate source reports. No source record or component output becomes qualified merely by incorporation.

Apply through recorded impact review to active drafts and prospectively to templates and generators. Already issued reports remain immutable. A single revised engagement or this policy text does not establish enterprise template/software migration. Retain the original 24-hour clock, eight actual substantive review passes, separate terminal editorial/format pass, exact-file and every-page acceptance, source qualification, rights, human approval, review-stop controls and all existing holds. A document revision is not renewed research, operations, spending, reader activation or permission to send. Immediate authorized private report restructuring is permitted; this exact repository implementation remains a candidate until governed publication and promotion.


## Authority and applicability

SALUS-ASSESSMENT-STRUCTURE-001 / proposed SAL-D152. Owner direction received 23 November 2026. Apply immediately to the authorized private assessment revision. This repository implementation remains a controlled candidate until exact-revision promotion. Apply prospectively to every new assessment and material reassessment, the integrated report and its same-level analytical records, templates and generators through recorded impact review. No universal template migration, software enforcement or deployment is claimed.

## One assessment and one integrated report

Maintain one controlled analytical record for each finding, including stable source, scenario, target, gap, option and decision identities, evidence classification, qualification, contradictions, priority, assumptions, recommendation, ownership, resource basis, limitations and verification. Produce one integrated SalCAPA report from that record; retain SalINTEL, SalTHIRA and SalOPS as distinct internal analytical functions. This is an internal assessment-control record, not an additional customer recommendation register. Preserve the approved architecture, Salus six-step SalTHIRA method, five mission areas, required capability screens and level-specific content. SalCAPA remains the coordination environment throughout; evidence production remains SalINTEL to SalTHIRA to SalOPS to integrated SalCAPA qualification and issue.

## Required production stages

1. Define the decision. Record the Protected Member decision, authorized function and geography, population, evidence level, assessment questions, exclusions, acceptance criteria and accountable authority. An incident-specific hypothesis does not silently enlarge the decision unit.
2. Establish source coverage before substantive conclusions. Inventory supplied originals and editions; consider the actual Knowledge Library and OSINT corpus; search relevant public Member and responsible-authority materials and contrary evidence. Record access and rights restrictions, named references, missing sources, dates and the research-entry decision. Counts and search previews do not establish complete review. Do not promise to find everything on the internet.
3. Review and qualify evidence in coherent batches. Distinguish written instructions, self-report, observation, guidance and analyst inference. Resolve material contradictions and source identity before consequential use. Record exclusions with reasons and retain the originals. Existing qualified uses retain their exact limits; a new edition or new use needs its own impact decision and qualification where required.
4. Analyze scenarios and coordination. Follow people, information, authority, resources and accepted responsibilities through credible scenarios. Examine handoffs, shared dependencies, degraded conditions, accessibility, continuing care, recovery and unintended consequences across all five mission areas. Preserve the six-step method and all required applicability screens. Proposed targets are not agreed or demonstrated performance.
5. Compare responses. Compare no-action and feasible alternatives through operational, legal and financial perspectives; challenge assumptions and methodologies, contrary evidence, feasibility and residual risk. State owners, professional boundaries, cash/time/resource assumptions, missing client inputs, acceptance proof and reconsideration triggers. Do not invent probabilities, costs, ROI, legal duties or professional endorsements.
6. Produce and accept the package. Stabilize a coherent analytical batch before generating the integrated report. Reconcile same-level versions and stable identities, perform required substantive and editorial/format reviews, verify exact Word files and every rendered page, and return the package for owner approval. Separate internal delivery, source qualification, human acceptance, external issue and operations.

## Speed with preserved assurance

Review substantial source batches before report production. Apply a dependency-based impact review when a source changes; recheck every affected finding and report passage without treating unaffected material as newly reviewed. Record the exact unchanged baseline and evidence supporting reuse. Research may be marked complete only when defined material source locations and questions have dispositions, relevant contrary evidence has been addressed, consequential uses are qualified and remaining searches are unlikely to change a decision. A review wait under SALUS-ASSESSMENT-REVIEW-STOP-001 may pause work with these gates still open; it never marks that research complete. The assistant records its rationale and remaining release conditions; this rule is not an exception to complete corpus consideration, named-original review, required capture, human qualification or applicable Charter gates.

Keep the original 24-hour start and deadline across versions and interruptions. Establish source access, capacity and qualified-review availability at entry. Escalate an unavoidable missing prerequisite early; record the actual breach or bounded internal return, never silently restart the clock or call an incomplete assessment complete. Missing customer confirmations may remain explicit limitations where permitted; they do not excuse unfinished Salus-owned review.

Retain at least eight substantive re-passes after the initial result: source coverage; qualification and provenance; methodology and assumptions; scenario/capability logic; coordination and affected people; alternatives and unintended consequences; integrated operational/legal/financial reasoning; and evidence-to-decision and internal report consistency. Follow with a separate editorial and format pass. Each pass needs actual scope, evidence, corrections or a reasoned no-change result. Repetition, a version increment or eight labels alone is not assurance. Material changes reopen affected checks.

## Required review stopping and feedback cycle

SALUS-ASSESSMENT-REVIEW-STOP-001 / SAL-D153. Owner direction received 23 November 2026. Apply to each assessment, material reassessment and related source-review, assurance and report-revision cycle. Incorporate the checkpoint in stages 1, 3 and 6: plan the decision and resource boundary, stabilize a coherent evidence batch, then decide whether another internal action or Protected Member feedback offers the next material benefit. This is a mandatory process requirement; repository promotion and evidence of execution remain separate.

At entry, identify the decision to improve, required quality gates, material unknowns, available access/review capacity and time/cost limits. Retain the original 24-hour start, deadline and any breach. Do not promise an undefined level of perfection or treat the volume of work, version count or repeated successful checks as proof of decision value.

At each consolidated checkpoint, name the actual next internal action, the uncertainty or defect it could resolve, expected effect on a finding, priority, recommendation, reader understanding or mandatory acceptance requirement, and its likely effort, delay, cost and prerequisites. The comparison may be qualitative; do not invent a financial return or a numerical stopping score. Correct known decision-changing errors and mandatory defects within current authorization. A tool/access failure requires diagnosis and a bounded disposition, not repetitive paid runs or report rebuilding.

When further optional refinement has low expected material benefit relative to cost/delay and Protected Member review, edits, comments or existing records are the next material input, pause that refinement. Preserve one exact review baseline, its source-use limits, findings, priorities, verified results, editable worksheet and formatting. Keep open items, owners, decision effects and the next required proof visible. A known acceptance defect remains a defect; a source-access gap remains open. Do not call waiting completed research or use it to waive a mandatory check. An explicit owner stop or pause takes effect immediately, even if a checkpoint or required review remains incomplete.

Record the review-wait disposition in the existing restricted task account, not a new client recommendation register: authorized decision and scope; exact report versions/hashes; completed review and evidence references; unresolved requirements and affected release claims; the next input and responsible role; stopping rationale and resource boundary; actual pause instruction/state; sending and reader restrictions; and the conditions and authority needed to resume. The assistant owns this routine record and conformance check. Do not ask the owner to attest that the assistant performed ordinary compliance.

Return the preserved package for internal owner review only within existing handling authority, with its actual readiness and unresolved limits. Owner review of drafts, qualified source-use adjudication, risk acceptance, exact-file acceptance, permission to contact the Protected Member and external report issue remain separate decisions. A timely bounded return satisfies only its stated delivery claim. Neither this rule nor a pause authorizes sending, new source access, spending, monitoring or reader activation.

On authorized resumption, consolidate the Protected Member's feedback and new evidence into one coherent batch. Trace each material change to affected sources, assumptions, findings, scenarios, targets, options, decisions and report passages. Reopen affected qualification and acceptance gates, perform the required corrective review and final editorial/format checks on the changed outputs, and reuse unaffected exact verified results within their recorded versions, rights, currency and scope. Do not count reused results as new reviews, silently waive a required review or mechanically restart a full review/report cycle for every comment. Retain the existing eight substantive and terminal editorial review requirements; any newly commissioned assessment or reassessment retains its own applicable review obligations.

Resume only under an actual instruction covering the paused work. Receipt of comments, the arrival of a proposed review date, a repository merge or an automatic notification does not revoke an explicit owner pause. Record changes to the Protected Member's document editions separately from Salus report-production changes. Do not regenerate reports solely to record a pause, software diagnostic or administrative status change that has no report-impacting substance.

## Required integrated report outline

1. Leadership brief: conclusion, principal choices, urgency, limits and next decision.
2. Scope and documented strengths: decision unit, people, source baseline, level and exclusions.
3. Coordination journey: people, information, authority and continuing responsibilities through the relevant event or decision.
4. Prioritized findings and recommendations: one substantive home for each stable finding and its integrated reasoning.
5. Implementation: options, alternatives, accountable roles, dependencies, resources, fallback and ID-specific proof/checklists.
6. Financial worksheet where applicable: one blank editable set of client inputs, supported ratios, assumptions and no invented ROI.
7. Open questions and limitations: existing-record requests, decision effects, owners and honest release conditions.
8. Supporting appendices: six-step SalTHIRA analysis, five mission areas, full required capability screens, detailed source account, transfer limits, qualification and actual review history.

SalINTEL establishes what is known and its limits; SalTHIRA explains consequence, priority and capability; SalOPS explains feasible action and proof; SalCAPA integrates the decisions. Identify their contribution in this report and the internal record without recreating four repeated reports. Keep critical safety limits near the claim, not solely in an appendix. Retain historical batch progress privately.

## Reading depth and presentation

Give each report a first-page purpose, principal conclusion, material uncertainty and next decision; a main report containing decision-relevant reasoning; and supporting appendices containing detailed evidence, calculations and review records. Use a concise linked reading route. Keep required limitations beside the claims they affect. Preserve needed standalone context while assigning detailed analysis one principal home.

Use finding, consequence, supporting basis, response, owner, verification and limitation as a consistent explanatory sequence. List immediate and consequential needs first, explaining the order and dependencies. Keep priority, confidence, source status, approval and implementation separate. Red/yellow/green indicators need a defined dimension and words; unknown or unassessed performance remains explicit.

The communication objectives of SAL-D146 remain mandatory wherever relevant. Select and combine graphics, perspectives, timelines, comparisons and call-outs to help the reader; a separate repeated graphic, page or table for each method in every report is not mandatory. Record the applicable objective, chosen expression, retained location and any reasoned alternative. This refines representation only and does not delete a substantive duty. Preserve native navigation, accessible alternatives, all evidence limits and the Charter's OPM-referenced Salus typography, branding, table treatments, page rules and editable financial controls. Protected Member change history compares customer editions, not Salus report production versions; state first-review status when no predecessor is available.

## Acceptance and adoption

Retain the pre-edit inventory, original report hashes, block-level relocation/change account, required-content crosswalk, finding/priority/source/qualification preservation checks, worksheet control identities and blank-state proof, native navigation and page validation, eight actual passes and terminal editorial/format evidence. Inspect every page and correct defects before claiming visual acceptance. An unfamiliar reader or specialist review may be reported only when it occurred; unavailable feedback stays explicit. Historical approval does not approve the current edition or sending.

Keep Member evidence and reports private. No reader activation, new source access, spending, runtime change, deployment, source promotion or external sending follows. Exact-revision promotion remains separate. Apply the approved requirement through an artifact-family impact queue and distinguish specified, candidate-revised, rendered/verified, approved and deployed states. Update continuity and artifact records without representing a local amendment or one revised package as enterprise-wide implementation.
