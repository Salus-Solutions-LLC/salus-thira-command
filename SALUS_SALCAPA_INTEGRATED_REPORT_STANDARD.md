# SalCAPA™ Integrated Report Standard

**Status:** Approved enterprise process and report structure, effective 27 September 2026 (SAL-D046); multidisciplinary assurance amendment approved 28 September 2026 (SAL-D050); organizational-health method approved 28 September 2026 (SAL-D051); analytical assumptions-to-action amendment approved 28 September 2026 (SAL-D055). **Owner:** Salus Solutions. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential. Apply with the Program Charter, Decision Register, approved system presentation, three-level framework and same-level product standards. This standard does not issue a Protected Member report, approve its findings, deploy dashboard functionality, set a price or authorize operations.

## Purpose and controlled process

SalCAPA™ is the secure coordination, decision, action and evidence environment. Its integrated report is the decision summary for a qualified service level. After approved same-level SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™ and SalOPS™ reports, it reconciles what is known, what matters, feasible choices, who may decide or act, what proof will close an action and what remains. It links exact source report versions and stable IDs; the detailed product reports remain the evidence and analysis authorities. Do not create a duplicate report or treat a working component as issued.

The integrated workflow is: collect and control sources in the correct Protected Member boundary; qualify SalINTEL™ evidence; assess with SalTHIRA™; develop and, if separately authorized, perform SalOPS™ options; reconcile the three approved products; approve a Member decision; track action state, owner, resource, evidence, outcome and residual risk; return verified results and reviewed lessons to the governed learning loop. Preserve provenance, contradictions, human review, immutable releases, supersession, access controls and auditability.

## Required decisions and traceability

Under SAL-D065, the integrated report shall show the actual research boundary and the highest-value unresolved information beside the decisions it could change. Reconcile the same stable gap IDs and receiving dispositions from SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™ and SalOPS™; identify who will collect or validate each item, what proof is needed, when to reconsider the decision and which Level 2 or Level 3 scope is justified. A count of documents or citations is not a completion claim. Keep the detailed research log in the restricted Member record and explain the material limits plainly to the Protected Member.

The report shall answer:
1. What was learned, by which source and with what confidence or limit?
2. Which threat, hazard, essential-function, capability, governance and dependency issues deserve priority?
3. Which action or service options are feasible, with tradeoffs, competing resources and decision timing?
4. What must the Protected Member authorize, own, defer, reject or verify now?
5. Which specific uncertainty, implementation need or performance question would a higher level resolve?

Each material decision/action row has stable links to SalINTEL™ evidence and contradiction IDs, SalTHIRA™ scenario/capability/target/gap IDs, SalOPS™ option and state, SalCAPA™ decision, responsible authority, due date, proof, residual exposure and supersession. Reconcile disagreements explicitly. A later source may reflect changed conditions rather than a contradiction; record the dates and decision effect.

## Sequential level depth

| Level | Integrated report outcome | Next-level case |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Initial Pass | Source-bound baseline; ranked strengths, risks, capability gaps, feasible options, immediate decisions, estimates and evidence gaps. Do not imply field validation. | Name interviews, records, site observations, target measures or partner confirmations that would change a particular Level 2 decision. |
| 2 Focused Assessment | Validated operational picture for the selected scope; confirmed targets/performance and partner assumptions; cost/resource comparisons, adopted tasks and remaining exceptions. | Name separately approved exercise, assurance, implementation or longitudinal proof needed for Level 3. |
| 3 Deep Dive | Sustained/tested operating picture, performance evidence, improvement cycle and residual-risk decisions for the approved complex scope. | Offer continuing SalOPS™ work or reassessment only for a named measurable need. |

Every Protected Member begins with Level 1; Level 2 follows a qualified integrated Level 1 package, and Level 3 follows a qualified integrated Level 2 package. A completed level is useful on its own. Higher levels and operational services require distinct scope and authorization. Urgent safety action is not held for commercial advancement. Describe the value of additional work through a named uncertainty, decision, deliverable and expected use; do not manufacture urgency, guarantee outcomes or conceal material risks. Price or contractual commitments appear only when separately approved and sourced.

## Cross-service discovery and Member choice [SAL-D047]

The integration step shall compare qualified SalINTEL™ needs, SalTHIRA™ priorities and SalOPS™ options against the current approved Salus service catalog. Use `https://www.salus.solutions/services` and its relevant linked service pages for the current public service names and descriptions, and record the access date. Recheck the site before any member-facing revision; validate actual service authority and capacity separately. For each relevant additional service, record the source finding, Member outcome sought, why the service fits, immediate versus next-level timing, prerequisites, limits, service owner and authorization path. Show how a service could change the Member's decision or capability; do not present a list of offerings without a specific connection to this Member. Examples may include licensed armed security where lawful and appropriate, unarmed protective services, planning, training, exercises, crisis communications, continuity, recovery support and separately authorized specialist assessment or investigation. Verify licensing, insurance, jurisdiction, personnel/partner availability, conflicts, price and scope before making a commitment.

The report shall have a **Relevant Salus services and Member choices** section, distinct from required safety advice, SalOPS options already offered and sequential Level 2/3 assessment. Use a concise table with finding or need, service and expected benefit, prerequisites and evidence limit, decision owner and voluntary next step. Include a simple route to ask Salus for a scoped discussion or proposal; record acceptance, deferral or rejection in SalCAPA™. A referral is not authorized work, a guaranteed outcome, a package entitlement or a reason to delay urgent protective action. The Member may decline additional services without losing the value of the completed assessment. Price and contracting remain separately approved.

## Multidisciplinary decision and assurance register [SAL-D050]

The integration step shall compare the applicable professional lenses in `proposals/SALUS_MULTIDISCIPLINARY_ASSURANCE_REVIEW_20260928.md` against the exact approved same-level source reports. A board-ready decision row shall show objective and consequence, source and confidence, legal/technical/insurance applicability status, financial and cash effect, affected groups, common dependencies, chosen/deferred option, competent authority and specialist, resource/funding source, due date, proof, residual exposure and dissent or unresolved uncertainty. Separate the Member's risk acceptance from Salus analysis and from regulator, insurer or licensed professional determinations. Summarize only the material, applicable lenses in the client report; retain the full applicability and source register internally or in a restricted annex. At Level 1, unresolved decision-changing specialist questions become a bounded Level 2 collection plan, not a generic sales claim.

## Organizational health decision summary [SAL-D051]

The integrated report shall summarize material verified strengths, leadership/authority/process gaps, unknowns and contradictions from the same-level organizational-health assessment. Show their effect on daily work, stress scenarios, capability targets, SalOPS™ options and the Member's decision. For each selected change, link source and finding IDs to the mechanism, authorized owner, funding/resource need, expected benefit, measure, retest, residual exposure and next-level validation. Do not average away a critical failure or imply that an offered service is authorized work. Keep sensitive role and personnel evidence in its protected source record.

## Protection Advisor and organizational-health trace [SAL-D052]

Where an organizational-health concern began in Protection Advisor, preserve the question and version as a preliminary origin only. Link the Member-specific SalINTEL™ validation, SalTHIRA™ scenario/capability effect, SalOPS™ option and state, and SalCAPA™ decision, owner, proof and residual uncertainty. Reconcile a later finding that differs from the initial answer without erasing either record. The integrated report describes the qualified result and its limits, not a questionnaire-derived organizational rating.

## Missing-information and lens assurance trace [SAL-D053]

The integrated report shall disclose material decision-changing information not yet obtained and identify its source owner, sensitivity, collection level, interim decision and review trigger. Reconcile the applicable professional lenses, organizational domains and same-level source report IDs in the existing assurance register. Do not claim completeness from a generic checklist or duplicate the Member's evidence files in the integrated summary.

## Required stand-alone report structure

Name the report `[Protected Member customer name] - SalCAPA Level [1, 2, or 3]`. The title/front matter and executive summary state represented level and requested/authorized/completed status. The Salus Confidential cover identifies approved viewers, permitted use and distribution, storage, restricted annex treatment, version and release authority. **Page 2 is entirely blank**, and the body begins on page 3. Nonblank pages use `page X of Y`. Use Arial for title, headings, running headers and table column headings and Times New Roman for body, table body, notes and footers. Apply brand, accessibility, navigation and rendered-page checks.

The executive Bottom Line Up Front has numbered, descriptive table titles subordinate to the BLUF heading, with a one-sentence purpose and repeating column heads as needed:
- Decision priorities and requested authority.
- Five-mission-area and applicable core/sector capability posture, with evidence strength.
- Immediate, near-term and longer-term action choices, with owner, resources, dependencies and state.

The body covers:
1. How to use this summary and the three source reports.
2. Scope, level, methods, dates, boundaries and confidence.
3. Exact source versions and approval states; reconciliation, material contradictions and unresolved disputes.
4. Integrated findings: external context, hazards, people and leadership, governance and finance, continuity, infrastructure, supply chains, assets, strengths and vulnerabilities.
5. SalTHIRA™ priorities across prevention, protection, response, recovery and mitigation; applicable core and sector capabilities, targets where established, gaps and residual uncertainty.
6. SalOPS™ options with objective, owner, executor, partners, prerequisites, time, resources, cost basis if approved, success measure and authorization state.
7. Sequence, dependencies, competing public/mutual-aid resources and no-action consequences.
8. Member decisions and Salus follow-through: urgent matters, bounded actions, accountable owner, target date and closure proof.
9. Next-level or continuing-service opportunity, each mapped to a specific uncertainty or implementation decision, deliverable, prerequisite and separate scope/authorization.
10. Relevant Salus services and Member choices, with evidence-linked service fit, verified availability conditions and an optional request-for-scope route.
11. Evidence, limitations, document control and restricted annex index. Point to source reports instead of copying unnecessary sensitive detail.

Each major finding teaches the Protected Member in plain language: pathway to harm or disruption, current evidence, contrary or missing information, practical choices, what a choice requires and what information would change it. Distinguish observed fact, report, inference, estimate, unknown, not assessed and verified outcome. Include material compliance issues only at their qualified status; do not present an unsupplied permit as a violation. Minimize PII and keep person-specific records in restricted annexes and the correct member boundary.

## Release and lifecycle gates

Before issue, verify that all three component reports are approved at the same level and link their immutable exact versions. Reconcile ratings, numbers, chronology, contradictions and action states; obtain analyst and authorized human review. Check legal/compliance qualifications, Protected Member isolation, PII minimization, evidence restrictions, operational authority and price/contract boundaries. Perform and retain the SAL-D043 report-specific matrix; render and inspect every DOCX/PDF/print page, tables, links, confidentiality treatment, blank second page, pagination, fonts and accessibility. Register the issued artifact and release decision, preserve prior versions and rollback, update handoff and impact-check active or issued reports under SAL-D027.

The approved method and structure are reusable; the local `SalCAPA Integrated Report Template DRAFT.docx` and `Tierra Linda Ranch - SalCAPA Level 1.docx` remain working design references. They are not issued reports. The Ranch source SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™ and SalOPS™ reports remain working; their findings require separate qualification and approval before any final SalCAPA™ issue.

## SAL-D054 integrated assurance

The integrated report reconciles applicable expanded-lens findings and unresolved unknowns from the same-level SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™ and SalOPS™ records. For each decision-changing dependency or second-order effect, identify source confidence, affected people/functions, alternative, decision owner, funding/authority, proof and recheck trigger. Preserve a visible distinction between verified performance, planned capability, unconfirmed partner capacity and a proposed service. Identify which issue needs a Level 2 or Level 3 package and why; do not promote a chat lead or Member-only case detail into an enterprise claim. Apply the controlled report impact and release checks before incorporating this section into any Word template or issued report.

## SAL-D055 integrated decision narrative and master checklist

For every material decision, SalCAPA™ reconciles the three same-level reports into one concise narrative of evidence, working assumption, alternative, consequence if true/false, recommended option, expected result and residual risk. Place a matching master action checklist immediately with it, using the same recommendation and source IDs. Each line states the exact decision or task, accountable Member/Salus role, authorization state, sequence/window, resource and competing dependency, safety/stop condition, evidence of completion and recheck trigger. Do not duplicate or silently alter component-report recommendations; identify the difference, reason, reviewer and approved revision when source reports conflict. Level 1 remains decision-useful despite uncertainty; Levels 2 and 3 show what was validated, revised or tested. Protection Advisor signals remain preliminary and cannot replace a qualified assumption. Apply `SALUS_ANALYTICAL_ASSUMPTIONS_TO_ACTION_STANDARD.md`, Member isolation, human approval and SAL-D027/SAL-D043 release controls to the existing report identity.

Reconcile geospatial findings across the three exact same-level reports. State material location/dependency, map date and confidence, affected function/capability, decision, owner and proof without disclosing exploitable overlays. ## SAL-D057 essential-output and supply decision summary

For each material supply-chain decision, reconcile the three same-level reports into one plain-language view of essential output, safety/welfare threshold, limiting input, current evidence and uncertainty, interruption/endurance and restoration assumption, SalTHIRA™ capability consequence, feasible SalOPS™ options, Member choice, accountable owner, resource/funding requirement, proof and review trigger. Show normal-day and stress benefits, supplier/route common-mode risk and any Level 2/3 data that could change the decision. Keep restricted contracts, prices, customer lists and security-sensitive logistics out of ordinary summaries. Apply `SALUS_SUPPLY_CHAIN_RESILIENCE_ASSESSMENT_STANDARD.md`.

## SAL-D060 research-to-decision trace

The integrated report identifies only decision-relevant, verified external concepts that affected the approved same-level SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™ and SalOPS™ products. Reconcile those concepts with Member facts, alternatives, reviewer decisions and any source conflict; cite appropriately without copying nonpublic third-party or Salus implementation. Record which candidate research was adopted, rejected, deferred or requires next-level proof in the internal decision ledger. A fresh research run improves the decision but does not by itself validate a Member condition or change an issued report.

## SAL-D061 integrated performance and learning check

In the same-level decision summary, connect each material role, communication, minimum-function or system-control issue to qualified SalINTEL™ evidence; the SalTHIRA™ scenario, target, capability gap and priority; the SalOPS™ option, authority and resource; and the observable result or open retest. Show both favorable and contrary evidence, the consequence of a failed assumption, and whether service has returned at the required level rather than merely whether an asset was repaired. Preserve an unverified improvement as an open action, not an accepted capability. An incident/near-miss count requires context about exposure and reporting. Route tested lessons through SAL-D025 and report impacts through SAL-D027; maintain Member isolation.

## SAL-D062 one Member decision view

For each material decision ID, reconcile the exact same-level component report versions into one concise line of reasoning: objective and affected people/function; source status and contrary evidence; working assumption and falsifier; target/current capability; no-action and recommended option; owner, authority, resources and competing demand; implementation/verification state; residual risk; and next review or level trigger. Teach the causal reason for the choice in plain language and show a matching actionable checklist. When the component reports differ, identify the conflict, owner and approved correction rather than averaging ratings or silently choosing one. Keep detailed methods and restricted personnel/security facts in controlled annexes. An approved generalized lesson returns to enterprise use only under SAL-D025. `SALUS_ENTERPRISE_DECISION_TO_PROOF_OPERATING_STANDARD.md` is the shared gate, not a substitute for report-specific release proof.

## SAL-D063 integrated clarity and acceptance

Before a SalCAPA™ decision summary is issued, check that each material SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™ and SalOPS™ input has the same Member, level and decision ID, an exact report version, and an accepted/returned/qualified/excluded disposition. Resolve or display contradictions and preserve proof limits; never silently average conflicting rankings. Present the Member with a short answer to what is known, why it matters, which choice is open, who owns it and how success will be checked. Test a nonexpert readback and the favorable, adverse, unknown and conflicting paths. Record the standing portfolio audit, per-report conformance and active/issued impact under `SALUS_PORTFOLIO_COHERENCE_AND_HANDOFF_STANDARD.md`.
