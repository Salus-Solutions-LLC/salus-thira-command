# SalCAPA™ Integrated Report Standard

**Status:** Approved enterprise process and report structure, effective 27 September 2026 (SAL-D046). **Owner:** Salus Solutions. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential. Apply with the Program Charter, Decision Register, approved system presentation, three-level framework and same-level product standards. This standard does not issue a Protected Member report, approve its findings, deploy dashboard functionality, set a price or authorize operations.

## Purpose and controlled process

SalCAPA™ is the secure coordination, decision, action and evidence environment. Its integrated report is the decision summary for a qualified service level. After approved same-level SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™ and SalOPS™ reports, it reconciles what is known, what matters, feasible choices, who may decide or act, what proof will close an action and what remains. It links exact source report versions and stable IDs; the detailed product reports remain the evidence and analysis authorities. Do not create a duplicate report or treat a working component as issued.

The integrated workflow is: collect and control sources in the correct Protected Member boundary; qualify SalINTEL™ evidence; assess with SalTHIRA™; develop and, if separately authorized, perform SalOPS™ options; reconcile the three approved products; approve a Member decision; track action state, owner, resource, evidence, outcome and residual risk; return verified results and reviewed lessons to the governed learning loop. Preserve provenance, contradictions, human review, immutable releases, supersession, access controls and auditability.

## Required decisions and traceability

The report shall answer:
1. What was learned, by which source and with what confidence or limit?
2. Which threat, hazard, essential-function, capability, governance and dependency issues deserve priority?
3. Which action or service options are feasible, with tradeoffs, competing resources and decision timing?
4. What must the Protected Member authorize, own, defer, reject or verify now?
5. Which specific uncertainty, implementation need or performance question would a higher level resolve?

Each material decision/action row has stable links to SalINTEL™ evidence and contradiction IDs, SalTHIRA™ scenario/capability/target/gap IDs, SalOPS™ option and state, SalCAPA™ decision, responsible authority, due date, proof, residual exposure and supersession. Reconcile disagreements explicitly. A later source may reflect changed conditions rather than a contradiction; record the dates and decision effect.

## Sequential level depth

| Level | Integrated report outcome | Next-level case |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Initial Pass | Source-bound baseline; ranked strengths, risks, capability gaps, feasible options, immediate decisions, estimates and evidence gaps. Do not imply field validation. | Name interviews, records, site observations, target measures or partner confirmations that would change a particular Level 2 decision. |
| 2 Focused Assessment | Validated operational picture for the selected scope; confirmed targets/performance and partner assumptions; cost/resource comparisons, adopted tasks and remaining exceptions. | Name separately approved exercise, assurance, implementation or longitudinal proof needed for Level 3. |
| 3 Deep Dive | Sustained/tested operating picture, performance evidence, improvement cycle and residual-risk decisions for the approved complex scope. | Offer continuing SalOPS™ work or reassessment only for a named measurable need. |

Every Protected Member begins with Level 1; Level 2 follows a qualified integrated Level 1 package, and Level 3 follows a qualified integrated Level 2 package. A completed level is useful on its own. Higher levels and operational services require distinct scope and authorization. Urgent safety action is not held for commercial advancement. Describe the value of additional work through a named uncertainty, decision, deliverable and expected use; do not manufacture urgency, guarantee outcomes or conceal material risks. Price or contractual commitments appear only when separately approved and sourced.

## Required stand-alone report structure

Name the report `[Protected Member customer name] - SalCAPA Level [1, 2, or 3]`. The title/front matter and executive summary state represented level and requested/authorized/completed status. The Salus Confidential cover identifies approved viewers, permitted use and distribution, storage, restricted annex treatment, version and release authority. **Page 2 is entirely blank**, and the body begins on page 3. Nonblank pages use `page X of Y`. Use Arial for title, headings, running headers and table column headings and Times New Roman for body, table body, notes and footers. Apply brand, accessibility, navigation and rendered-page checks.

The executive Bottom Line Up Front has numbered, descriptive table titles subordinate to the BLUF heading, with a one-sentence purpose and repeating column heads as needed:
- Decision priorities and requested authority.
- Five-mission-area and applicable core/sector capability posture, with evidence strength.
- Immediate, near-term and longer-term action choices, with owner, resources, dependencies and state.

The body covers:
1. How to use this summary and the three source reports.
2. Scope, level, methods, dates, boundaries and confidence.
3. Exact source versions and approval states; reconciliation, material contradictions and unresolved disputes.
4. Integrated findings: external context, hazards, people and leadership, governance and finance, continuity, infrastructure, supply chains, assets, strengths and vulnerabilities.
5. SalTHIRA™ priorities across prevention, protection, response, recovery and mitigation; applicable core and sector capabilities, targets where established, gaps and residual uncertainty.
6. SalOPS™ options with objective, owner, executor, partners, prerequisites, time, resources, cost basis if approved, success measure and authorization state.
7. Sequence, dependencies, competing public/mutual-aid resources and no-action consequences.
8. Member decisions and Salus follow-through: urgent matters, bounded actions, accountable owner, target date and closure proof.
9. Next-level or continuing-service opportunity, each mapped to a specific uncertainty or implementation decision, deliverable, prerequisite and separate scope/authorization.
10. Evidence, limitations, document control and restricted annex index. Point to source reports instead of copying unnecessary sensitive detail.

Each major finding teaches the Protected Member in plain language: pathway to harm or disruption, current evidence, contrary or missing information, practical choices, what a choice requires and what information would change it. Distinguish observed fact, report, inference, estimate, unknown, not assessed and verified outcome. Include material compliance issues only at their qualified status; do not present an unsupplied permit as a violation. Minimize PII and keep person-specific records in restricted annexes and the correct member boundary.

## Release and lifecycle gates

Before issue, verify that all three component reports are approved at the same level and link their immutable exact versions. Reconcile ratings, numbers, chronology, contradictions and action states; obtain analyst and authorized human review. Check legal/compliance qualifications, Protected Member isolation, PII minimization, evidence restrictions, operational authority and price/contract boundaries. Perform and retain the SAL-D043 report-specific matrix; render and inspect every DOCX/PDF/print page, tables, links, confidentiality treatment, blank second page, pagination, fonts and accessibility. Register the issued artifact and release decision, preserve prior versions and rollback, update handoff and impact-check active or issued reports under SAL-D027.

The approved method and structure are reusable; the local `SalCAPA Integrated Report Template DRAFT.docx` and `Tierra Linda Ranch - SalCAPA Level 1.docx` remain working design references. They are not issued reports. The Ranch source SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™ and SalOPS™ reports remain working; their findings require separate qualification and approval before any final SalCAPA™ issue.
