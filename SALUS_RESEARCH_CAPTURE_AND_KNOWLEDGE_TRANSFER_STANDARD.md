# Salus Research Capture and Knowledge Transfer Standard

**Authority:** SAL-D127, Marc's 3 November 2026 direction. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential. **State:** authoritative after approved PR #1156 merged as `e7326687b313487f7bc8ee614700f781bb43f19e` on 3 November 2026.

## Purpose and scope

This standard makes research capture and knowledge transfer mandatory for every Salus document and every material Salus research activity. It prevents useful sources, concepts, methodologies, strategies, lessons and contrary evidence from remaining only in an analyst's notes, a chat, a browser session or an ungoverned file collection.

It applies to Salus Insights white papers, Protection Advisor work, SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™ and SalCAPA™ outputs, assessments, reports, proposals, agreements, methods, strategies, plans, presentations, training, marketing, internal decision records and every other new or materially revised Salus document. When a document has no research dependency and produces no reusable knowledge, its record must state `Not applicable` and give the reason; silence is not a disposition.

## Required research-capture record

For each document or research activity, create or update a source-linked research-capture record that identifies:

- the work identifier, title, owner, reviewer, purpose, Protected Member or member-neutral boundary, dates, version and current state;
- the questions, explicit topics and reasonably inferred member-neutral topics searched;
- every concept, methodology, strategy, model, framework, technique, objective, tactic, scenario, hypothesis, lesson, control, test, counterexample and material contradiction used, developed, rejected or left pending;
- every link, web page, dataset, API, feed, file, document, image, map, presentation, recording and other source referred to, cited, collected or materially considered;
- the exact address or governed source pointer, publisher or custodian, title, jurisdiction, retrieval and publication dates, version and hash when available, collection method, search query or route, rights and restrictions, transformations, currency, applicability, confidence, contradictions and access limitations;
- the disposition of each item as applicable, not applicable with reason, duplicate, superseded, unavailable, excluded with reason, or pending with an owner and next action;
- the Salus document sections, findings, claims, methods, controls, recommendations or decisions in which the item is used, challenged or intentionally not used; and
- every reusable knowledge output, its source lineage, proposed destination, qualification state, reviewer, approval state, where-used links and supersession path.

The record must preserve original-source lineage and distinguish source discovery, capture, consideration, human qualification, authoritative adoption, consequential use, external citation and release. One state never proves another.

## Routing rules

1. **Public and third-party material.** Add every materially useful new Internet address and its material linked artifacts to the governed OSINT Library or endpoint register as `Unvetted Candidate`. Preserve exact provenance, redirects, duplicates, changed or unavailable status, rights, restrictions and the originating work and topic labels. Public availability does not establish truth, permission to reproduce or authority to disclose.
2. **Reusable Salus knowledge.** Route qualified member-neutral concepts, methods, strategies, lessons, counterexamples and derivatives to the Salus Knowledge Library through governed intake, review, versioning and approval. Keep candidate doctrine visibly distinct from approved doctrine.
3. **Protected Member material.** Keep originals, identifiable applications, sensitive facts and security-relevant derivatives inside the authorized engagement boundary. Enterprise knowledge may contain only an approved non-disclosing pointer or a qualified member-neutral lesson that cannot identify or expose the Protected Member.
4. **Restricted or unavailable material.** Preserve the source pointer, metadata, restriction, failure or hold state and next action. Never claim unread, inaccessible, unextracted or unreviewed content was understood.
5. **Duplicates and supersession.** Do not create an uncontrolled duplicate. Link to the governed record, retain replacement and supersession lineage and update the existing index or record when appropriate.

## Research-completion and document-release gate

A Salus document may not be represented as research-complete or released as a final customer-facing product until its research-capture record:

- reconciles the applicable Salus Knowledge Library and OSINT Library boundaries required by the Charter;
- captures the sources and knowledge outputs actually used or discovered;
- records contrary evidence, exclusions, inaccessible material and unresolved gaps;
- identifies the human qualification and rights decisions required for every material reliance or external citation;
- updates the applicable source indexes, candidate queues, knowledge records and where-used links; and
- is reviewed for Protected Member segregation, confidentiality, intellectual-property rights, privacy, security, provenance and release authority.

Urgent work may proceed when delay would create greater harm, but the document must disclose the bounded limitation and create a durable pending-capture queue with an accountable owner and due date. Urgency does not convert an unvetted candidate into qualified evidence or authorize disclosure.

## Minimum closeout statement

Every completed Salus document record must state:

- Knowledge Library boundary considered and receipt or limitation;
- OSINT families and topic-expansion boundary considered;
- sources added, updated, duplicated, superseded, unavailable and pending review;
- concepts, methodologies, strategies and lessons proposed, qualified, rejected or pending;
- Protected Member segregation and rights review status;
- unresolved gaps, owner and next action; and
- reviewer and release decision.

Capture and indexing are not proof of substantive review, truth, qualification, approval, model training, deployment, a Protected Member finding, consequential authority or customer release. Those gates remain governed separately.
