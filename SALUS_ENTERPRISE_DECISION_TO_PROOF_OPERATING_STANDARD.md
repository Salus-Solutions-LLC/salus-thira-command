# Salus enterprise decision-to-proof operating standard

**Authority:** SAL-D062, 28 September 2026. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential. **Scope:** Salus strategy, research, proposals, pricing, Protection Advisor, Level 1–3 SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™ and SalCAPA™, recurring services, reporting, knowledge management and product design. Apply with the Program Charter, Decision Register and existing product standards. This standard organizes approved methods; it does not alter the six SalTHIRA™ steps, authorize a customer operation, set a price, approve a finding, release a report or deploy software.

## The operating promise and decision unit

Salus helps each Protected Member **see, decide, coordinate, act and prove** improvements to safety, security and wellbeing. Every material work item must name the decision it will improve and the people, essential outputs and functions affected. The minimum internal decision unit is one traceable chain:

`decision question → evidence and source limits → competing explanations → qualified finding/working assumption → scenario and capability effect → no-action and feasible options → recommended decision and paired checklist → authority and resources → observed result → residual risk and next review`.

Use one stable decision/recommendation ID across same-level products. A raw source, questionnaire answer, thought-leader idea, map layer, model result or absence of a document is a lead until tested for its actual decision use. Classify an item as fact, report, inference, assumption, unknown, not assessed or bounded no-evidence finding. Record supporting and contrary evidence, source date/version, scope, uncertainty and who qualified it. A finding about a person, legal duty, engineering condition, insurance coverage, price or protective performance needs the relevant lawful authority and specialist review. Do not promote a hypothesis by repeating it in later reports.

## How Salus works: six controlled moves

| Move | Personnel action | Required output or stop gate |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Frame and scope** | Identify the Member objective, affected people/function, jurisdiction, requested level, decision owner, time horizon, contract/consent, source boundary and consequence if wrong. Include a low-burden Protection Advisor signal only where relevant. | Written decision question, permitted collection, level/prerequisite and scope; stop or narrow collection when authority or rights are absent. |
| **2. Collect and challenge** | Search authorized Member, external, local and global sources; do a fresh proportionate primary-source and opposing-view review under SAL-D060; preserve provenance, contradictions, access limits and source rights. Ask what evidence would change the decision. | SalINTEL™ evidence/claim ledger, strengths, unknowns and targeted information request; do not claim complete internet coverage or copy another Member's facts. |
| **3. Analyze the system** | Map real and formal roles, decision rights, personnel/contractor access, warning comprehension, essential outputs, interconnected lifelines, supply chains, finance, public resources and recovery to usable function. Test normal and stressed operations. Use the approved six SalTHIRA™ steps and applicable five mission areas/32 core capabilities; compare no action and alternatives. | Explicit working assumption, falsifier, scenario/target/capacity/gap, ranked priority and sensitivity; distinguish plan, inventory and exercise/incident performance. |
| **4. Recommend and authorize** | Explain the causal mechanism, ordinary/stressed benefit, burden, competing resources, unintended effects and residual risk. Pair plain-language narrative with a specific checklist. Show a relevant Salus service only when evidence-linked and separately scoped. | Member decision, accountable role, resource/cost basis, authority state, prerequisite, stop condition and acceptance measure. A recommendation, referral or quote is not an operational order. |
| **5. Deliver and verify** | For separately authorized SalOPS™ work, perform within scope; log deviations, incidents, cost and partner capacity; compare observed results with the target. Exercise messages, roles, backup channels, minimum function and corrective actions where material. | Implemented evidence, reviewer, retest result, unresolved gap and residual risk. Purchased equipment, a sent message, training attendance or a closed ticket alone is not proof of capability. |
| **6. Report and learn** | Reconcile the same-level SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™ and SalOPS™ records in SalCAPA™; explain what was learned, decided, done and proven; qualify reusable member-neutral lessons and reassess active/issued report impact. | One level-labeled decision summary and checklist, exact source versions, open actions, next-level trigger, report release record and governed learning disposition. Keep Member evidence isolated. |

## How Salus thinks and analyzes

Before a material conclusion, the analyst asks: What is the actual objective? What could be true instead? What would falsify the working assumption? Which interacting rules, feedback, delays, roles or dependencies could defeat a component that appears adequate? Does a change improve the Member's ordinary operation and stressed performance, and who might bear an unintended cost? What is the minimum safe service and the time to restore it? Which resource is shared with neighboring demand? Would additional research, a specialist or Level 2/3 validation change the decision enough to justify its cost? Which early signal, exercise or observed outcome would tell us we are wrong?

Use each relevant discipline as a **lens with a stated transfer limit**. The dated thought-leader reconciliation is a source lead; verify the original work and current applicability for the decision. Give credible dissent and direct contrary evidence a place in the record. Do not infer threat from identity, lawful affiliation or a profile; use behavior and context only within lawful, proportionate, human-reviewed processes. Do not convert nonbinding literature or federal guidance into a Member legal requirement. Protect safety-critical details, privacy, IP and source restrictions while providing the Member enough explanation to make a reasoned choice.

## How Salus provides services at each level

| Package | Decision and service outcome | Evidence and performance gate |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 1 — Initial Pass** | Educate, screen all applicable domains, set qualified priorities and same-level SalOPS™ options; make a specific Member decision possible now. Identify decision-changing proof for Level 2. | Source-bound findings and working assumptions, alternatives, level-labeled component reports and integrated SalCAPA™ summary. No claim of field-validated capability. |
| **Level 2 — Focused Assessment** | Validate selected Level 1 questions with authorized records, interviews, observation, partner confirmation and specialists; update targets/options and scope selected services. | Trace what was confirmed, rejected or revised; test a selected intervention and show measured or observed result where contracted. Requires qualified Level 1 package. |
| **Level 3 — Deep Dive** | Test scoped compound scenarios, system interactions, sustained performance, resource competition and sensitivity; design/verify complex programs where authorized. | Comparative evidence, scenario performance, residual uncertainty, monitoring and retest plan. Requires qualified Level 2 package. |

Assessments and additional Salus services are distinct purchases and authorities. A life-safety action under an existing lawful authority is not delayed for a package upgrade. Scope and price the actual evidence effort, personnel, specialists, partner capacity, tools, validation, reporting and uncertainty under the approved rate/estimate process. Never promise an exhaustive review, guaranteed response, capability improvement or recurring monitoring without funded capacity and approval.

## How Salus reports

Every work product and stand-alone report states the represented Level 1, 2 or 3 in the title/front matter and executive summary; status of requested, authorized and completed work; exact time/source boundary; material strengths, problems, contradictions and unknowns; analyst judgment with alternative; ranked mission-area and capability implications where applicable; the specific decision and paired narrative/checklist; owner, authorization, resource and proof; and what a next level would resolve. Explain each material area in language a Protected Member can use. Do not disguise missing evidence as no risk, a repair as restored service, or a proposed SalOPS™ option as executed work.

The four reports have distinct jobs: SalINTEL™ explains and qualifies the picture and collection needs; SalTHIRA™ applies the six-step scenario/capability judgment and ranks the choice; SalOPS™ makes feasible options and records separately authorized delivery; SalCAPA™ gives one reconciled decision view. Use exact same-level source report IDs and versions. Apply the controlled Salus cover, blank second page, body beginning on page three, `page X of Y`, Arial headings and Times New Roman body, confidentiality, accessibility and every-page rendered parity gates. Minimize PII and restrict sensitive annexes. A source or method change triggers SAL-D027 review of active and issued reports; it does not silently replace a released report.

## Internal record and quality gate

For each material item retain: `member/engagement boundary; decision ID; product/level; objective and affected function; collection authority; source IDs/versions/dates/rights; research search boundary; supporting/contrary claims; fact/inference/assumption status; confidence and falsifier; scenario/capability link; no-action/alternatives; recommendation narrative/checklist ID; cost/resource/competing demand; reviewer and specialist gate; Member decision and authorization; implementation evidence; acceptance/retest; residual risk; report/version/recipient; lesson disposition; refresh trigger`. Keep restricted details in the correct Member record; the enterprise index uses only a non-disclosing pointer and approved generalized lesson.

Use the controlled member-neutral `SALUS_DECISION_RECORD_WORKSHEET.md` as a practical form for this chain. A completed copy belongs only in the authorized Member or internal decision record; do not place identifiable, person-level, site-security or commercial facts in the enterprise source repository. The worksheet supports, but does not replace, qualified analyst review, the approved product templates or final Member reports.

At a decision or report gate, a human reviewer checks causal logic, source status, applicability, privacy, lawful authority, proportionality, opposing evidence, financial/resource feasibility, specialist need, checklist/narrative parity, level/scope truth, document style and exact-version release proof. Return weak items for collection or state the limit plainly; never fabricate precision to fill a field. SalCAPA™ may display states and prompt owners, but does not independently qualify facts, authorize action, accept risk, publish a report or approve a price. Retest an adopted change against outcomes and reopen it when performance contradicts the assumption.
