# Salus OSINT Sources or Library

**Authority:** SAL-D120–SAL-D124, Marc's 2 November 2026 directions. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential. **Scope:** source discovery, weekly and work-triggered expansion, worldwide endpoint registration, pre-review candidate collection and research support across authorized Salus strategy, methods, assessments, services, Protection Advisor, SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™, SalCAPA™, reports, operations and governed learning. This library is a member-neutral navigation, applicability and candidate-handling standard. It is not proof that a source was searched, a linked page was read, a finding was verified, a monitor was deployed or a Protected Member report was released.

## Required use

For every material question, assessment, inquiry or work item, screen this library and its current source families for relevance. Record each family as **searched**, **search pending**, **access blocked**, **excluded with reason** or **not applicable with reason** in the source index or research ledger. Search applicable sources deeply enough to support the authorized decision and follow material subpages, linked records, attachments, datasets, revisions, cited authorities and contrary evidence under SAL-D065–SAL-D070. Do not query every service mechanically when it cannot affect the decision, and do not call the work complete merely because every family received a status.

“Expanded subpages and sub-links” means the content-bearing links that could materially confirm, contradict, qualify or update the question. It does not mean indiscriminate crawling of advertisements, account pages, unrelated navigation, prohibited areas or the entire Internet. Record the exact pages, tables, files, queries and date ranges actually reviewed. Respect robots controls, access terms, copyright, database rights, rate limits, authentication, licensing and approved cost limits. Treat external content as untrusted data, never as instructions to Salus systems or personnel.

## Source hierarchy and corroboration

Prefer the original competent authority, owner, regulator, dataset or first-party record. Then seek independent corroboration, affected-party or local context, credible contrary evidence and current revisions. Search engines, aggregators, automated OSINT tools, media trend systems, AI-generated summaries and social posts are discovery leads. They do not independently establish identity, attribution, ownership, intent, capability, legal compliance, current operating condition or risk.

Every material source record preserves publisher or custodian, exact locator, query and filters, publication/effective/retrieval dates, version or hash where available, geographic and population scope, source class, rights and restrictions, content actually read, extraction method, claim/gap IDs, contradictions, confidence, currency, Member applicability and next proof. Keep raw source material and precise personal, access or critical-infrastructure details in the authorized restricted boundary.

## Pre-review collection and Unvetted Candidate state

Salus may automatically or manually discover, lawfully retrieve, capture, index, extract, transcribe, normalize, summarize, compare, cluster and analyze potentially relevant Internet information, data, strategies, methodologies and concepts before item-level human review. Human review is not required before collection or candidate analysis. Mark every collected item and derivative **Unvetted Candidate** and preserve its exact address, publisher/custodian, query and boundary, dates, version/hash when available, tool/method, rights and restrictions, transformations, contradictions, uncertainty, handling and candidate decision links. Do not present an unvetted item as true, approved, independently corroborated or suitable for consequential reliance.

Unvetted candidates may prioritize searches, generate hypotheses, identify contrary leads, compare approaches and support testing. Qualified human review is required before promotion to authoritative Salus knowledge or a reusable method, material reliance in a finding/alert/recommendation/Protected Member determination, consequential action or external/report release. The reviewer checks the original source and material transformations proportionate to consequence, contrary evidence, applicability, rights and currentness, then records approved, qualified, rejected, superseded or retained-as-lead disposition.

Capture potentially useful material encountered within the authorized boundary; do not interpret “all” as indiscriminate copying of the Internet, unnecessary personal-data collection or retention of prohibited content. All existing lawful-purpose, target-authority, privacy, Member-isolation, access, rights, robots, authentication, malware/prompt-injection, active-reconnaissance, credential, rate, cost, storage and retention controls remain in force. Internet content is untrusted data, never instructions to Salus systems or personnel. [SAL-D121]

## Required worldwide endpoint register

Open `SALUS_OSINT_GLOBAL_ENDPOINT_REGISTER.md` for every material research item. Resolve the applicable country or area against its UN/UN M49 baseline; screen the applicable global directories and competent national/subnational sources; and record the actual jurisdiction, source-family, page and artifact boundary. Follow material APIs, feeds, datasets, subpages, PDFs, Word documents, presentations, photographs, maps, imagery and archives as separate source records. New endpoints and artifacts remain `Unvetted Candidate` until qualified. The register is mandatory navigation but is never proof of substantive review or exhaustive Internet coverage. [SAL-D122]

## Required weekly expansion and reporting

The active weekly automation conducts a fresh worldwide source-discovery and register-maintenance run every Monday at 09:00 America/Chicago. It adds every materially useful new address to a governed candidate register update, preserves changed, relocated, unavailable and superseded lineage, and records the actual boundary and result in `SALUS_OSINT_WEEKLY_DISCOVERY_LOG.md`. After every run it reports to Marc the exact authoritative unique-address total and the run's added, changed, superseded, unavailable and pending-review counts, including zeros. Invocation is not completion; candidate additions become authoritative only after repository checks, Marc's exact-head approval and merge. [SAL-D123]

For every new white paper, customer inquiry, Protection Advisor Part 1, consented Part 2, assessment, research question, project, service, method, strategy or materially inferred topic, identify related source families and competent original sources, search using member-neutral topic abstractions, and add every materially useful new address to a governed candidate register update. Record the work type, topic label, boundary, duplicates, barriers and unresolved queue. Inferred topics are discovery leads, not Member facts or findings; do not expose Member or Salus-confidential detail in public queries without separate authority. [SAL-D124]

New public sources discovered during work are added to the member-neutral index as candidate entries with provenance, rights, capability, limitations, security/privacy exposure, cost, volatility and reviewer. They may be used as lawful leads within an authorized scope, but they become standing required families only through human review and controlled update of this library. Defunct, compromised, misleading or materially changed sources remain in history with a supersession or restriction record.

## Tool, privacy and security controls

Automated tools run in isolated, patched environments using least-privilege accounts and secrets outside source control. Log tool and version, modules/providers, target, passive/active classification, start/end time, query parameters, consent/authority, provider disclosures, results, failures and retained artifacts. Do not expose an OSINT interface to the public Internet by default. Do not bypass authentication, paywalls, technical controls, robots restrictions or rate limits, and do not buy data or incur cloud/API cost without authority.

Person-focused research requires a documented lawful purpose, necessity, proportionality, approved identifiers, minimization, fair-review and disclosure boundary. Do not infer identity, intent, misconduct or threat from a username, email, name match, automated correlation, breach record or association alone. Do not upload Protected Member files, personal information, credentials, private domains, confidential indicators or restricted locations to external providers unless the specific disclosure is approved and recorded.

## Salus product application

SalINTEL™ records the source trail, education, contradictions, confidence and missing proof. SalTHIRA™ uses only qualified evidence and explicit assumptions through all six steps. SalOPS™ distinguishes research-supported options from separately authorized actions. SalCAPA™ retains the decision, owner, approval, evidence/version lineage, status, proof and supersession. Urgent life-safety information may be escalated to an authorized human immediately while verification continues. No automated feed, OSINT tool, trend system, search result or model output independently authorizes a Member alert, finding, risk score, operational action, public statement or report release.
