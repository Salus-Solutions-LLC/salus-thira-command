## Cross-product application and decision rule

For every selected idea, SalINTEL™ states the concern in plain language, records source, current Member evidence, contrary facts, unknowns and the specific Level 2 proof request. SalTHIRA™ tests its effect through the approved six steps, five mission areas and applicable 32 capabilities, with targets, demonstrated capacity, uncertainty and treatment sensitivity. SalOPS™ gives a named, feasible option, owner, authority, resource constraint, check and retest. SalCAPA™ summarizes the same-level decision, priority, owner, funding/resource choice, proof and residual risk. Level 1 may make qualified recommendations; Levels 2 and 3 validate and stress selected decisions. An enterprise method does not change an issued Member report automatically; apply SAL-D027 impact review and the exact release gates.
