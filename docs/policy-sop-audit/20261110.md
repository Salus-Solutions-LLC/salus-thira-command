## Material controlled changes

Added the approved organization-wide Red-Team review standard. Every Salus report, analysis, assessment, and improvement plan now requires a documented Red-Team perspective twice: first on evidence, assumptions, findings, uncertainty, and likely failure modes; and again on material recommendations or improvement actions. The control requires labeled report discussions, accountable disposition, evidence retention, and the approved one-reviewer rule with the designated reviewer controlled within the authoritative document. FRM-09-10 now captures the related review evidence.

Added a proportionate post-quantum cryptography transition control for material systems using public-key cryptography. It requires a cryptographic inventory, dependency and confidentiality-life analysis, prioritized transition planning, vendor roadmaps, crypto agility, testing, rollback/recovery, controlled deployment, milestones, and accountable residual-risk approval. It is explicitly a Salus management control informed by final NIST IR 8547, not an automatically binding federal duty.

Added a proportionate AI/model-enabled incident-management control addressing cyberattacks, misuse, anomalous behavior, severe malfunction, unsafe integration, and unauthorized action while preserving human incident authority, provenance, non-human identity governance, scoped containment, evidence, reporting triggers, continuity, fallback, and recovery. It is explicitly optional good practice adopted as a Salus control and identifies NIST IR 8621 as a workshop report/foundation rather than a binding standard.

The source register was updated with descriptive links for NIST IR 8547 and NIST IR 8621. A missing page-8 body navigation anchor was stabilized with unique drawing and paragraph identifiers. No pre-existing substantive requirement was removed.
