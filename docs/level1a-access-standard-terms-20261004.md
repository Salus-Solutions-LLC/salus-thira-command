# Level 1A Access Standard Terms

**Approved by Marc:** 4 October 2026. **Source Word SHA-256:** `5506C3D2E6994D9E4850AA532392BD09B546939F22CF1751A2ADDA44A9818626`. This is the owner-approved terms text for incorporation into the controlled Level 1A agreement. It does not authorize opening checkout or file intake before governed acceptance.

For Salus owner review. These terms are proposed language for the one unit, $1,500 online agreement. They are not yet approved for Member issue or payment collection. The approved scope, four reports, prepayment rule, automatic purchase decision and protected file controls remain as recorded in the Level 1A agreement candidate.

## 1  Order and electronic acceptance

A buyer may order one Level 1A Access assessment for $1,500 without a Salus review of the buyer, organization, selected unit, price category or signer. The buyer names one site or one defined function, its activity and boundary; identifies any excluded areas; and confirms authority to bind the organization. Before payment, the buyer can read, download and save the complete agreement, the exact scope and an itemized total. The buyer affirmatively selects �I agree and authorize this purchase.� Salus records the agreement version, scope, total, signer identity, acceptance time and payment reference. A changed scope, total or agreement version requires a new acceptance. No prechecked consent box or general payment link substitutes for this step.

Authority to buy does not by itself authorize disclosure of Protected Member records. A separate records coordinator must confirm that the organization may provide each class of records before the secure upload route opens.

## 2  Price and tax

The fixed package fee is $1,500 USD for the one identified unit. The standard Texas Level 1 consulting scope uses the approved zero Texas sales tax treatment only while the order matches that classification. A separate investigation, security service, expanded unit or other taxable scope requires its own agreement, classification and price. For an order outside the approved Texas rule, the system must apply a reviewed jurisdiction and sourcing rule, show each applicable tax and the total before acceptance, and retain the calculation and rule version. Missing or conflicting tax information is not assumed to mean zero tax. If the calculation is temporarily unavailable, the order request remains saved and the calculation retries; payment is not taken until the correct total can be shown and accepted.

## 3  Start, delivery and Member cooperation

Salus begins substantive assessment work after verified full prepayment and receipt of a usable Level 1 intake, organization chart with roles and authority, and the minimum information needed to identify the selected unit. The order confirmation states any missing items. Salus aims to provide the four qualified reports within 30 business days after those inputs are complete. If a necessary source, clarification or qualified review causes delay, Salus gives the buyer a revised date and reason; the buyer may then cancel under Section 4. The timeline is a proposed service target, not a promise of a safety result or a report issued without qualified review.

One remote clarification is included. The buyer may send corrections to factual information. Missing records are identified as missing; they do not automatically require an upgrade. Salus may recommend separately priced work, but the buyer need not buy it to receive the included Level 1A reports.

## 4  Cancellation and refunds

The buyer may cancel through the order page or by written notice using the contact shown on the receipt. If Salus has not started substantive assessment work, Salus refunds the package fee and any related tax paid. After work starts, Salus refunds the unearned portion: the $1,500 fee minus the value of work actually completed and documented for that order, never more than $1,500 in retained fees. The buyer receives a plain language work and refund statement. A delivered report counts as completed work; an unfinished internal draft alone does not count as a delivered report. Salus does not charge a separate cancellation penalty. Any tax refund follows the tax adjustment on the same transaction. Salus targets initiating an approved refund within five business days; card settlement timing depends on Stripe and the buyer's issuer. A billing error or duplicate charge is corrected in full.

## 5  Report review and correction

Salus issues the four reports only after qualified review. Within ten business days after delivery, the Member may identify a specific factual error or omitted record that was within the accepted scope and available to Salus. Salus reviews that item and either corrects the affected governed report version or explains why the evidence does not support the change. A disputed judgment is recorded with its source and uncertainty. Silence does not waive a right to report a later discovered factual error, but the included review window does not add new sites, scenarios or services without a separate scope. Salus does not promise a regulator, insurer or other third party will accept the report.

## 6  Confidentiality, information and records

The Member keeps ownership of its source records and client-specific information. Salus may use them only to perform and document the authorized service, protect the parties, and meet legal or record obligations. Access is restricted by Member and engagement; files remain held until their exact version clears malware scanning. Salus does not sell Member-specific findings or personal information to another customer. A report may be shared outside the Member only on authorized instruction or as required by law. Salus retains the governed agreement, source and decision records under its approved retention policy, including the standing Protection Advisor retention rule where applicable; this proposal does not create a new deletion period. An operative privacy notice, retention schedule, incident-notice route and subprocessors list must be linked by exact version before automatic issue.

The buyer must avoid uploading unnecessary personal directories or person-level case files. If Salus learns of unauthorized access to Member information, Salus will contain and investigate it, preserve evidence, and give legally required notices without an unreasonable delay. The final notice clause must identify a monitored contact and any stricter jurisdictional deadline.

## 7  Responsibility and disputes

Salus provides a professional assessment and decision support, not a guarantee of prevention, compliance, insurance savings or a particular outcome. The Member makes and owns its operational decisions. Neither party excludes liability that law does not permit it to exclude. Proposed allocation for review: for ordinary contract claims, each party's aggregate direct-damages liability is capped at the amount paid for this order; the cap does not apply to fraud, willful misconduct, gross negligence, unpaid fees, or a party's misuse or unauthorized disclosure of the other's confidential information. Neither party is liable for remote or speculative lost profits on an ordinary contract claim. This allocation needs Salus legal and insurance review before use.

The parties first give written notice describing a dispute and allow 30 days for good faith resolution. A buyer may still pursue any nonwaivable right or card dispute. Texas law and courts in the county of Salus's principal Texas office are proposed for contract disputes, subject to mandatory consumer and local law. The exact county and notice address must be inserted before issue; this proposal does not waive statutory remedies.
