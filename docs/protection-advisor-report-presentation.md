# Protection Advisor report presentation contract

The approved `Protection Advisor Report Template.docx` controls Salus identity, cover design, watermark treatment, letter-size page, report reference, and footer. The PDF report should follow those cues while preserving its independent, governed download route.

## Editorial and print checks

- Lead with the preliminary conclusion, the reason for review, and the next action.
- Distinguish self-reported conditions, verified evidence, inference, and unanswered questions.
- Use descriptive headings, plain language, repeatable table headers, readable type, and column widths that wrap at word boundaries.
- Keep the report reference, generated date and time, requested handling, preliminary status, and page number visible.
- Render sample Word and PDF outputs and inspect every page at normal reading size before changing the production generator.
- Match the Salus template's brand and visual hierarchy; do not imply that a Salus report was issued by OPM or another federal agency.

## Authority and limits

OPM's public plain-language guidance calls for descriptive headings, short paragraphs, adequate margins and white space, consistently used fonts, tables where they clarify information, dates and page numbers, and line breaks that preserve names and dates. It is editorial guidance, not a universal OPM font, margin, or report-cover specification for private assessments:
https://www.opm.gov/information-management/plain-language/

Federal Section 508 guidance provides tests for accessible Word documents and PDFs:
https://www.section508.gov/create/documents/
https://www.section508.gov/create/pdfs/
Salus adopts these as quality targets. The current ReportLab PDF generator does not produce a tagged structure tree, so a visual review alone cannot establish Section 508 conformance. Do not call the output "Section 508 compliant" until a semantic PDF authoring/remediation and validation gate is in place.

The National Archives' CUI policy applies to federal agencies and applicable contractors under governing authority. A questionnaire handling choice alone is not a verified CUI designation:
https://www.archives.gov/cui/registry/policy-guidance
The generated cover and page markings must state the actual requested handling and retain human review before any formal government marking or release.
