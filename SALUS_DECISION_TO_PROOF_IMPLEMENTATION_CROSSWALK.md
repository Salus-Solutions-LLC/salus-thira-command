### Advisor question clarity candidates

The current anonymous Part 1 uses long compound questions to screen broad subjects. The following are **wording candidates for usability testing**, not an approved key, scoring, question-count or live-form change. Keep the current answer key and qualified evidence meaning if later adopted; put definitions in optional help text rather than another mandatory question.

| Current key | Member-first candidate question | Optional explanation to preserve the technical meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `capability_targets` | “For your most serious emergencies, have you written down what your team must be able to do and how quickly?” | Ask the Level 1 analyst to identify the responsible role, conditions, measurable result and acceptable time across applicable mission areas. |
| `dependency_map` | “Do you know which people, services and supplies your essential work depends on?” | In Level 1, trace facilities, utilities, technology, suppliers, transport and shared failure points; do not ask for sensitive diagrams in anonymous intake. |
| `resource_competition` | “Could you still get the help and supplies you need if nearby organizations need them too?” | Level 1 tests shared responders, fuel, medical support, vendors, equipment, communications and alternate sites under simultaneous demand. |
| `validation_evidence` | “Has your team recently shown that its emergency arrangements work when normal help or systems are unavailable?” | A written plan or purchase is not performance proof. The Level 1 reviewer asks for a relevant exercise, incident, demonstration or other current evidence. |
| `recovery_targets` | “Have you decided how soon each essential service must be working again, even at a reduced level?” | Level 1 checks minimum usable service, alternate resources, owner and restoration order; a repair date alone is not a recovery target. |
| `investment_tradeoffs` | “When choosing safety improvements, do leaders compare benefit, cost and how soon each option can help?” | Level 1 also checks implementation dependencies, competing resources, adverse effects and consequence reduction. |

Acceptance requires a timed comprehension test with nonexpert personnel and a sample Protected Member, answer-path regression checks, consent/privacy review and exact PDF/DOCX/print parity. A shorter question that changes the source meaning or misses a safety-critical distinction must be revised before release.
