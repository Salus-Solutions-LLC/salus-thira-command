## Purpose and application

Every Salus assessment report and professional deliverable must make its conclusion, evidence, consequence for people, decision, responsible role and verification understandable to its intended reader. Preserve analytical depth, contrary evidence, limitations, stable identities, approved methods and qualified human authority.

Apply every method below to its relevant subject. Use a proportionate representation within the requested format. A short answer can satisfy a method in a sentence or link; it does not require a new file, graphic or section. For every omitted representation, record the method, source-specific reason, applicable alternative and retained content location. Lack of a meaningful subject requires an honest bounded statement. The assistant owns applicability, corrections and routine assurance without asking the owner to certify ordinary compliance.

All nine foundational methods and all twenty additions below are required where their subject is present. An attractive layout is insufficient evidence of comprehension or operational performance.

## Foundational methods

| ID | Required method | Application and necessary limit |
|---|---|---|
| M01 | Layered reading | Provide a concise decision-oriented entry and a direct path to the full analysis and source detail. Material uncertainty belongs beside the conclusion it affects. |
| M02 | Consistent finding briefs | State the finding, why it matters to people, existing strengths, uncertainty, next decision and evidence of a result. Distinguish unsupported causes from established facts. |
| M03 | Interacting-party handoff | Show the relevant person, internal team and external partner, including the exchange, accepted task, retained duty and follow-up. Identify proposed versus observed relationships. |
| M04 | Recommendation reasoning | Present the recommendation, supporting reasons, assumptions, strongest relevant counterargument, limitations and evidence that would change it. Use no invented probability or financial return. |
| M05 | Status impact and action | Connect the stated status to its effect on people, responsible role, next action and limiting factors. Keep priority, evidence confidence and implementation progress distinguishable. |
| M06 | Safeguards before and after an event | Use an appropriate bowtie or equivalent diagram to connect the unwanted transition, preventive safeguards, consequence-limiting safeguards and possible consequence. Do not imply verified control effectiveness from diagram placement. |
| M07 | Improvement storyboard | Connect current condition, desired outcome, proposed change, accountable role and verification. Preserve actual implementation and acceptance states. |
| M08 | Understandable uncertainty | Explain what an instruction, self-report, observation, conflicting account or unavailable record can establish. Confidence and event likelihood require separate support. |
| M09 | Person or family example | Use a bounded example to explain why the finding matters to its intended recipient. Label hypothetical illustrations and distinguish them from actual evidence. |

## Status colors and accessible graphics

Use red, yellow and green as labeled status cues when the evidence supports that dimension. For evidence status, red means an answer remains open, yellow means partial evidence or confirmation required, and green means the precisely stated fact has been established. A verified written duty does not establish operational performance. Unobserved or unassessed performance must remain explicit and unrated.

If a different dimension is shown, define it next to the indicator and keep it distinct from evidence status. Human approval, source qualification, priority, implementation and observed effectiveness retain separate records. Use words and meaningful labels alongside color. Provide descriptive alternative text, readable type, adequate contrast and an accessible textual account of each material diagram.

Preserve the Charter's federal/OPM-referenced document standards, current Salus template, approved colors/assets, Arial headings and page furniture, 12-point Times New Roman body, absolute font floors, margins, cover clearances, prescribed second page, table and footer baseline, confidentiality and pagination. Call-out boxes are a specifically authorized component within that common system. Change layout or page count rather than shrinking text.

## Editorial integration and acceptance

Integrate the methods into the existing reading structure. Use one representation to satisfy several related methods when clear. Preserve unique substance and avoid expanding a report merely to display another format. Keep the four reports understandable individually and their substantive records mutually consistent.

Before delivery, retain a per-method and per-report applicability record and inspect the exact versions. Verify source/finding/target/option/decision trace, scope, approvals, uncertainty, status meaning, alternative explanations, unintended consequences, authority, privacy, realistic resources and acceptance evidence. Render and inspect all pages, correct layout defects, test bookmarks and links, verify changed and retained content, and retain hashes and review decisions. Apply the existing minimum eight substantive re-passes and a separate terminal editorial pass.

This standard creates no additional recommendation register and adds no new unfamiliar-reader or teach-back testing requirement. Previously authoritative assurance obligations retain their recorded scope and limitations. Unavailable human feedback remains honestly documented. Routine checks are assistant-owned. Risk acceptance, qualified human decisions, exact-revision promotion, external issue and operational permissions remain substantive human authorities.

## Governance and implementation

This is a member-neutral method standard. Keep Protected Member originals, findings, source details, diagrams and review evidence within their authorized private engagement. Public guidance supplies concepts within its scope; it does not establish a local duty or constitute new source qualification.

Research completeness, rights, governed retention and all source-coverage requirements remain in force. A presentation or Charter amendment cannot cure missing assessment evidence, change a paused engagement, enable a reader, deploy a service or authorize external distribution.

Apply prospectively to new and materially revised work. Review existing report and template families through the governed impact process. Record each family's state as specified, candidate revised, rendered/verified, approved or deployed, as applicable. Adoption of this standard does not establish that every existing artifact has been remediated.
