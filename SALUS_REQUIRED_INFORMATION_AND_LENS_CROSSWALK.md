# Salus required-information and lens crosswalk

**Status:** Approved member-neutral collection and analysis control under SAL-D052, 28 September 2026. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential.  
**Use with:** Program Charter, SAL-D050 multidisciplinary assurance, SAL-D051 organizational health, `SALUS_ORGANIZATIONAL_HEALTH_PRODUCT_INTEGRATION.md`, the four product standards and the three-level framework.  
**Boundary:** This is a menu and tracking structure, not a demand for every document from every Protected Member. Member-specific evidence remains in its segregated engagement record. A missing item is unknown until the analyst determines whether it exists and applies.

## 1. Intake rule

Protection Advisor Part 1 remains anonymous and low burden. Part 2 asks only bounded context, not document upload or a comprehensive discipline survey. After the Member authorizes Level 1, Salus issues a tailored **minimum initial request**, explains why each item matters, accepts an existing equivalent, and names a secure handoff route. The analyst performs lawful public and approved proprietary research independently; the Member is not asked to supply public material that Salus can reasonably obtain. Escalate to Level 2 or 3 requests only for a named decision-changing question and authorized scope.

For each requested item record: `request_id, Protected Member/engagement, lens, decision/question served, source owner, relevant entity/location/time, exact version/date, applicability, confidentiality/access, requested/received/not-held/declined/not-applicable status, received location and hash where available, reliability, contradiction, analyst/reviewer, SalTHIRA input ID, next action`. Do not place credentials, detailed protective vulnerabilities, medical records, allegations or privileged legal advice in an ordinary request list.

## 2. Minimum Level 1 Member packet

Ask for existing materials or a short description where a document does not exist. Tailor before sending; do not make all ten mandatory.

| Item | Purpose | Safe initial form |
|---|---|---|
| Governing identity, charter/bylaws or equivalent, organization chart | Verify legal form, governing body, authority and scope | Current adopted document and role chart, with personal contact details minimized |
| Essential functions, locations and populations served | Define what must continue and who could be affected | Function and site list; aggregate population ranges |
| Current risk, emergency, continuity and crisis-communication plans | Compare stated arrangements and version control | Current adopted plans and effective dates; restricted transfer for sensitive annexes |
| Decision, delegation, succession and emergency-spending rules | Identify lawful and practical decision rights | Board resolutions/policies or role-based summary; counsel review if disputed |
| Priority incident, exercise and corrective-action summaries | Compare plans with actual performance | Redacted summaries, dates, outcomes and open actions |
| Key staffing, volunteer, contractor and public-partner roles | Identify capability and single points of failure | Role counts, qualifications and agreement types; no personnel file |
| Critical assets, lifelines, systems and supplier dependencies | Find common-mode failure and interruption pathways | High-level asset/dependency map; sensitive details separately controlled |
| Insurance, financial and capital-planning overview | Test recovery feasibility and authority | Policy schedule, limits summary, reserve/budget range and decision owner; detailed policies if later material |
| Applicable permits, licenses, inspection and compliance register | Identify actual duties and evidence | Existing register or list of competent authorities and current status |
| Known priorities, constraints and desired decisions | Focus analysis on Member value | Short leadership statement, budget/timing envelope and prior actions |

Offer a secure, staged upload and a “not held / not known / prefer to discuss” response. Receiving a file does not validate its currency or implementation. For communities and HOAs, include board/committee rules, election and volunteer authority, common versus private property, mutual-aid relationships, dues/reserves and resident communication where applicable. For animal, aviation, industrial or healthcare operations, add sector-specific items only after applicability screening.

## 4. Organizational-health twelve-domain evidence overlay

Apply SAL-D051 to every applicable lens. The same evidence may support multiple domains; link it once rather than upload duplicate copies. Minimum decision traces: (1) one routine consequential decision, (2) one change in policy or instruction from approval to actual use, and (3) one recent disruption or exercise if available. At Level 1, a short role-based walkthrough or an explicit “untested” status may suffice. For a material gap, Level 2 seeks adopted instruments, exact delegated limits, process owners, timestamps, recipient comprehension and independent counterevidence. Level 3 observes or exercises the approved complex case and measures sustained results.

The crosswalk must preserve **what works**, what fails, where accounts conflict and what remains unknown. It must not manufacture a strength and gap in every domain. Person-level background or insider matters require separate lawful authority, restricted access and human review.

## 5. SalTHIRA-ready data contract

The SalINTEL collector prepares these inputs before any six-step SalTHIRA analysis:
1. **Context and scenario:** protected objective, location/period, hazard or intentional-threat basis, exposed functions/people/assets, source and uncertainty.
2. **Target:** required outcome, time, quality, capacity, accessibility and accountable owner for each applicable mission area/core or sector capability.
3. **Current capacity:** personnel, partners, equipment, authority, training, communications, finance, insurance and demonstrated incident/exercise performance.
4. **Gap and mechanism:** difference between target and demonstrated capacity; leadership/process/behavior/dependency cause hypothesis; correlated and unequal consequences.
5. **Options:** no action, prevention/protection/mitigation/response/recovery treatments; lawful authority, cost/cash timing, implementation lead, public-resource competition, insurance/permit gate and side effects.
6. **Decision and test:** priority, confidence, residual risk, Member decision owner, proposed SalOPS action state, measure, validation/recheck trigger and exact Level 2/3 evidence needed.

Use stable source, contradiction, domain, scenario, capability, option and decision IDs. Unknown inputs remain unknown; do not invent target precision or treat a plan as performance proof.

## 6. Report and collection conformance

- **SalINTEL™:** include an explained organizational-health section, dedicated compliance section, multidisciplinary applicability register, eight evidence dimensions, five mission areas/32-capability applicability screen, qualified strengths/gaps/contradictions and a tailored **Missing information and Member collection requests** table. Each row states why the item matters, which decision it can change, who can provide it, source sensitivity and level/timing.
- **SalTHIRA™:** in the existing six-step report, link each material unknown or validated lens to scenario, target, current capability, gap, treatment and priority; do not omit organizational causes because a physical hazard dominates.
- **SalOPS™:** compare feasible alternatives and separate offered from authorized action. Include competence, legal, insurance, funding, communication, safety/stop and verification gates.
- **SalCAPA™:** reconcile approved same-level reports into one action/assurance register with Member authority, selected/deferred treatment, owner, resource, due date, proof, residual risk and next-level need.
- **Protection Advisor:** show only educational, preliminary signals and a voluntary Level 1 or separately scoped service path; no compulsory upload packet in Part 1 or long Part 2 questionnaire.

Review each existing report/template under SAL-D027 without creating a duplicate. Apply SAL-D043, OPM typography, confidentiality, blank second page, “page X of Y,” accessibility and rendered DOCX/PDF/print parity where applicable. Never copy this generic register into a client report as if it were Member-specific findings.

## 7. Coverage and limits

This register consolidates SAL-D050's eighteen lenses, SAL-D051's twelve domains, SAL-D038's eight evidence dimensions, SAL-D039's six-step handoff, and the established five mission areas and 32 core-capability applicability screen. It is not a claim that every past chat, upload, website, law or Protected Member record has been read or reconciled. An engagement-specific collection ledger must record the actual 64-upload or other source boundary, versions, missing files, contradictory records and unresolved questions. Salus must update the register when a governed new lesson changes a decision, with provenance, applicability, Member isolation and human approval.
