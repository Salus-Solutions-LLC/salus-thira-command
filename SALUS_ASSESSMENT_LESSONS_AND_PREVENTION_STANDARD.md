# Salus Assessment Lessons and Prevention Standard

**Authority:** Marc's direction of 19 November 2026, SAL-D147. The requirement is authorized; this implementation becomes authoritative through governed promotion.

## Purpose and scope

Apply the following lessons to every assessment and related research, source review, report revision, assurance task and release decision. Use applicable controls on other Salus work. The assistant performs ordinary verification and correction; the owner is not asked to certify that the assistant complied.

Maintain a restricted engagement account of observed mistakes, user corrections, improvements, supporting evidence, consequences, corrections and unresolved limits. Separate an observed defect from a concern or an untested improvement. Preserve the original record, exact affected versions and supersession. A generalized lesson is not proof that a private case is complete.

Transfer only member-neutral methods into enterprise requirements. Keep Protected Member identities, documents, source details, diagrams, commercial information, personal data and findings in their authorized engagement. Govern qualification, copyright and permitted reuse separately.

## Learning closure and regression prevention

For each material lesson, identify the failure or improvement, actual evidence, affected rule or artifact, correction, verification, recurrence risk, owner and open consequence. Record a prospective suggestion as untested. Do not turn a proposed control into a claim that it is implemented or effective.

Before a similar task or revision, inspect the relevant lessons and apply their controls to the actual work. Maintain the existing source, analytical, accessibility, eight-pass and editorial records; do not introduce a duplicate recommendation register or an additional unfamiliar-reader test. Existing human assurance requirements retain their own scope.

A newly detected acceptance defect invalidates the affected acceptance claim. Correct the artifact, rerun affected checks, inspect the resulting version and reopen downstream decisions where necessary. Retain the earlier claim and correction in the audit history.

Assess impact on templates and generators as well as populated reports. Record specification, candidate correction, verified artifact, approved adoption and deployed enforcement separately. A requirement is not evidence that every historical file has migrated.

## Authority and release

This standard adds prevention requirements and a governed learning account. It grants no new source access, qualification, Member finding, operational action, deployment, financial commitment, sensitive disclosure or report release. Preserve least privilege and all explicit holds.

Research gaps, absent originals, unavailable independent readers and historical chronology cannot be closed by a checkbox. Continue authorized alternatives, prepare concrete corrective work and report the remaining dependency honestly. The assistant owns routine assurance; substantive human decisions remain with their competent authority.
