# Salus portfolio coherence and handoff standard

**Authority:** SAL-D063, 28 September 2026. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential. **Applies to:** every material new or revised method, questionnaire, proposal, price description, service design, software workflow, template and Protected Member report. Apply with the Program Charter, Decision Register, approved artifacts, SAL-D027 report impact, SAL-D062 decision-to-proof method and release rules. This standard is a repeatable review gate; it does not itself approve a finding, document version, price, operation or deployment.

## The plain-language promise

Every Salus item should help a nonexpert answer five questions: **What is happening? Why does it matter? What do we know and still need to check? What choice is available now? Who will do what, and how will we know it worked?** Explain technical terms once, use the same term for the same concept, and show the Member the practical consequence. Keep sensitive evidence and proprietary working detail in controlled records while providing enough reasoning for an informed decision.

## When the review runs

Run this review at the start and end of material research or design work; before changing a questionnaire, method, service or report template; before each same-level product handoff; before a report is released; after a material incident, contradiction, new source, test result or lesson; and before revising pricing, marketing or a live workflow. A small editorial correction can use a short recorded check. A change that alters meaning, collection, rating, offer, authority, access, Member decision or report lineage requires the full check and applicable human approval. The review is proportional and source-bound: name what was inspected and what remains unreviewed. Never imply that a recurring rule creates autonomous monitoring or an exhaustive internet search.

## One handoff record for one material decision

Use a stable decision ID within one Protected Member and engagement. SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™ and SalCAPA™ each retain their own report identity and level. The minimum internal handoff is:

| Field | Plain question the receiver must be able to answer |
|---|---|
| Identity and authority | Which Member, engagement, decision ID, level, sponsor and authorized scope does this concern? |
| Purpose and affected function | What daily or emergency outcome is at stake, for whom, and by when? |
| Evidence | What source/version/date supports the claim, what contradicts it, what was actually observed and what remains unknown? |
| Working judgment | Is this a verified fact, reported condition, inference or assumption? How confident are we, and what would change our mind? |
| Capability and consequence | Which scenario, mission area, capability, target, current ability and gap does it affect? What does no action mean? |
| Choice | What feasible options, constraints, competing resources, adverse effects, cost basis and next-level proof matter? |
| Authority and action | Who can decide; is the item suggested, selected, authorized, performed or validated; and what is the safe stop rule? |
| Proof and return | What observable outcome, retest, residual risk, next review and report/lesson impact closes the loop? |

Do not turn a missing answer into a capability failure, a questionnaire signal into a finding, a planned action into actual performance, a service suggestion into a contract, or a repaired asset into restored function. Link restricted source detail in the proper Member record; do not reproduce unnecessary PII in the client summary or enterprise method library.

## Exact product transfer

| From → to | Required transfer and receiver check |
|---|---|
| Protection Advisor → Level 1 scope | Send only consented, relevant signals, Member objective, strengths and information gaps. Explain a preliminary hypothesis and the value of the first integrated Level 1 package; let the Member decline or request separate service discussion. A professional confirms the scope and authority. |
| SalINTEL™ → SalTHIRA™ | Transfer source and contrary-evidence IDs, actual versus planned performance, affected people/functions, scenario leads, possible requirements, confidence, falsifier and decision-changing collection questions. SalTHIRA checks applicability and does not silently upgrade a lead to a finding. |
| SalTHIRA™ → SalOPS™ | Transfer each ranked decision ID with all six-step reasoning, target, available capability, gap, five-mission-area/32-capability relevance, assumptions, alternatives, urgency and resource competition. SalOPS checks feasibility, authority, safety and capacity before proposing or doing work. |
| SalOPS™ → SalCAPA™ | Transfer proposed, selected, authorized, performed and validated states separately, with owner, cost/resource basis, deviations, observed outcome, proof, residual risk and retest. SalCAPA does not mark a recommendation complete from a purchase or closed ticket. |
| SalCAPA™ → Member and enterprise learning | Reconcile exact same-level report versions, conflicts and one clear decision/checklist; state next-level value and unresolved matters. Generalize only reviewed, member-neutral lessons. A report update returns through impact review and one governed report identity. |

At each transfer, the receiving analyst records **accepted, returned for correction, qualified with a stated limit, or excluded with a reason**. Record the source version, receiver, date and effect on the decision. If the upstream report is still working, the downstream work may be a working hypothesis but must not be issued as if the upstream report were approved. Urgent lawful life-safety action follows its separate authority.

## Coherence and simplicity check

Before a change is accepted, one reviewer reads the Charter, decisions, approved artifact, actual current implementation and affected reports together. The reviewer checks: (1) terms and level/phase/entitlement distinction; (2) the same decision ID and source meaning across products; (3) strengths, contrary evidence and unknowns; (4) rating direction and denominator; (5) target versus observed capability and time to usable recovery; (6) actionable narrative and matching checklist; (7) no-action/alternatives, resources and competing demand; (8) status/authority, PII boundary and specialist gate; (9) Member-facing clarity, question burden and accessibility; and (10) report title, confidential cover, blank page two, page numbering, Arial/Times typography, rendered quality and offered-format parity. Test a favorable, adverse, unknown and conflicting case. Ask a colleague unfamiliar with the method to explain the Member's choice and next step without coaching; revise language that fails this readback.

The closeout record lists exact artifacts and versions, source/search boundary, contradictions, corrections, test/readback evidence, impacted active and issued reports, owner, reviewer, remaining gates and next action. Record **met**, **pending with named gate**, or **not applicable with reason** for each affected surface. A Charter edit is method incorporation; an edited template is a candidate; a working Member report is not an issued determination; a passing bounded test is not deployment. Use current handoff and governed registers to preserve those distinctions.

## Exact-source and report-release companion [SAL-D064]

For material Member work, pair this handoff check with the Charter's source-to-release integrity rule. Find the actual engagement inventory rather than relying on a mirror, citation count or previous summary; ledger exact versions, handling and reviewed/excluded disposition. Compare dated operating instructions for real changes in authority, sites, alternates, capacity and fallback, then ask the responsible owner what is currently approved and what has been exercised. Treat message issuance, receipt, understanding and action as separate evidence states. Keep historical contact records and access details restricted, and transfer only the minimum necessary aggregates and source IDs into reports. Before release, inspect every rendered page of the exact file and record remaining native-format, accessibility and parity gates. Preserve original historical report artifacts and model versions so a later renderer cannot silently rewrite an earlier result. Record unresolved source and retrieval gaps as named gates, with a Member-bound audit note where details are sensitive.
