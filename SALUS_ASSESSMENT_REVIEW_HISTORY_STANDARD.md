# Salus assessment review history standard

## Authority and purpose

SAL-D151 / SALUS-ASSESSMENT-REVIEW-HISTORY-001. The Salus owner directed this requirement on 22 November 2026. Apply to every assessment, material reassessment and SalCAPA integrated report, with consistent references in its same-level SalINTEL, SalTHIRA and SalOPS reports. Apply immediately to the authorized private assessment; enterprise promotion follows existing exact-revision change control.

Show how the assessment and reporting work was actually reviewed. This documents the controlled Salus process; a review count alone does not establish quality, source qualification, legal defensibility, proprietary rights, professional approval or permission to issue.

## Required human review account

Include a clearly labeled review-history statement in SalCAPA, readable without interpreting the private audit log. State the as-of date, assessment/report edition, number of documented human assessment-and-reporting review events, number involving the Salus Investigations Officer or Salus Investigator, number involving other Salus personnel, review scope, disposition and current-edition approval status. Use approved role descriptions in the client report; retain authorized identities privately. Identify whether the Investigator is included in the personnel total. Do not add overlapping role subtotals as if they were independent events or people.

Count a distinct review event once when the retained record identifies the reviewer, role, actual work reviewed, edition or exact artifact identity, date or explicitly unknown date, result and evidence reference. A joint review is one event with its participating roles; one reviewer examining four reports is one package review unless four separate reviews are evidenced. A continuation or repeated confirmation of the same event is not another review. A chat comment, requested edit, filename, report version, assistant assertion or number of source decisions does not prove another complete assessment review.

Separate whole-package review, limited report/section review, source qualification, intended-reader testing, specialist legal/financial/clinical review and final issue approval. Report each actual scope. Eighteen source dispositions from one review are one source-review event with eighteen decisions, not eighteen whole-assessment reviews. Historical approval applies only to the reviewed edition and scope; it does not approve later editions, unreviewed material or external sending.

When a complete count cannot be verified, state the confirmed minimum and the missing evidence. Distinguish zero documented additional-personnel reviews from proof that no additional reviews occurred. Do not invent a number, imply independent personnel, count software as a person, assign an unverified credential or ask the owner to certify routine assistant conformance. Obtain a factual review record only when substantive human judgment is required or an actual human-review claim needs confirmation.

## Automated and assistant review account

Report automated or assistant-assisted substantive passes, editorial/format passes and file/functional checks separately from human review. Retain the eight distinct substantive re-passes and terminal editorial pass required by SAL-D144; do not present nine check labels as nine people, independent opinions or human approvals. An assistant or agent is never counted as Salus personnel or the Salus Investigator. Identify the exact edition and actual checked scope, and retain limitations, corrections and rechecks. Do not infer cumulative lifetime passes by multiplying a version number by eight.

## Evidence and acceptance

Retain one private review-event ledger linked to exact report versions and source decisions, with unique event IDs, participating roles, human/automated classification, scope, result, provenance, limitations and any superseding event. Keep human authentication and qualification evidence separate from a schema check. Reconcile the displayed counts with the ledger and check that reporting across all four reports is consistent. Verify that no current approval, independent-reader test, specialist endorsement, qualification or issue state is inferred from historical or automated reviews.

Use `tools/assessment_review_history.py` to check event identity and count separation when available. Its result checks the supplied record only; it cannot establish that a human performed a review or is qualified. Preserve OPM-referenced Charter formatting, privacy, originals, evidence limits, worksheet controls, the original 24-hour clock, all other acceptance gates and disabled readers. No sending, deployment, access expansion, source promotion or protected merge is authorized by this standard.
