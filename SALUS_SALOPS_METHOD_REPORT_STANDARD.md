# SalOPS™ Method and Report Standard

**Status:** Approved enterprise method and report structure, effective 27 September 2026 (SAL-D045). **Owner:** Salus Solutions. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential. Apply with the Program Charter, Decision Register, approved system presentation, three-level framework, SalINTEL/SalTHIRA standard, and current controlled templates. This standard authorizes methodology and report design, not live operations, spending, pricing, software deployment, or any Protected Member finding.

## Purpose and domain structure

SalOPS™ converts a same-level SalTHIRA™ priority into a bounded, feasible option; it performs a task only after the Protected Member and Salus establish authority and scope. Its four operating domains are **Readiness and Preparedness; Protective Operations; Incident and Crisis Response; Recovery and Sustainment**. Crosscutting screens are the five mission areas and all 32 core capabilities, supplemented by sector-specific capabilities where relevant. Recommendations are decision support; operational authority must be separately recorded.

## Sequential levels

| Level | Required SalOPS™ report depth | Activity gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Initial Pass | Ranked options, immediate decisions, constraints, resources and Level 2 proof needs; bounded plan, readiness check or advisory work only if separately authorized. | Qualified same-level SalINTEL™ and SalTHIRA™ inputs; explicit activity authorization. |
| 2 Focused Assessment | Validated partner, target, cost and performance assumptions; targeted plan, training, exercise, protective or incident-support package and corrective-action design. | Qualified integrated Level 1 package, named Level 2 scope and separate activity authorization. |
| 3 Deep Dive | Approved complex or multi-site work, sustained program, recovery sequence, exercise program and measured revalidation. | Qualified integrated Level 2 package, specialist/resource approval and separate activity authorization. |

Urgent competent-authority life-safety measures are not delayed for a higher package. No level or report silently authorizes live response, staffing, field work, purchases, vendor awards or a contract.

## Input and option-to-action method

Use exact source versions and stable IDs linking SalINTEL™ source, contradiction, assets and essential functions to SalTHIRA™ scenario, target, demonstrated capacity, gap, treatment, priority, uncertainty and residual risk. Capture the Member's mission, authority, existing plans, personnel, facilities, partner/contract terms, funding, insurance, permits, accessibility and privacy restrictions. State missing proof and its effect on a decision.

Use these distinct states: **proposed, screened, offered, authorized, planned, performed, verified, sustained or closed, deferred, rejected**. Never conflate them. Before authorization, compare no action and alternatives on consequence, time to harm, essential-function effect, expected capability gain, evidence confidence, cost and lead time, partner availability under competing demand, safety, accessibility, legal/contract terms, side effects, reversibility and sequence. Explain qualitative judgment; do not present an unvalidated score as measured performance.

For authorization, record the accountable Member and Salus decision makers, scope, period, resource and cost ceiling, conditions, information access, safety and stop authority. A plan states tasks, command/partner interface, communications, milestones and acceptance measures. Performance records actual work, deviations, incidents and costs. Verification compares observed results with an approved baseline, target and acceptance measure; records residual gaps, owner, due date and retest. Deferred or rejected options retain authority, reason, residual exposure, interim control and recheck trigger. Return reviewed results and lessons through SalCAPA™ to requalify SalINTEL™ evidence and SalTHIRA™ assessments.

## Operating controls

Map member policy authority, Salus lead, incident-command or unified-command interface where applicable, alternates, vendor authority, escalation and stop-work. Crisis communications specify trigger, approver, message/channel, accessible reach, receipt confirmation, correction, media voice and offline fallback. Match activities to relevant permits, licenses, contracts, insurance, worker and public safety, privacy and information controls. A missing record is an unknown, not a proved failure or violation. Keep person-specific, resident, medical, credential and tactical detail in restricted member records. Reviewed member-neutral lessons may enter enterprise learning without copying Protected Member facts.

## Required stand-alone report

Name the report `[Protected Member customer name] - SalOPS Level [1, 2, or 3]`; put status, date and version in front matter, not as a substitute for the level. Use a Salus Confidential cover identifying approved viewers, use, distribution, storage, restricted annexes and release authority; an **entirely blank page 2**; and body from page 3. Nonblank pages show `page X of Y`. Arial is used for titles, section headings, running heads and table column headings; Times New Roman for body, table body, notes and page footers. Apply SAL-D043 and rendered DOCX/PDF/print review.

The body includes a plain-language executive summary and Bottom Line Up Front; titled tables for ranked decisions/options and four-domain posture; represented level, scope and predecessor lineage; desired outcomes and decision standard; four-domain analysis; crisis communications; command, personnel, contractors and safety; ranked option/action register with states; five mission areas, 32 core capabilities and sector additions; resources, finance and competing demand; schedule and verification; residual exposure and next-level needs; SalCAPA™ handoff; sources, contradictions and restricted annex index. Each material recommendation states the observed/reported/inferred basis, owner, time, resources, prerequisite, authorization state, measure and uncertainty. Educate the Member on why each priority matters and what changes the decision.

## Approval and release boundary

The approved enterprise method and report structure are reusable. The working `SalOPS Report Template DRAFT.docx` and `Tierra Linda Ranch - SalOPS Level 1.docx` remain design references, not approved populated reports or operational orders. Qualify the exact member evidence, reconcile same-level inputs, complete analyst and authorized human review, perform the report-specific conformance check, and issue only through the governed record. Preserve member isolation, immutable release/version history and SAL-D027 impact review.
