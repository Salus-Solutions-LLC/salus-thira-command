# SalOPS™ Method and Report Standard

**Status:** Approved enterprise method and report structure, effective 27 September 2026 (SAL-D045); multidisciplinary assurance amendment approved 28 September 2026 (SAL-D050); organizational-health method approved 28 September 2026 (SAL-D051); analytical assumptions-to-action amendment approved 28 September 2026 (SAL-D055). **Owner:** Salus Solutions. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential. Apply with the Program Charter, Decision Register, approved system presentation, three-level framework, SalINTEL/SalTHIRA standard, and current controlled templates. This standard authorizes methodology and report design, not live operations, spending, pricing, software deployment, or any Protected Member finding.

## Purpose and domain structure

SalOPS™ converts a same-level SalTHIRA™ priority into a bounded, feasible option; it performs a task only after the Protected Member and Salus establish authority and scope. Its four operating domains are **Readiness and Preparedness; Protective Operations; Incident and Crisis Response; Recovery and Sustainment**. Crosscutting screens are the five mission areas and all 32 core capabilities, supplemented by sector-specific capabilities where relevant. Recommendations are decision support; operational authority must be separately recorded.

## Sequential levels

| Level | Required SalOPS™ report depth | Activity gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Initial Pass | Ranked options, immediate decisions, constraints, resources and Level 2 proof needs; bounded plan, readiness check or advisory work only if separately authorized. | Qualified same-level SalINTEL™ and SalTHIRA™ inputs; explicit activity authorization. |
| 2 Focused Assessment | Validated partner, target, cost and performance assumptions; targeted plan, training, exercise, protective or incident-support package and corrective-action design. | Qualified integrated Level 1 package, named Level 2 scope and separate activity authorization. |
| 3 Deep Dive | Approved complex or multi-site work, sustained program, recovery sequence, exercise program and measured revalidation. | Qualified integrated Level 2 package, specialist/resource approval and separate activity authorization. |

Urgent competent-authority life-safety measures are not delayed for a higher package. No level or report silently authorizes live response, staffing, field work, purchases, vendor awards or a contract.

## Input and option-to-action method

Use exact source versions and stable IDs linking SalINTEL™ source, contradiction, assets and essential functions to SalTHIRA™ scenario, target, demonstrated capacity, gap, treatment, priority, uncertainty and residual risk. Capture the Member's mission, authority, existing plans, personnel, facilities, partner/contract terms, funding, insurance, permits, accessibility and privacy restrictions. State missing proof and its effect on a decision.

Use these distinct states: **proposed, screened, offered, authorized, planned, performed, verified, sustained or closed, deferred, rejected**. Never conflate them. Before authorization, compare no action and alternatives on consequence, time to harm, essential-function effect, expected capability gain, evidence confidence, cost and lead time, partner availability under competing demand, safety, accessibility, legal/contract terms, side effects, reversibility and sequence. Explain qualitative judgment; do not present an unvalidated score as measured performance.

For authorization, record the accountable Member and Salus decision makers, scope, period, resource and cost ceiling, conditions, information access, safety and stop authority. A plan states tasks, command/partner interface, communications, milestones and acceptance measures. Performance records actual work, deviations, incidents and costs. Verification compares observed results with an approved baseline, target and acceptance measure; records residual gaps, owner, due date and retest. Deferred or rejected options retain authority, reason, residual exposure, interim control and recheck trigger. Return reviewed results and lessons through SalCAPA™ to requalify SalINTEL™ evidence and SalTHIRA™ assessments.

## Relevant Salus service discovery and referral [SAL-D047]

At intake, option screening, after-action review and report handoff, identify whether the Member's documented need could be served by another currently approved Salus service. Use `https://www.salus.solutions/services` and its relevant linked service pages for the current public service names and descriptions, and record the access date. Recheck the site before any member-facing revision; validate actual service authority and capacity separately. The analyst or service lead shall map the specific need and evidence to the service outcome, identify prerequisites and a qualified service owner, distinguish a present action need from a Level 2/3 assessment question, and offer a short discussion or scoped proposal. Potential categories include licensed armed security where lawful and appropriate, unarmed protective services, security or emergency planning, training and exercises, crisis communications, continuity and recovery support, and specialist investigation or assessment only when separately lawful and authorized. The current approved service catalog, licensing, insurance, staff/partner availability, jurisdiction, conflicts and Member consent control what may actually be offered. Do not imply an armed response, availability, guaranteed result, price or contract from a report.

A referral or cross-service opportunity is a separate **offered** item, not a SalOPS task or risk treatment until the Member authorizes a defined scope. Record the linked finding/option ID, need, expected benefit, alternative, limits, service owner, Member decision, follow-up route and outcome. Show meaningful services that may help even when they are not part of the current package; avoid generic catalogs, pressure or withholding urgent safety advice. Refer approved offers and decisions to SalCAPA™ for tracking within the Member boundary.

## Operating controls

Map member policy authority, Salus lead, incident-command or unified-command interface where applicable, alternates, vendor authority, escalation and stop-work. Crisis communications specify trigger, approver, message/channel, accessible reach, receipt confirmation, correction, media voice and offline fallback. Match activities to relevant permits, licenses, contracts, insurance, worker and public safety, privacy and information controls. A missing record is an unknown, not a proved failure or violation. Keep person-specific, resident, medical, credential and tactical detail in restricted member records. Reviewed member-neutral lessons may enter enterprise learning without copying Protected Member facts.

## Required stand-alone report

Name the report `[Protected Member customer name] - SalOPS Level [1, 2, or 3]`; put status, date and version in front matter, not as a substitute for the level. Use a Salus Confidential cover identifying approved viewers, use, distribution, storage, restricted annexes and release authority; an **entirely blank page 2**; and body from page 3. Nonblank pages show `page X of Y`. Arial is used for titles, section headings, running heads and table column headings; Times New Roman for body, table body, notes and page footers. Apply SAL-D043 and rendered DOCX/PDF/print review.

The body includes a plain-language executive summary and Bottom Line Up Front; titled tables for ranked decisions/options and four-domain posture; represented level, scope and predecessor lineage; desired outcomes and decision standard; four-domain analysis; crisis communications; command, personnel, contractors and safety; ranked option/action register with states; five mission areas, 32 core capabilities and sector additions; resources, finance and competing demand; schedule and verification; residual exposure and next-level needs; a clearly labeled relevant Salus services section with evidence-linked options, prerequisites and a voluntary request-for-scope route; SalCAPA™ handoff; sources, contradictions and restricted annex index. Each material recommendation states the observed/reported/inferred basis, owner, time, resources, prerequisite, authorization state, measure and uncertainty. Educate the Member on why each priority matters and what changes the decision.

## Multidisciplinary option and report screen [SAL-D050]

For each material option, screen the applicable professional lenses in `proposals/SALUS_MULTIDISCIPLINARY_ASSURANCE_REVIEW_20260928.md`. In addition to the existing option-to-action record, name: operating authority and qualified/licensed specialist; legal, permit, code, contract and insurance gates; capital cost, cash timing and funding source; supplier/public-resource competition; power/fuel and other common-mode dependencies; accessible and trusted communications; human performance under stress; affected groups; safe stop condition; verification method; and residual exposure. Mark unknown or not applicable explicitly. A report's recommendation does not approve construction, investigation, procurement, deployment or field operations. At Level 1, present bounded choices and specialist triggers; at Level 2, validate selected feasibility; at Level 3, compare approved complex alternatives and measured performance. Add a concise multidisciplinary feasibility and assurance table to the stand-alone SalOPS report, linked to exact SalINTEL evidence and SalTHIRA target/gap IDs.

## Organizational health and command options [SAL-D051]

For material leadership, authority, process or communication gaps, construct an evidence-linked change case from the approved organizational-health method: current strength to preserve, observed mechanism, no-action and proportionate alternatives, legal and practical decision owner, resources and constraints, expected daily and stress benefit, baseline and target, adverse effects, retest and residual risk. Examples may include bounded delegation, alternate training, decision brief and escalation design, promulgation checks, crisis communications, command-transfer rehearsal and corrective-action review. These are options, not automatic prescriptions or authorized services. Match the option to the approved SalOPS™ action state and record separate authorization before implementation.

## Organizational-health cross-product handoff [SAL-D052]

Carry each material organizational-health option with its originating Advisor signal if any, qualified SalINTEL™ domain/source ID, SalTHIRA™ scenario and target effect, Member decision owner, action state, daily/stress benefit and proof. An Advisor answer alone does not authorize or justify an operational service. Use the approved integration crosswalk and return verified performance to SalCAPA™ for reassessment.

## Required-information trace [SAL-D053]

When an organizational or multidisciplinary gap leads to an option, link the specific SalINTEL™ request/evidence status and SalTHIRA™ decision effect. Identify which missing document, interview, specialist review or exercise would change feasibility, authority, cost, safety or predicted benefit. A generic request list does not authorize collection or a service.

## SAL-D054 operating-option challenge

Use the applicable sub-lenses in `SALUS_EXPANDED_LENS_DISCOVERY_METHOD_20260928.md` to test whether an offered option depends on unconfirmed dispatch or mutual aid, access, fuel, water, communications, veterinary/medical care, permits, insurance, volunteers or a single vendor. State which counterpart can confirm the dependency, the safe stopping condition, alternative mode, adverse or unequal effects, funding and proof. Match the selected SalTHIRA™ scenario and same level; distinguish offered, authorized, implemented and validated states. A chat assertion or paper plan cannot establish operational readiness. Any change to an existing controlled report/template follows SAL-D027 and SAL-D043.

## SAL-D055 assumption-to-option narrative and checklist

Each material SalOPS™ option and recommendation uses the same stable ID in a plain-language narrative and a verifiable action checklist under `SALUS_ANALYTICAL_ASSUMPTIONS_TO_ACTION_STANDARD.md`. The narrative names its SalINTEL™ evidence and working assumption, SalTHIRA™ scenario/target/gap, alternative and no-action consequence, causal benefit, constraints and residual risk. The checklist gives the specific proposed steps, Member/Salus authority, timing, cost/resource basis, prerequisite, partner and competing demand, safety/stop or fallback, completion proof and validation trigger. Mark suggested, offered, selected, authorized, implemented and validated states separately. Do not present a checklist as an order or a service commitment. At Level 1 recommend bounded, feasible actions while explicitly stating unconfirmed assumptions; at Levels 2/3 revise action detail after validation or stress testing. Keep Member-facing narrative and checklist consistent with the SalCAPA™ master action record and controlled report versions.

## SAL-D056 location-dependent options

Distinguish map-screened options from field, owner and specialist validated work. Recommend specific route, lifeline, access, monitoring, continuity and responder-coordination steps with accountable owner, prerequisite, authority, safe-stop condition and proof. A mapped pipeline is never an excavation locate; a mapped route is never proof of access or load capacity. Keep precise private access and critical-system overlays restricted. Link each proposed task to the same-level SalINTEL™ source and SalTHIRA™ scenario/target/gap; separately authorize execution under `SALUS_GEOSPATIAL_INFRASTRUCTURE_INTELLIGENCE_STANDARD.md`.
