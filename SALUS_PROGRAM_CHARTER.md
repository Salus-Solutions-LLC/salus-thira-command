# Salus Solutions — Master Program Charter

## Purpose

Salus Solutions LLC is an intelligence, security, investigations, emergency-management, and operational-support company. The Salus program turns fragmented information into qualified intelligence, prioritized risk decisions, coordinated actions, and defensible records for Protected Members.

### The Salus namesake and Member promise [SAL-D058]

Salus, the Roman personification of health, safety and public welfare, expresses the purpose Marc sets for the company: improve Protected Members' **safety, security and wellbeing** in ordinary life and under stress. Define whose lives, essential services, livelihoods, animals and communities a decision affects; educate the Member, preserve dignity and privacy, make specific feasible recommendations, support authorized action and test whether outcomes improved. This is an operating standard across Protection Advisor, SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™ and SalCAPA™, not a promise that risk can be eliminated or an authorization to sell or perform unapproved services.

## Approved operating architecture

### Media-neutral file screening [SAL-D091]

No Salus-controlled upload, source ingest, preview, working-report transfer or Protected Member download may bypass malware screening because its content is audio, video, a photograph, map, diagram, PDF, Office file, handwritten scan, archive or an unfamiliar type. Acceptance limits and staged large-media transfer may vary by workflow, but every accepted object needs one tenant-bound quarantine identity, original-byte hash and object version, current scanner/engine evidence, and a result bound to that exact object before it becomes available for parsing, preview, sharing or download. A `Clean` result is one security gate, never proof that the file's factual contents are correct or that OCR, transcription, spatial interpretation or expert review is complete.

Keep infected, missing-result, delayed, errored, timed-out, too-large, encrypted/password-protected, deeply nested and otherwise unscanned content inaccessible. Give the sender a plain-language retry or secure alternative and a governed reviewer path. Do not silently treat an unsupported file as safe or discard material subject to retention or legal hold. Test real representative files and failure cases across documents, images, audio/video, geospatial formats and archives on the exact release path; record scanner limits, throughput, cost, isolation, recovery and rollback. The current production package intake remains locked until that proof exists. This requirement applies to future intake channels as well as Protection Advisor and SalCAPA™; a new workflow must not reuse a `Clean` label for an unscanned transformation or replacement object.

### Source-to-release integrity [SAL-D064]

For a material Member assessment or report revision, first locate the actual engagement source set and make an exact-file ledger: filename or controlled ID, source location, date and version, hash where available, handling restriction, reviewed or excluded status, decision relevance and unresolved access gaps. A cited source count, a synced-folder count or a prior chat claim is not proof that every file was read or that the current version was used. Reconcile material findings against the original records, prior issued reports and current governing sources before a same-level handoff or release; state the actual review boundary and the items still pending.

Compare dated plans, notices, exercises, incident records and other operational instructions by **what changed in authority, capability, location, fallback, population, timing and conditions**. Distinguish a true contradiction from a later revision, a different audience or a different responsible agency. When a change affects an emergency role, route, command post, alternate site, power source, staffing or service promise, identify the currently adopted instruction and obtain the owner's confirmation or performance evidence; carry unresolved differences as qualified questions through SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™ and SalCAPA™. A plan's description is not proof that personnel, equipment, partners or alternate facilities are ready.

For actual event communications, separate **message composed, sent, delivered, received, understood and acted on**. A sent warning does not prove delivery, accessible reach, safe compliance or conditions on every route. Use dispatch records, channel coverage, acknowledgements, field observations and after-action evidence where available; preserve unknowns and competing information. Treat historic directories as restricted contact leads, not current household, assistance or readiness rosters. Verify consent, date, reach and aggregate completeness through a separately protected process. Keep names, contact details, medical or mobility information, access credentials, gate codes and detailed security routes out of general client summaries and member-neutral methods unless a specific authorized need and protected distribution justify them. Never infer individual misconduct or vulnerability from a directory entry.

Before delivery, render the **exact** report version and inspect every page, its confidential cover, wholly blank second page where required, `page X of Y`, Arial headings and Times New Roman body, readable tables, navigation and accessibility, and substantive DOCX/PDF/print agreement for formats offered. Record renderer, file hash, page count, defects, fixes, rerender, reviewer and any native Word or production-print parity still open. Automated extraction, fonts, page-count checks and contact sheets are useful screens but do not by themselves establish release readiness. Preserve the existing governed report identity and immutable predecessor. A historical result must be served from its original authorized artifact or requalified under an explicit new version; do not silently regenerate it with later scoring, content or report logic. If the original cannot be retrieved, give the recipient an accurate review path rather than mislabeling a new rendering as the old report. ### Protected Members

Protected Members are the people and organizations Salus serves. In architecture graphics, Protected Members appear at the top.

### SalCAPA™ — Secure Coordination Environment

SalCAPA provides the secure environment for intake, exchange, coordination, delivery, and records. Its operating cycle is Collect, Analyze, Prioritize, Act. Approved expressions include “See • Coordinate • Act” and “See, Decide, Coordinate, Act, Prove.”

Primary member-facing outputs:

- Intelligence and alerts.
- Assessments and priorities.
- Workflows and requests.
- Salus experts, support, and service outcomes.

### SalINTEL™ — Qualified Intelligence

SalINTEL converts member insight, human intelligence, OSINT, partner capability, and research into qualified and validated intelligence.

### SalTHIRA™ — Threat and Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment

SalTHIRA evaluates threats, hazards, vulnerabilities, capabilities, consequences, priorities, and recommendations using six steps:

1. Identify threats and hazards.
2. Give threats and hazards context.
3. Establish capability targets.
4. Assess capabilities.
5. Identify risks and address gaps.
6. Describe the impact of changes so leaders can prioritize what should be fixed first.

SalTHIRA is a practical budget-prioritization and decision-support method, not merely a risk list.

### SalOPS™ — Operational Readiness, Protective Services, and Incident Support

SalOPS implements approved actions, sustains readiness, provides protective and incident services, and feeds results, field reporting, and lessons back into SalCAPA.

## Approved information flow

Information and reporting sources enter SalCAPA. Relevant information and requirements flow to SalINTEL. Qualified intelligence informs SalTHIRA. Findings, assessments, priorities, and recommendations return through SalCAPA to Protected Members. Approved actions, requests, and tasks flow to SalOPS. Operational results and lessons return to SalCAPA.
