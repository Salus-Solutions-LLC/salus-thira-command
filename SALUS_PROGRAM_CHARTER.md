# Salus Solutions — Master Program Charter

## Purpose

Salus Solutions LLC is an intelligence, security, investigations, emergency-management, and operational-support company. The Salus program turns fragmented information into qualified intelligence, prioritized risk decisions, coordinated actions, and defensible records for Protected Members.

### The Salus namesake and Member promise [SAL-D058]

Salus, the Roman personification of health, safety and public welfare, expresses the purpose Marc sets for the company: improve Protected Members' **safety, security and wellbeing** in ordinary life and under stress. Define whose lives, essential services, livelihoods, animals and communities a decision affects; educate the Member, preserve dignity and privacy, make specific feasible recommendations, support authorized action and test whether outcomes improved. This is an operating standard across Protection Advisor, SalINTEL™, SalTHIRA™, SalOPS™ and SalCAPA™, not a promise that risk can be eliminated or an authorization to sell or perform unapproved services.

## Approved operating architecture

### Standing coherence and plain-language handoff gate [SAL-D063]

Every material enhancement, learning event, questionnaire or report revision, service description, and same-level SalINTEL™ → SalTHIRA™ → SalOPS™ → SalCAPA™ transfer receives a recorded accuracy, consistency, continuity and clarity check under `SALUS_PORTFOLIO_COHERENCE_AND_HANDOFF_STANDARD.md`. Review the governing decision, approved artifact, actual current implementation and affected Member work together. Keep one decision ID, source and contrary evidence, confidence, falsifier, level, action state and proof meaning intact across products. The receiver accepts, returns, qualifies or excludes the item with a reason; silence is not acceptance. Test favorable, adverse, unknown and conflicting cases, then use a nonexpert readback to confirm that personnel and the Protected Member can explain what is known, why it matters, the choice, owner and proof. Record the exact reviewed boundary and open gates. A method update does not itself revise an issued report or deploy a workflow.

### Protected Members

Protected Members are the people and organizations Salus serves. In architecture graphics, Protected Members appear at the top.

### SalCAPA™ — Secure Coordination Environment

SalCAPA provides the secure environment for intake, exchange, coordination, delivery, and records. Its operating cycle is Collect, Analyze, Prioritize, Act. Approved expressions include “See • Coordinate • Act” and “See, Decide, Coordinate, Act, Prove.”

Primary member-facing outputs:

- Intelligence and alerts.
- Assessments and priorities.
- Workflows and requests.
- Salus experts, support, and service outcomes.

### SalINTEL™ — Qualified Intelligence

SalINTEL converts member insight, human intelligence, OSINT, partner capability, and research into qualified and validated intelligence.

### SalTHIRA™ — Threat and Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment

SalTHIRA evaluates threats, hazards, vulnerabilities, capabilities, consequences, priorities, and recommendations using six steps:

1. Identify threats and hazards.
2. Give threats and hazards context.
3. Establish capability targets.
4. Assess capabilities.
5. Identify risks and address gaps.
6. Describe the impact of changes so leaders can prioritize what should be fixed first.

SalTHIRA is a practical budget-prioritization and decision-support method, not merely a risk list.

### SalOPS™ — Operational Readiness, Protective Services, and Incident Support

SalOPS implements approved actions, sustains readiness, provides protective and incident services, and feeds results, field reporting, and lessons back into SalCAPA.

## Approved information flow

Information and reporting sources enter SalCAPA. Relevant information and requirements flow to SalINTEL. Qualified intelligence informs SalTHIRA. Findings, assessments, priorities, and recommendations return through SalCAPA to Protected Members. Approved actions, requests, and tasks flow to SalOPS. Operational results and lessons return to SalCAPA.

## Governed enterprise learning and continuous improvement

Salus operates as a cumulative learning system across Protected Members, sectors, methods, strategies, objectives, tactics, resources, statutory and regulatory contexts, competing-resource conditions, outcomes, and derivatives. Each authorized engagement should test relevant prior knowledge and record transferable lessons; qualified lessons and subsequent field results should improve future research, assessments, services, and decisions. Cross-domain connections and counterexamples are part of the method, not isolated repositories.

The learning loop is: capture source-bound observations and results; generalize member-neutral patterns; compare them with other sectors and domains; qualify provenance, applicability, confidence, currency, restrictions and conflicts; approve controlled changes; apply the current version; and measure results and feed lessons back through SalCAPA. Preserve version history and superseded material. Record what was not searched or remains unknown instead of asserting an exhaustive view.

Salus may correlate authorized information internally across engagements, but enterprise learning does not grant automatic cross-Protected-Member disclosure. Keep identifiable Protected Member evidence and security-sensitive derivatives segregated. A reusable enterprise record may cite a restricted source without copying its protected contents. Apply legal, contractual, privacy, licensing, access, purpose and human disclosure controls to every derivative. Human approval remains required for consequential methodology changes, final findings, recommendations, risk acceptance, external publication and service commitments. See SAL-D025 and the approved Knowledge and Records Architecture.

## Sequential integrated service packages and level labeling

Every customer first engages in a Level 1 package. A qualified Level 1 package is the prerequisite for Level 2; a qualified Level 2 package is the prerequisite for Level 3. Existing lawful, current and reliable evidence may be reviewed and accepted to satisfy lower-level checks, but the lower-level package, same-level product handoffs and human qualification must still be documented. Do not duplicate collection solely to recreate a level. This sequential rule supersedes earlier direct-entry language in the three-level framework (SAL-D032).

At **each level**, SalINTEL qualifies the evidence and identifies current conclusions and missing information; SalTHIRA applies all six steps at that same level to establish capability targets, assess gaps, compare treatments and set priorities with level-appropriate confidence; SalOPS provides same-level recommendations and options, and performs only separately authorized activities. SalOPS actions may be urgent operational measures or documented prerequisites for the next package. SalCAPA tracks the integrated package, component statuses, prerequisites, handoffs, decisions, prices, approved actions, report versions and lessons. SalINTEL completion is not a reason to defer all SalTHIRA or SalOPS work until a deeper level. A required safety action is never held for a commercial upgrade.

Every proposal, work order, work product and report prominently states the **level represented**, requested/authorized/completed status and prerequisite package in its title or front matter and executive summary. Each material section marks where its evidence and analysis support the current level, what needs the next level, and any SalOPS action needed now or to enable progression. Report statements must distinguish a working Level 1 picture from a completed qualified Level 1 package and from Level 2/3 determinations. Refine and approve the applicable methodologies and report standards first. Then issue the level-labeled SalINTEL report, the level-labeled SalTHIRA report, and the level-labeled SalOPS report in order. SalCAPA finally aggregates their approved findings, priorities, decisions, limits, actions and next-level needs into a level-labeled SalCAPA report. Link exact product versions and reconcile disagreements; aggregation does not substitute for the three product reports or duplicate an existing governed report (SAL-D033). Marketing and pricing must describe the required sequence and each level's actual scope, value, limits and human-approved price; they may not present Level 2 or Level 3 as a stand-alone first purchase. Protected Membership entitlements remain a separate dimension.

## Analyst judgment to actionable recommendations

Salus shall answer decision questions with defensible sources and documented limits, then exercise qualified analyst judgment. When a material answer is incomplete, state a precise, testable **working assumption**, its basis, contrary or alternative explanation, consequence if true or false, confidence and validation trigger. Do not present an assumption as a verified finding, noncompliance, person-level allegation or permission to act. A material concern shall not end as a question list alone: produce a proportional current-level recommendation, including a reversible interim step or no-action choice when warranted, and state what Level 2 or 3 evidence would change it.

Each material recommendation in the four product reports has a **paired narrative and action checklist** using the same ID. The narrative explains why the issue matters, the causal mechanism, observed strengths, evidence and assumption, alternatives, expected benefit, constraints, adverse effects and residual risk. The checklist states verifiable steps, owner and approval authority, timing or trigger, resources and dependencies, safe stop/fallback, proof and review. Compare no action and feasible alternatives for consequential choices. Label suggested, selected, authorized, implemented, validated, deferred or rejected states accurately. Neither a recommendation nor a report authorizes field work, purchases or a service. Salus does not wait for perfect evidence to give proportionate Level 1 options or urgent safety guidance under appropriate authority. The controlling detail is `SALUS_ANALYTICAL_ASSUMPTIONS_TO_ACTION_STANDARD.md` [SAL-D055].

Protection Advisor Parts 1 and 2 may explain preliminary answer-based hypotheses and offer bounded next steps, with Part 1 remaining anonymous and low burden. Formal analyst assumptions, findings and action checklists begin in the authorized assessment chain. Existing report identities, approved Word templates, live questionnaire/report outputs and issued findings change only through SAL-D027 impact, exact-version review, SAL-D043 rendered conformance, Protected Member isolation and governed release.
