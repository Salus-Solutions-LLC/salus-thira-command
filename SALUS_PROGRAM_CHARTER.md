# Salus Solutions — Master Program Charter

## Purpose

Salus Solutions LLC is an intelligence, security, investigations, emergency-management, and operational-support company. The Salus program turns fragmented information into qualified intelligence, prioritized risk decisions, coordinated actions, and defensible records for Protected Members.

## Approved operating architecture

### Protected Members

Protected Members are the people and organizations Salus serves. In architecture graphics, Protected Members appear at the top.

### SalCAPA™ — Secure Coordination Environment

SalCAPA provides the secure environment for intake, exchange, coordination, delivery, and records. Its operating cycle is Collect, Analyze, Prioritize, Act. Approved expressions include “See • Coordinate • Act” and “See, Decide, Coordinate, Act, Prove.”

Primary member-facing outputs:

- Intelligence and alerts.
- Assessments and priorities.
- Workflows and requests.
- Salus experts, support, and service outcomes.

### SalINTEL™ — Qualified Intelligence

SalINTEL converts member insight, human intelligence, OSINT, partner capability, and research into qualified and validated intelligence.

### SalTHIRA™ — Threat and Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment

SalTHIRA evaluates threats, hazards, vulnerabilities, capabilities, consequences, priorities, and recommendations using six steps:

1. Identify threats and hazards.
2. Give threats and hazards context.
3. Establish capability targets.
4. Assess capabilities.
5. Identify risks and address gaps.
6. Describe the impact of changes so leaders can prioritize what should be fixed first.

SalTHIRA is a practical budget-prioritization and decision-support method, not merely a risk list.

### SalOPS™ — Operational Readiness, Protective Services, and Incident Support

SalOPS implements approved actions, sustains readiness, provides protective and incident services, and feeds results, field reporting, and lessons back into SalCAPA.

## Approved information flow

Information and reporting sources enter SalCAPA. Relevant information and requirements flow to SalINTEL. Qualified intelligence informs SalTHIRA. Findings, assessments, priorities, and recommendations return through SalCAPA to Protected Members. Approved actions, requests, and tasks flow to SalOPS. Operational results and lessons return to SalCAPA.

## Governed enterprise learning and continuous improvement

Salus operates as a cumulative learning system across Protected Members, sectors, methods, strategies, objectives, tactics, resources, statutory and regulatory contexts, competing-resource conditions, outcomes, and derivatives. Each authorized engagement should test relevant prior knowledge and record transferable lessons; qualified lessons and subsequent field results should improve future research, assessments, services, and decisions. Cross-domain connections and counterexamples are part of the method, not isolated repositories.

The learning loop is: capture source-bound observations and results; generalize member-neutral patterns; compare them with other sectors and domains; qualify provenance, applicability, confidence, currency, restrictions and conflicts; approve controlled changes; apply the current version; and measure results and feed lessons back through SalCAPA. Preserve version history and superseded material. Record what was not searched or remains unknown instead of asserting an exhaustive view.

Salus may correlate authorized information internally across engagements, but enterprise learning does not grant automatic cross-Protected-Member disclosure. Keep identifiable Protected Member evidence and security-sensitive derivatives segregated. A reusable enterprise record may cite a restricted source without copying its protected contents. Apply legal, contractual, privacy, licensing, access, purpose and human disclosure controls to every derivative. Human approval remains required for consequential methodology changes, final findings, recommendations, risk acceptance, external publication and service commitments. See SAL-D025 and the approved Knowledge and Records Architecture.

## Chat-derived records and reporting currency

Chat discussions, user corrections, field observations and generated work may propose program records, but do not become approved doctrine merely by appearing in a conversation. SalCAPA shall preserve a traceable candidate record, check it against governing decisions and artifacts, record supporting and conflicting evidence, qualify customer scope and reuse rights, and route it to an accountable reviewer. Disposition and implementation state must be visible in the SalCAPA dashboard. Approved generalized lessons may inform other engagements through the governed learning loop; restricted originals remain segregated. See SAL-D026.

Every new or materially revised report must use the latest approved applicable Salus method, template and reporting standard. A material improvement prompts an impact check of active drafts and issued reports. Analysts document the decision to revise, supplement, or retain an issued report; any revised release is versioned and linked to its predecessor. Preserve immutable issued versions, source provenance, reviewer authority and customer boundaries. Client-facing DOCX, PDF and print/save outputs must agree in substance and be checked for legibility, accessibility, navigation and rendered pages. SalCAPA shall surface applicability, review and delivery status. See SAL-D027.

## Approved presentation and three-level service model

The approved seven-slide `Salus System and Methodolgy.pptx` is the visual methodology authority as of 27 September 2026 (SAL-D028; exact artifact and hash in APPROVED_ARTIFACTS.md). The three SalTHIRA phases group the six steps; they do not define commercial service levels.

Organize new SalINTEL, SalTHIRA, SalOPS, SalCAPA-mediated proposals, work orders, reports and product design around Level 1 Initial Pass, Level 2 Focused Assessment, and Level 3 Deep Dive, using the bounded, service-specific framework in `SALUS_THREE_LEVEL_SERVICE_FRAMEWORK.md` (SAL-D029). Protected Membership plans are separate entitlements; any level may be included, discounted or separately quoted. All levels retain applicable source, analyst, human-review, security, safety and disclosure controls. A level change, price, partner, staffing or operational promise requires explicit authority. Record impact on active and issued reports under SAL-D027. Approval of this model does not establish software deployment or published prices.

For every work package, record the requested, authorized and completed level, boundary, evidence profile, analyst/reviewer, estimate, deliverable and stop or escalation gate. Level 1 provides a source-bound baseline and applicability screen; Level 2 resolves selected material questions through targeted collection and validation; Level 3 addresses approved complex modules, interdependencies, sensitivity and residual risk. Reports must state what was completed, unresolved, excluded and proposed for a later level. ## Sequential integrated service packages and level labeling

Every customer first engages in a Level 1 package. A qualified Level 1 package is the prerequisite for Level 2; a qualified Level 2 package is the prerequisite for Level 3. Existing lawful, current and reliable evidence may be reviewed and accepted to satisfy lower-level checks, but the lower-level package, same-level product handoffs and human qualification must still be documented. Do not duplicate collection solely to recreate a level. This sequential rule supersedes earlier direct-entry language in the three-level framework (SAL-D032).

At **each level**, SalINTEL qualifies the evidence and identifies current conclusions and missing information; SalTHIRA applies all six steps at that same level to establish capability targets, assess gaps, compare treatments and set priorities with level-appropriate confidence; SalOPS provides same-level recommendations and options, and performs only separately authorized activities. SalOPS actions may be urgent operational measures or documented prerequisites for the next package. SalCAPA tracks the integrated package, component statuses, prerequisites, handoffs, decisions, prices, approved actions, report versions and lessons. SalINTEL completion is not a reason to defer all SalTHIRA or SalOPS work until a deeper level. A required safety action is never held for a commercial upgrade.

Every proposal, work order, work product and report prominently states the **level represented**, requested/authorized/completed status and prerequisite package in its title or front matter and executive summary. Each material section marks where its evidence and analysis support the current level, what needs the next level, and any SalOPS action needed now or to enable progression. Report statements must distinguish a working Level 1 picture from a completed qualified Level 1 package and from Level 2/3 determinations. Refine and approve the applicable methodologies and report standards first. Then issue the level-labeled SalINTEL report, the level-labeled SalTHIRA report, and the level-labeled SalOPS report in order. SalCAPA finally aggregates their approved findings, priorities, decisions, limits, actions and next-level needs into a level-labeled SalCAPA report. Link exact product versions and reconcile disagreements; aggregation does not substitute for the three product reports or duplicate an existing governed report (SAL-D033). Marketing and pricing must describe the required sequence and each level's actual scope, value, limits and human-approved price; they may not present Level 2 or Level 3 as a stand-alone first purchase. Protected Membership entitlements remain a separate dimension.

## Customer education, insider-risk and trusted-access method

Every SalINTEL research area shall teach the Protected Member the relevant concept, why it matters to that member's mission, the credible concern and evidence limit, practical strategy and controls, the decision it informs, and the information that would change the conclusion at the next level. Explain terms in plain language and distinguish observed facts, reported conditions, inference, unknowns and work outside scope. This education is part of the analysis and report body, not a questionnaire dump or generic warning (SAL-D034).

Every organizational or facility assessment shall inventory documented and plausible role families across leadership, officers, managers, staff, volunteers, committees, partner responders, vendors, contractors, subcontractors and temporary personnel. For each role, map governing organization, function, authority, physical/digital/financial/data access, supervision, alternates, duration, controls and evidence state. Explain role-specific exposure and observable control anomalies without treating a position, identity, affiliation or rumor as proof of misconduct. Include third-party sponsors, contracts, least privilege, logs, incident reporting and offboarding. Individual background, misconduct or insider-threat investigation requires its own lawful, bounded purpose, human authorization, contrary-evidence review, restricted case record and fair correction route. SalINTEL qualifies the evidence; SalTHIRA assesses scenario/capability implications at the authorized level; SalOPS develops approved preventive, protective or corrective options; SalCAPA controls access, decisions and learning.

## Stand-alone report confidentiality and pagination

Every stand-alone SalINTEL, SalTHIRA, SalOPS and final SalCAPA report shall have a dedicated Salus Confidential cover sheet stating product, represented level, Protected Member, version and release status; who is approved to view it; permitted use and distribution; storage; restricted annex treatment; and release authority. “Salus Confidential” is a company handling designation, not a government classification or CUI marking unless separate competent authority establishes one. **Page 2 shall be entirely blank, with no header, footer or page indication; the report body begins on page 3.** The cover and body use the required “page X of Y” format. This layout is a rendered-page release gate, not a text-only assertion (SAL-D035).

Use the exact client-facing SalTHIRA title and filename pattern **`[Protected Member customer name] - SalTHIRA Level [1, 2, or 3]`**. The customer name varies by engagement; for a given customer, only the level number changes across this report type. Do not add “Facility Assessment,” working-status wording, date, version or internal descriptors to the report title or filename. Put completion status, date, version and release authority on the confidential cover and in the governed record. Apply the rule to active drafts, future templates and delivery without duplicating an existing report record (SAL-D041).

## Integrated organizational, personnel, capability and external-context assessment

Every authorized Salus engagement shall examine the organization that operates a facility, service or mission as well as its physical assets. The scoped SalINTEL collection plan must identify leadership and governance; legal and practical command authority; succession and alternates; workforce, volunteers, contractors and trusted access; finance and decision controls; crisis communications; essential functions; technology and records; partners; and lessons from incidents and exercises. Test whether stated roles and plans can operate during simultaneous failures or loss of key people. Distinguish adopted authority and measured performance from proposals, descriptions and assumptions.

Use the five mission areas—Prevention, Protection, Mitigation, Response and Recovery—and FEMA's 32 core capabilities as an **applicability screen**, supplemented by sector- and organization-specific capabilities. Planning, Public Information and Warning, and Operational Coordination cut across all five. For each capability record applicability, owner or external partner, required outcome and timing, current evidence, limitations, dependencies, gap, confidence and an explicit reason for any not-applicable disposition. This screen does not imply every Protected Member must independently provide all 32, nor does it substitute for the six SalTHIRA steps or authorize unsupported scores. Preparedness, training, exercises, governance, personnel and finance underpin the mission areas.

Personnel and insider-risk considerations are part of organizational resilience. Map roles with access to people, money, facilities, vehicles, aircraft, animals, data, warning systems and incident authority. Review role-based screening, credentials, training, supervision, conflicts, access recertification, reporting, offboarding and fair response to concerns. An individual investigation requires a lawful purpose, authorized bounded level, relevant evidence, source provenance, corroboration, contradiction review, privacy and employment-law controls, qualified analyst review and a fair correction process. Salus's prior personnel-assessment work, including the Thomas R. Fox engagement, supplies transferable **scope and stopping principles, source weighting, identity adjudication, multi-hypothesis review, contrary-evidence analysis and negative-result discipline**, not person-specific findings or automatic authority to investigate other people. Apply relevant lenses to every trusted role without assuming either trustworthiness or misconduct from title or association; consider inadvertent compromise separately from malicious intent. Record supporting and exculpatory facts, alternative explanations, confidence, unassessed sources and review owner. “No evidence found” means a defined search produced no supported finding; “not assessed” means the question remains open. Do not turn belief, association, protected status, rumor, directory data or unverified allegations into an adverse conclusion. Human approval is required before labeling, restriction or disclosure. Keep identifiable personnel and investigation records in their Protected Member boundary.

For every material scenario, trace external context from the facility and neighborhood through county/region, Texas, United States and global systems. Examine actual local economic and infrastructure conditions, public-service competition, civic and political decisions that affect operations or funding, law and regulation, insurance, labor, utilities, transport, health care, communications, agriculture, technology, vendors and supply chains. Trace each relevant upstream condition to a named function, dependency, lead time, alternate, cost, trigger and accountable decision. Use FEMA Community Lifelines and sector guidance where applicable. A broad trend is context, not proof of local impact; research must record sources checked, time bounds, unavailable sources and unresolved unknowns. No assessment can claim to have reviewed the entire internet or every Salus record.

### Proactive SalINTEL research and contradiction review

During each active, authorized SalINTEL engagement, the analyst shall collect relevant, lawful public information without waiting for a separate request for every search. Use official primary records where available; include appropriate external reports, local and global open sources, member insight, authorized human sources and approved Salus tools. Record the entity and jurisdiction searched, terms or source classes, retrieval and event dates, source version, effective period, coverage and blind spots. Recheck material time-sensitive claims before issue. This standing research duty is bounded by the engagement purpose, level, privacy, terms of access and available capacity; it does not create perpetual monitoring, unrestricted personal investigation or an exhaustive-internet claim.

Across applicable SalINTEL domains, collect and explain eight evidence dimensions: (1) essential-function failure thresholds, backup endurance and restoration time; (2) physical, cyber, geographic and logical dependencies and cascades; (3) observed incidents, exercises, near misses, corrective actions and retest results; (4) public-service, mutual-aid and supplier availability under concurrent regional demand; (5) aggregate population reach, accessible warning, assistance and accountability, with person-level information restricted; (6) digital systems, data integrity, privileged access, vendors, backup and recovery; (7) animal-health, biosecurity, water and environmental interfaces where applicable; and (8) financial, insurance, contract, permit, labor and procurement feasibility of response and recovery. At Level 1, screen each relevant dimension and identify the decision and missing proof; at Level 2, validate selected material claims and performance; at Level 3, conduct separately approved specialist, compound or sensitivity work. Educate the Protected Member on the meaning, controls and decisions for each applicable area. [SAL-D038]

Compare each material external finding against member files, earlier report versions and relevant government records. Keep a claim-level contradiction record with both source locators, dates, exact disagreement, plausible reasons, decision effect, owner, resolution attempt and open status. Do not silently choose a convenient source or call a later source a contradiction when it merely reports a changed condition. Unresolved conflicts reduce confidence and remain visible in client reporting, with protected details in the correct annex.

### Dedicated SalINTEL compliance assessment and report section

Every SalINTEL report shall include a dedicated compliance section, even when the finding is that applicability remains unresolved. The section is a structured research and evidence assessment, not a legal opinion or an allegation.

Screen potential noncompliance as a risk question: identify the exact standard, permit, rule, statute or contract term; issuing authority and jurisdiction; effective date; affected asset or activity; applicability or exemption; observed conduct or missing record; conflicting evidence; potential consequence; and competent reviewer. Distinguish binding obligations from voluntary guidance and private policy. Absence of a supplied permit, inspection or test does not prove absence of the document or a violation. Label supported facts, possible gaps and verified findings separately; seek owner and qualified legal, engineering, health or regulatory review before alleging a violation. Escalate credible urgent life-safety concerns through authorized channels. [SAL-D038]

### SalTHIRA-ready SalINTEL evidence contract

At every authorized service level, SalINTEL shall prepare one traceable input chain for each priority scenario: threat or hazard and selection rationale; operating context, exposed assets and essential functions; plausible consequences and their baseline or range; measurable target inputs; demonstrated current capability; gap and root-cause hypotheses; and no-action and improvement-option inputs. Use stable IDs linking the scenario, asset, function, capability, source, assumption, contradiction and collection question. Distinguish observed event, tested performance, reported plan, model, inference, unknown and not assessed. State the likelihood basis only where supported; do not fill missing facts with a draft score. [SAL-D039]

Collect for **Step 1** local history, forecasts, mechanisms, planning horizon and inclusion/exclusion reasons. For **Step 2**, collect mapped exposure, geography, onset, duration, season, concurrent conditions, aggregate people and animals, essential-service interruption and consequence measures with uncertainty. For **Step 3**, collect minimum safe outcome, denominator, measurable unit, scale and time, tolerable interruption, partner assumption and decision authority; SalTHIRA approves the target. For **Step 4**, collect authority and plans, people by shift, qualifications, equipment and maintenance, training, exercise and incident results, capacity and endurance, agreements and external availability under simultaneous demand. For **Step 5**, collect current-versus-target comparison inputs, control effectiveness, root-cause alternatives, contrary evidence and consequence of a shortfall. For **Step 6**, collect no-action baseline, feasible options, cost and lead-time ranges, permits, funding, expected performance gain, tradeoffs, residual risk and proof of effectiveness. SalINTEL qualifies the facts and unresolved questions; SalTHIRA performs the assessment and prioritization.

A Level 1 handoff may use bounded qualitative consequences and provisional target inputs if uncertainty and its decision effect are explicit. Level 2 validates selected material parameters and performance; Level 3 examines approved compound, specialist and sensitivity cases. Review readiness for each of the six steps before same-level handoff and name the collector, custodian, reviewer and next-level validation for every material gap. Urgent authorized protective action is not delayed. The six-step process is Salus methodology; FEMA CPG 201 describes a three-step THIRA and a separate three-step SPR. Older Salus drafts and scoring workbooks do not authorize formula weights, tax or insurance conclusions, ROI claims or statements of federal method compliance. The approved Charter, presentation, Decision Register and current qualified evidence govern. [SAL-D039]

## Protection Advisor

Protection Advisor is a public marketing, initial-intake, and decision-support tool for prospective Protected Members.

- Part 1 is an anonymous initial profile and risk picture.
- Part 2 is a detailed, authenticated assessment.
- Recommendations must differ based on answers and may recommend service Levels 1, 2, or 3.
- Scoring and analysis must reflect the five mission areas and the 32 core capabilities.
- Collected information is retained indefinitely under the approved policy.
- Customer-facing results must be useful, professional, and understandable.
- The governed report must include an approved cover, confidentiality treatment, executive summary, identity and scope, methodology and limitations, findings, strengths, gaps, unresolved information needs, prioritized recommendations, service recommendations with rationale, implementation sequence, next steps, and a structured answer appendix.

## Protection of Salus methods, strategies and report assets

Salus shall protect its original work and commercially valuable confidential know-how throughout the complete service cycle. Maintain a rights-and-disclosure inventory for each method, model, rubric, scoring weight, scenario library, prompt, tool configuration, graphic, template, report and software component. Record creator, owner or license basis, third-party and Protected Member contributions, version, repository, intended audience, contractual restrictions and disclosure history. Do not assume that a report notice transfers ownership or that a Salus label converts public or customer information into Salus property. The source and contract rights of each contributor control.

Apply four practical handling tiers: **Public** (approved marketing and high-level service descriptions); **Protected Member deliverable** (the authorized, member-specific findings, rationale, limits and actions); **Salus internal confidential** (reusable templates, detailed work instructions and draft strategy); and **Salus restricted trade secret candidate** (nonpublic, economically valuable implementation details such as proprietary models, weighting, source-selection and validation procedures, tool configuration and internal playbooks). Classify by actual content and value, not filename. Keep the member's restricted evidence in its own engagement boundary even when Salus intellectual property appears in the same workstream. A trade-secret claim depends on continuing reasonable secrecy controls and may be lost by uncontrolled disclosure; no blanket label makes a disclosed idea secret. Copyright can protect original report expression, but not ideas, systems or methods themselves. Brand names and logos are addressed through service-mark or trademark clearance and registration review. Patentable inventions, if any, require separate counsel-led evaluation before public disclosure. [SAL-D037]

### Controls through the service cycle

1. **Design and creation:** use controlled repositories and version history; record authorship, assignment and contractor rights; clear third-party text, graphics, data and software licenses; keep restricted algorithms, full prompts, weights and internal source methods out of public decks and member reports. Counsel evaluates copyright registration and trademark filings for the specific assets that justify the cost.
2. **Sales, marketing and pricing:** describe the Level 1 → Level 2 → Level 3 sequence, customer outcomes, scope and limitations without publishing complete templates, proprietary scoring rules, pricing formulas or operational playbooks. Approve sample reports as deliberately reduced, de-identified examples. Require appropriate confidentiality terms before a prospect or partner receives restricted implementation detail; log the exact materials shown.
3. **SalCAPA™ intake and records:** maintain separate Salus-IP and Protected Member records, least-privilege roles, multifactor access, encryption, share controls, recipient and version logs, backups, revocation and retention. Restrict download/export of internal methods where feasible and alert on unusual access. A customer report, Salus method, third-party source and person-level case record have different owners and permissions even when linked for provenance.
4. **SalINTEL™:** teach each research concept and show the evidence, uncertainty and decision basis the member needs, while keeping the internal search playbook, weights, source-selection rules, proprietary-tool configuration and reusable prompts in controlled analyst records. Record what was disclosed. Client education and auditability do not require publication of a complete trade-secret implementation.
5. **SalTHIRA™:** provide credible scenarios, capability targets, evidence limits, gap and treatment rationale, priorities and decision authority. Keep internal scoring calibration, weighting, scenario libraries and reusable analytic worksheets in the restricted method record unless expressly licensed for delivery.
6. **SalOPS™:** deliver the approved plan, tasks, training and operating instructions needed for the member to act safely. Separate member-specific operating material from Salus background playbooks and reusable tools. Give vendors and partners only the minimum approved information; use confidentiality, permitted-use, subcontractor flow-down, return/deletion and offboarding controls in reviewed agreements.
7. **Reports and release:** every stand-alone report cover identifies owner/rights notice, authorized recipients, permitted internal and operational use, prohibited external redistribution or method extraction absent written authorization, controlled storage, release status, version and contact for additional permission. Include a concise copyright notice for original Salus-authored expression, subject to third-party and member rights. The executed engagement agreement, not a unilateral report legend, defines binding ownership, licenses, operational sharing and exceptions. Provide a controlled route for necessary sharing with counsel, insurers, regulators, responders or other authorized parties, including a redacted/action-only extract where appropriate; never impede urgent life-safety communication. Use recipient-specific copy identifiers or watermarks where justified, remove hidden metadata and comments, preserve a master, and log delivery. The required DOCX, PDF and print outputs remain substantively consistent.
8. **Review, departure and suspected misuse:** periodically recheck access, contract terms, public exposure, prior disclosures and whether a claimed secret remains secret. Revoke access and recover devices or copies when roles or engagements end. If leakage or copying is suspected, preserve logs and the original work, contain access, assess customer and third-party duties, and involve counsel before making infringement or misconduct allegations. SalCAPA records the incident, decision and corrective action.

Legal review is required before adopting standard confidentiality, intellectual-property, license, subcontractor or enforcement clauses and before filing registrations. No watermark, copyright notice, confidentiality legend or access control guarantees that a competitor cannot independently develop a similar method. [SAL-D037]

For reports and printable documents, apply the approved Salus visual authority together with applicable U.S. Office of Personnel Management federal guidance for clear, readable document style and plain language. Use the Salus confidentiality treatment and verify the final rendered DOCX, PDF and print pages. Do not label a document as government-classified solely because it follows a federal style guide.

- Use the correct Salus logo, balanced white space, readable type, and orange action controls.
- Avoid cartoonish graphics, overlapping elements, broken word wrapping, and raw technical presentation.
- Display version information on the left margin where required.
- Use day-month-year dates and 24-hour time.
- Use “Protected Members,” not superseded client labels.

### Salus report typography and federal-style treatment

Use **Times New Roman** for body paragraphs, table body, notes and page footers. Apply the same rule to report templates and maintain readable sizing, hierarchy, whitespace, accessibility, Salus branding and required confidentiality treatment. Validate the rendered DOCX, PDF and print/save results, including the blank second page and “page X of Y” indications where required. This font pair is Salus's prescribed implementation of Marc's federal-style preference; reviewed OPM materials contain document-specific instructions and do not establish a universal OPM rule for this exact pair.
