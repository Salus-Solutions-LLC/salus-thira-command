# Salus Solutions — Master Program Charter

## Purpose

Salus Solutions LLC is an intelligence, security, investigations, emergency-management, and operational-support company. The Salus program turns fragmented information into qualified intelligence, prioritized risk decisions, coordinated actions, and defensible records for Protected Members.

## Approved operating architecture

### Protected Members

Protected Members are the people and organizations Salus serves. In architecture graphics, Protected Members appear at the top.

### SalCAPA™ — Secure Coordination Environment

SalCAPA provides the secure environment for intake, exchange, coordination, delivery, and records. Its operating cycle is Collect, Analyze, Prioritize, Act. Approved expressions include “See • Coordinate • Act” and “See, Decide, Coordinate, Act, Prove.”

Primary member-facing outputs:

- Intelligence and alerts.
- Assessments and priorities.
- Workflows and requests.
- Salus experts, support, and service outcomes.

### SalINTEL™ — Qualified Intelligence

SalINTEL converts member insight, human intelligence, OSINT, partner capability, and research into qualified and validated intelligence.

### SalTHIRA™ — Threat and Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment

SalTHIRA evaluates threats, hazards, vulnerabilities, capabilities, consequences, priorities, and recommendations using six steps:

1. Identify threats and hazards.
2. Give threats and hazards context.
3. Establish capability targets.
4. Assess capabilities.
5. Identify risks and address gaps.
6. Describe the impact of changes so leaders can prioritize what should be fixed first.

SalTHIRA is a practical budget-prioritization and decision-support method, not merely a risk list.

### SalOPS™ — Operational Readiness, Protective Services, and Incident Support

SalOPS implements approved actions, sustains readiness, provides protective and incident services, and feeds results, field reporting, and lessons back into SalCAPA.

## Approved information flow

Information and reporting sources enter SalCAPA. Relevant information and requirements flow to SalINTEL. Qualified intelligence informs SalTHIRA. Findings, assessments, priorities, and recommendations return through SalCAPA to Protected Members. Approved actions, requests, and tasks flow to SalOPS. Operational results and lessons return to SalCAPA.

## Governed enterprise learning and continuous improvement

Salus operates as a cumulative learning system across Protected Members, sectors, methods, strategies, objectives, tactics, resources, statutory and regulatory contexts, competing-resource conditions, outcomes, and derivatives. Each authorized engagement should test relevant prior knowledge and record transferable lessons; qualified lessons and subsequent field results should improve future research, assessments, services, and decisions. Cross-domain connections and counterexamples are part of the method, not isolated repositories.

The learning loop is: capture source-bound observations and results; generalize member-neutral patterns; compare them with other sectors and domains; qualify provenance, applicability, confidence, currency, restrictions and conflicts; approve controlled changes; apply the current version; and measure results and feed lessons back through SalCAPA. Preserve version history and superseded material. Record what was not searched or remains unknown instead of asserting an exhaustive view.

Salus may correlate authorized information internally across engagements, but enterprise learning does not grant automatic cross-Protected-Member disclosure. Keep identifiable Protected Member evidence and security-sensitive derivatives segregated. A reusable enterprise record may cite a restricted source without copying its protected contents. Apply legal, contractual, privacy, licensing, access, purpose and human disclosure controls to every derivative. Human approval remains required for consequential methodology changes, final findings, recommendations, risk acceptance, external publication and service commitments. See SAL-D025 and the approved Knowledge and Records Architecture.

## Sequential integrated service packages and level labeling

Every customer first engages in a Level 1 package. A qualified Level 1 package is the prerequisite for Level 2; a qualified Level 2 package is the prerequisite for Level 3. Existing lawful, current and reliable evidence may be reviewed and accepted to satisfy lower-level checks, but the lower-level package, same-level product handoffs and human qualification must still be documented. Do not duplicate collection solely to recreate a level. This sequential rule supersedes earlier direct-entry language in the three-level framework (SAL-D032).

At **each level**, SalINTEL qualifies the evidence and identifies current conclusions and missing information; SalTHIRA applies all six steps at that same level to establish capability targets, assess gaps, compare treatments and set priorities with level-appropriate confidence; SalOPS provides same-level recommendations and options, and performs only separately authorized activities. SalOPS actions may be urgent operational measures or documented prerequisites for the next package. SalCAPA tracks the integrated package, component statuses, prerequisites, handoffs, decisions, prices, approved actions, report versions and lessons. SalINTEL completion is not a reason to defer all SalTHIRA or SalOPS work until a deeper level. A required safety action is never held for a commercial upgrade.

Every proposal, work order, work product and report prominently states the **level represented**, requested/authorized/completed status and prerequisite package in its title or front matter and executive summary. Each material section marks where its evidence and analysis support the current level, what needs the next level, and any SalOPS action needed now or to enable progression. Report statements must distinguish a working Level 1 picture from a completed qualified Level 1 package and from Level 2/3 determinations. Refine and approve the applicable methodologies and report standards first. Then issue the level-labeled SalINTEL report, the level-labeled SalTHIRA report, and the level-labeled SalOPS report in order. SalCAPA finally aggregates their approved findings, priorities, decisions, limits, actions and next-level needs into a level-labeled SalCAPA report. Link exact product versions and reconcile disagreements; aggregation does not substitute for the three product reports or duplicate an existing governed report (SAL-D033). Marketing and pricing must describe the required sequence and each level's actual scope, value, limits and human-approved price; they may not present Level 2 or Level 3 as a stand-alone first purchase. Protected Membership entitlements remain a separate dimension.

## Customer education, insider-risk and trusted-access method

Every SalINTEL research area shall teach the Protected Member the relevant concept, why it matters to that member's mission, the credible concern and evidence limit, practical strategy and controls, the decision it informs, and the information that would change the conclusion at the next level. Explain terms in plain language and distinguish observed facts, reported conditions, inference, unknowns and work outside scope. This education is part of the analysis and report body, not a questionnaire dump or generic warning (SAL-D034).

Every organizational or facility assessment shall inventory documented and plausible role families across leadership, officers, managers, staff, volunteers, committees, partner responders, vendors, contractors, subcontractors and temporary personnel. For each role, map governing organization, function, authority, physical/digital/financial/data access, supervision, alternates, duration, controls and evidence state. Explain role-specific exposure and observable control anomalies without treating a position, identity, affiliation or rumor as proof of misconduct. Include third-party sponsors, contracts, least privilege, logs, incident reporting and offboarding. Individual background, misconduct or insider-threat investigation requires its own lawful, bounded purpose, human authorization, contrary-evidence review, restricted case record and fair correction route. SalINTEL qualifies the evidence; SalTHIRA assesses scenario/capability implications at the authorized level; SalOPS develops approved preventive, protective or corrective options; SalCAPA controls access, decisions and learning.

## Stand-alone report confidentiality and pagination

Every stand-alone SalINTEL, SalTHIRA, SalOPS and final SalCAPA report shall have a dedicated Salus Confidential cover sheet stating product, represented level, Protected Member, version and release status; who is approved to view it; permitted use and distribution; storage; restricted annex treatment; and release authority. “Salus Confidential” is a company handling designation, not a government classification or CUI marking unless separate competent authority establishes one. **Page 2 shall be entirely blank, with no header, footer or page indication; the report body begins on page 3.** The cover and body use the required “page X of Y” format. This layout is a rendered-page release gate, not a text-only assertion (SAL-D035).

## Integrated organizational, personnel, capability and external-context assessment

Every authorized Salus engagement shall examine the organization that operates a facility, service or mission as well as its physical assets. The scoped SalINTEL collection plan must identify leadership and governance; legal and practical command authority; succession and alternates; workforce, volunteers, contractors and trusted access; finance and decision controls; crisis communications; essential functions; technology and records; partners; and lessons from incidents and exercises. Test whether stated roles and plans can operate during simultaneous failures or loss of key people. Distinguish adopted authority and measured performance from proposals, descriptions and assumptions.

Use the five mission areas—Prevention, Protection, Mitigation, Response and Recovery—and FEMA's 32 core capabilities as an **applicability screen**, supplemented by sector- and organization-specific capabilities. Planning, Public Information and Warning, and Operational Coordination cut across all five. For each capability record applicability, owner or external partner, required outcome and timing, current evidence, limitations, dependencies, gap, confidence and an explicit reason for any not-applicable disposition. This screen does not imply every Protected Member must independently provide all 32, nor does it substitute for the six SalTHIRA steps or authorize unsupported scores. Preparedness, training, exercises, governance, personnel and finance underpin the mission areas.

Personnel and insider-risk considerations are part of organizational resilience. Map roles with access to people, money, facilities, vehicles, aircraft, animals, data, warning systems and incident authority. Review role-based screening, credentials, training, supervision, conflicts, access recertification, reporting, offboarding and fair response to concerns. An individual investigation requires a lawful purpose, authorized bounded level, relevant evidence, source provenance, corroboration, contradiction review, privacy and employment-law controls, qualified analyst review and a fair correction process. Salus's prior personnel-assessment work, including the Thomas R. Fox engagement, supplies transferable **scope and stopping principles, source weighting, identity adjudication, multi-hypothesis review, contrary-evidence analysis and negative-result discipline**, not person-specific findings or automatic authority to investigate other people. Apply relevant lenses to every trusted role without assuming either trustworthiness or misconduct from title or association; consider inadvertent compromise separately from malicious intent. Record supporting and exculpatory facts, alternative explanations, confidence, unassessed sources and review owner. “No evidence found” means a defined search produced no supported finding; “not assessed” means the question remains open. Do not turn belief, association, protected status, rumor, directory data or unverified allegations into an adverse conclusion. Human approval is required before labeling, restriction or disclosure. Keep identifiable personnel and investigation records in their Protected Member boundary.

For every material scenario, trace external context from the facility and neighborhood through county/region, Texas, United States and global systems. Examine actual local economic and infrastructure conditions, public-service competition, civic and political decisions that affect operations or funding, law and regulation, insurance, labor, utilities, transport, health care, communications, agriculture, technology, vendors and supply chains. Trace each relevant upstream condition to a named function, dependency, lead time, alternate, cost, trigger and accountable decision. Use FEMA Community Lifelines and sector guidance where applicable. A broad trend is context, not proof of local impact; research must record sources checked, time bounds, unavailable sources and unresolved unknowns. No assessment can claim to have reviewed the entire internet or every Salus record.

### Product responsibilities

- **SalCAPA™** captures organization/role and capability records, source lineage, external dependencies, questions, approvals, changes, actions, report versions and lessons in the correct member boundary. Its dashboard shows applicability and evidence status, not unsupported completion or deployed capability.
- **SalINTEL™** researches and reconciles member insight, human sources, external reports, OSINT, approved proprietary tools and global partner inputs; maps personnel/leadership, insider-risk controls, local-to-global dependencies and all applicable capability questions; qualifies facts, inference, contradictions and gaps through human analyst validation.
- **SalTHIRA™** applies all six approved steps to validated scenarios, establishes capability targets, tests actual capability and competing resources, compares strategies and consequences, and records uncertainty, residual risk and the Member's decision authority. The 32-capability screen is a planning aid; sector-specific capabilities are added where needed.
- **SalOPS™** turns authorized decisions into preparedness, protective, incident/crisis-response, recovery and sustainment work, including plans, training, exercises, crisis communications, personnel controls, continuity, mitigation, corrective actions and measured retests. Results return through SalCAPA to SalINTEL and SalTHIRA.

At every service level, make the collection boundary and capability dispositions explicit. A lower commercial level may defer deeper investigation, but it must not silently omit an applicable life-safety issue, human qualification or required authorization. Apply approved improvements to active drafts and impact-check issued reports under SAL-D027; preserve release history and customer isolation.

## Protection Advisor

Protection Advisor is a public marketing, initial-intake, and decision-support tool for prospective Protected Members.

- Part 1 is an anonymous initial profile and risk picture.
- Part 2 is a detailed, authenticated assessment.
- Recommendations must differ based on answers and may recommend service Levels 1, 2, or 3.
- Scoring and analysis must reflect the five mission areas and the 32 core capabilities.
- Collected information is retained indefinitely under the approved policy.
- Customer-facing results must be useful, professional, and understandable.
- The governed report must include an approved cover, confidentiality treatment, executive summary, identity and scope, methodology and limitations, findings, strengths, gaps, unresolved information needs, prioritized recommendations, service recommendations with rationale, implementation sequence, next steps, and a structured answer appendix.
- Raw questionnaire exports, internal contracts, JSON, and printed web pages are not acceptable reports.

## Investigation and assessment levels

Every assessment domain should define bounded service levels so scope and effort do not expand indefinitely. The standing model is:

- Level 1 — Initial Pass: rapid review using readily available information.
- Level 2 — Focused Assessment: deeper validation and targeted investigation.
- Level 3 — Deep Dive: comprehensive, corroborated assessment with expanded evidence and analysis.

Exact hours, sources, and deliverables must be stated in each scope.

## Governance principles

- GitHub is the technical source of truth.
- Human authority and accountable records are required for consequential decisions.
- Evidence lifecycle state cannot be forged through generic editing.
- Releases are validated, frozen, immutably published, deployed with verification, and paired with a rollback target.
- Approved artifacts control implementation details that prose alone may not capture.
- Client deliverables must be visually inspected when layout is material.

## Brand and interface standards

- Use a clean, airy, professional visual style aligned with `www.Salus.Solutions`.
- Use the correct Salus logo, balanced white space, readable type, and orange action controls.
- Avoid cartoonish graphics, overlapping elements, broken word wrapping, and raw technical presentation.
- Display version information on the left margin where required.
- Use day-month-year dates and 24-hour time.
- Use “Protected Members,” not superseded client labels.

## Protective Intelligence cadence

The standing Protective Intelligence update cadence is the tenth day of every month, with Green, Yellow, Orange, and Red escalation tied to protective actions.
