# Salus Solutions — Master Program Charter

## Purpose

Salus Solutions LLC is an intelligence, security, investigations, emergency-management, and operational-support company. The Salus program turns fragmented information into qualified intelligence, prioritized risk decisions, coordinated actions, and defensible records for Protected Members.

## Approved operating architecture

### Protected Members

Protected Members are the people and organizations Salus serves. In architecture graphics, Protected Members appear at the top.

### SalCAPA™ — Secure Coordination Environment

SalCAPA provides the secure environment for intake, exchange, coordination, delivery, and records. Its operating cycle is Collect, Analyze, Prioritize, Act. Approved expressions include “See • Coordinate • Act” and “See, Decide, Coordinate, Act, Prove.”

Primary member-facing outputs:

- Intelligence and alerts.
- Assessments and priorities.
- Workflows and requests.
- Salus experts, support, and service outcomes.

### SalINTEL™ — Qualified Intelligence

SalINTEL converts member insight, human intelligence, OSINT, partner capability, and research into qualified and validated intelligence.

### SalTHIRA™ — Threat and Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment

SalTHIRA evaluates threats, hazards, vulnerabilities, capabilities, consequences, priorities, and recommendations using six steps:

1. Identify threats and hazards.
2. Give threats and hazards context.
3. Establish capability targets.
4. Assess capabilities.
5. Identify risks and address gaps.
6. Describe the impact of changes so leaders can prioritize what should be fixed first.

SalTHIRA is a practical budget-prioritization and decision-support method, not merely a risk list.

### SalOPS™ — Operational Readiness, Protective Services, and Incident Support

SalOPS implements approved actions, sustains readiness, provides protective and incident services, and feeds results, field reporting, and lessons back into SalCAPA.

## Approved information flow

Information and reporting sources enter SalCAPA. Relevant information and requirements flow to SalINTEL. Qualified intelligence informs SalTHIRA. Findings, assessments, priorities, and recommendations return through SalCAPA to Protected Members. Approved actions, requests, and tasks flow to SalOPS. Operational results and lessons return to SalCAPA.

## Protection Advisor

Protection Advisor is a public marketing, initial-intake, and decision-support tool for prospective Protected Members.

- Part 1 is an anonymous initial profile and risk picture.
- Part 2 is a detailed, authenticated assessment.
- Recommendations must differ based on answers and may recommend service Levels 1, 2, or 3.
- Scoring and analysis must reflect the five mission areas and the 32 core capabilities.
- Collected information is retained indefinitely under the approved policy.
- Customer-facing results must be useful, professional, and understandable.
- The governed report must include an approved cover, confidentiality treatment, executive summary, identity and scope, methodology and limitations, findings, strengths, gaps, unresolved information needs, prioritized recommendations, service recommendations with rationale, implementation sequence, next steps, and a structured answer appendix.
- Raw questionnaire exports, internal contracts, JSON, and printed web pages are not acceptable reports.

## Investigation and assessment levels

Every assessment domain should define bounded service levels so scope and effort do not expand indefinitely. The standing model is:

- Level 1 — Initial Pass: rapid review using readily available information.
- Level 2 — Focused Assessment: deeper validation and targeted investigation.
- Level 3 — Deep Dive: comprehensive, corroborated assessment with expanded evidence and analysis.

Exact hours, sources, and deliverables must be stated in each scope.

## Governance principles

- GitHub is the technical source of truth.
- Human authority and accountable records are required for consequential decisions.
- Evidence lifecycle state cannot be forged through generic editing.
- Releases are validated, frozen, immutably published, deployed with verification, and paired with a rollback target.
- Approved artifacts control implementation details that prose alone may not capture.
- Client deliverables must be visually inspected when layout is material.

## Brand and interface standards

- Use a clean, airy, professional visual style aligned with `www.Salus.Solutions`.
- Use the correct Salus logo, balanced white space, readable type, and orange action controls.
- Avoid cartoonish graphics, overlapping elements, broken word wrapping, and raw technical presentation.
- Display version information on the left margin where required.
- Use day-month-year dates and 24-hour time.
- Use “Protected Members,” not superseded client labels.

## Protective Intelligence cadence

The standing Protective Intelligence update cadence is the tenth day of every month, with Green, Yellow, Orange, and Red escalation tied to protective actions.
