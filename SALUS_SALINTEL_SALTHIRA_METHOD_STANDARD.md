# SalINTEL and SalTHIRA Controlled Method and Report Standard

**Status:** Approved enterprise method and report-template structure, effective 27 September 2026 (SAL-D044); multidisciplinary assurance amendment approved 28 September 2026 (SAL-D050); organizational-health method approved 28 September 2026 (SAL-D051); analytical assumptions-to-action amendment approved 28 September 2026 (SAL-D055).  
**Owner:** Salus Solutions. **Handling:** Salus Internal Confidential.  
**Applies to:** Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3 work under the Program Charter. This record governs method and reusable structure; it does not approve a Protected Member finding, a price, operational task, software release, or report issue.

## Authority and use

Use this standard with `SALUS_PROGRAM_CHARTER.md`, `SALUS_DECISION_REGISTER.md`, `SALUS_THREE_LEVEL_SERVICE_FRAMEWORK.md`, the approved Salus system presentation, and the two controlled member-neutral Word templates registered in `APPROVED_ARTIFACTS.md`. The exact approved decision and supersession record controls conflicts. The current Tierra Linda SalINTEL and SalTHIRA reports are design/provenance inputs, not reusable factual evidence for another Protected Member. Keep originals, personal data, security-sensitive details and case findings in their segregated engagement record. Generalize only reviewed, sourced, member-neutral lessons under SAL-D025.

## Integrated level sequence

Every Protected Member begins with a qualified Level 1 package before Level 2; Level 2 precedes Level 3. At **each** level, SalINTEL qualifies evidence and open questions; SalTHIRA performs the six steps below at that level; SalOPS provides same-level recommendations and options and performs only separately authorized work; SalCAPA aggregates approved component findings after the three reports. Level 1 establishes a bounded, source-bound picture and provisional decisions. Level 2 validates selected material gaps through focused collection and performance proof. Level 3 addresses approved complex, compound, specialist and sensitivity questions. Each report shows the represented, requested, authorized and completed level, prerequisite status, evidence depth, confidence, next-level need, owner and release gate. Urgent authorized life-safety actions are not deferred for a commercial level upgrade.

## SalINTEL required process

1. **Scope and protect.** Define the Protected Member, engagement, essential functions, facilities, population groups in aggregate, time horizon, authorized sources and methods, law/privacy boundaries, access restrictions and review authority. Separate person-level evidence in restricted annexes.
2. **Collect and inventory.** Preserve every received source's identity, date, version, origin, hash where available, owner, license/access limits and relevance. Search relevant lawful public sources proactively during active authorized work; record the actual search boundary, retrieval date and blind spots. Sources include member insight, local context, human reporting, external reports, open sources, global/regional developments, approved proprietary tools and partner reporting. Outputs are leads until qualified.
3. **Research the organizational system.** Examine leadership, command, succession, finance, business processes, personnel and volunteer roles, vendor/contractor and trusted access, insider-control indicators, facilities, dependencies, crisis communications, preparedness, protection, response, recovery and mitigation. Consider local economy and infrastructure, politics, regulation, supply chains, insurance/finance, environmental and animal interfaces, aviation and transport, health/medical access, resource competition and compound events where applicable.
4. **Screen capability.** Address all five mission areas and all 32 core capabilities for applicability; add sector-specific capabilities. Compare plans and declared resources with demonstrated incident, exercise, maintenance and partner performance. A missing record is an unknown, not proof of failure or success.
5. **Collect eight evidence dimensions.** Document (a) essential-function interruption and restoration thresholds; (b) cascading physical, cyber and geographic dependencies; (c) incident, exercise and corrective-action performance; (d) responder and supplier availability under simultaneous demand; (e) aggregate population reach, accessible warning and assistance; (f) digital/data/vendor continuity; (g) animal-health and environmental interfaces where applicable; and (h) financial, contractual, insurance and permit feasibility.
6. **Qualify claims.** For each material claim record source, support, contradiction, currency, relevance, confidence, analyst interpretation, reviewer, limitations and recheck trigger. Resolve or carry forward material contradictions visibly.
7. **Assess compliance in a dedicated section.** Identify jurisdiction, authority, effective version/date, applicability, exemptions, exact duty, evidence, conflict and human review. Distinguish binding requirements from guidance. Do not assert a violation from a missing record or unsupported inference.
8. **Prepare the SalTHIRA handoff.** Link stable scenario, asset, function, capability, source, assumption and collection-question IDs. Collect inputs for scenario/context, measurable target and owner, demonstrated capacity, gap/root cause, options/no-action, cost/feasibility, expected change, residual risk and validation. Record missing proof and its decision effect.
9. **Educate in every domain.** Explain the concept, why it matters to this member, credible concern and evidence limit, practical control or strategy, decision informed and what next-level information could change the conclusion. Distinguish observed, reported, inferred and unassessed.
10. **Report and review.** Use the approved SalINTEL template. Include the executive summary, scope and level, organization and governance, roles/trusted access, assets/dependencies, hazards/actual incidents, capability and eight-dimension screens, dedicated compliance and contradiction sections, qualified findings, collection register, same-level handoff, sources and lessons. Qualify findings through accountable analyst/human review before issue.

## SalTHIRA required process

SalTHIRA owns the target, gap and priority decisions. Apply all six Salus steps at each authorized level:
1. Identify threats and hazards.
2. Give threats and hazards context for the Protected Member, timing, exposure, consequence and cascading dependencies.
3. Establish measurable capability targets with an accountable approval authority and stated assumptions.
4. Assess demonstrated current capability against targets, using people, organization/authority, plans, equipment, training, exercises, incidents and partner availability.
5. Identify risks and address gaps, causes, credible treatments, feasibility and residual risk.
6. Describe the impact of changes, comparing no action and options by expected gain, resources, time, dependencies, tradeoffs and validation.

Keep linked scenario decision records. Analyze five mission areas, all 32 core capabilities plus applicable sector capabilities, essential-function continuity and recovery times, regional competing demand, mitigation-plan connections, resource allocation and uncertainty. Rankings are transparent ordinal analyst judgments unless a separate approved quantitative method and evidence support more; they are not automatically probability, measured capability grade, legal conclusion, spending authority or formal FEMA compliance. Use reviewer challenge and sensitivity checks proportionate to level.

## Required report design

The SalTHIRA client-facing title and filename are `[Protected Member customer name] - SalTHIRA Level [1, 2, or 3]`; status, date and version appear separately. Both stand-alone products use a Salus Confidential approved-personnel cover, a **wholly blank second page**, body beginning page three and visible `page X of Y` on nonblank pages. Use Arial for titles, headings, running headers and table headings; Times New Roman for prose, table body, notes and footers. Maintain brand, plain language, accessibility, navigation, source citations and rendered DOCX/PDF/print parity when offered.

The SalTHIRA BLUF begins the body with ranked narrative and four separate numbered tables: hazard scenario priorities; six-step assessment-work priorities; five mission-area priorities; and all 32 core-capability priorities. Put each table's heading at a lower level than BLUF, state its purpose immediately above, keep title/purpose/header together and repeat headers on continuation pages. Follow with executive assessment, scope, SalINTEL readiness, assessment practice, six steps, connected scenario records, continuity/recovery, resource competition, capability screen, mitigation/contradictions, analyst review/SalOPS handoff and sources. Same-level SalOPS recommendations/options are required; execution needs separate approval.

## Multidisciplinary assurance screen [SAL-D050]

At every level, screen the applicability of professional lenses for enterprise risk and board decisions; compliance, anti-bribery and fraud; CFO/CPA and liquidity; insurance/claims; engineering, construction and adopted codes; regulators; emergency management and public safety; homeland security; FBI-informed behavioral threat assessment; energy assurance; sociology/community trust; behavioral and human factors; business continuity; cybersecurity/privacy/AI; public health/accessibility; environment/animal health; procurement/supply chain; and independent review. Use the dated primary-source and evidence matrix in `proposals/SALUS_MULTIDISCIPLINARY_ASSURANCE_REVIEW_20260928.md` as a research lead, not as a Member finding or automatic legal duty. Mark each lens applicable, not applicable, unknown or outside approved scope, with rationale and a specialist trigger. Level 1 screens existing authorized sources and names decision-changing unknowns; Level 2 validates selected questions; Level 3 tests approved compound cases and alternatives.

SalINTEL's dedicated compliance section shall link each candidate duty to the actual regulated entity/activity, authority, jurisdiction, threshold, effective date, adopted code/standard edition and local amendment, exemption, evidence, owner and qualified reviewer. Its eight dimensions shall explicitly prompt financial survival and insurability, trusted and accessible warning, energy/fuel endurance, and behavioral performance when material. Keep person-specific allegations in restricted records. SalTHIRA shall test correlated failure, distribution of consequences, liquidity and recovery feasibility, common-mode lifeline failure, and human performance in its six steps when applicable. The report shall show the lens screen, qualified evidence, contradictory sources, specialist decisions and next-level collection needs without adding unsupported numerical precision.

## Organizational health and command integration [SAL-D051]

In SalINTEL™, explain and screen the twelve domains in the approved organizational-health method. Establish legal and practical authority, succession and delegations, decision rights, process handoffs, the promulgation lifecycle, upward information flow, contractor and volunteer roles, controls, capacity, continuity and learning. Preserve strengths and contrary evidence. Trace representative decisions and changes at the authorized level; label document-only arrangements as unproven and unknowns as unknown. Include a titled organizational-health section with a domain/evidence/confidence table, ordinary and stressed-operation implications, ranked changes and exact next-level collection needs. Keep person-specific allegations in separately governed records.

In SalTHIRA™, carry qualified leadership, decision, communications and process effects through the existing six steps and applicable mission areas/core capabilities. Make explicit how authority failure, delayed decisions, misinformation, personnel absence or hidden dependencies change scenario consequences, targets, demonstrated capacity, gaps, treatment comparisons and residual risk. Do not substitute a generic organization score for capability evidence. Explain which SalOPS™ option or safeguard would change performance, who may authorize it, and how it will be retested.

## Protection Advisor organizational-health handoff [SAL-D052]

Accept Protection Advisor authority, ownership, reporting, leadership and exercise answers only as preliminary leads, with questionnaire version, consent and limitations. Within a qualified Level 1 scope, convert material leads into the twelve-domain evidence plan and stable finding IDs; preserve strengths, unknowns and contradictions. Transfer supported organizational effects to the existing six SalTHIRA™ steps and applicable capability targets, then to same-level SalOPS™ and SalCAPA™ by the crosswalk in `SALUS_ORGANIZATIONAL_HEALTH_PRODUCT_INTEGRATION.md`. An unverified answer or “not sure” response is not a performance rating, person-level allegation or service trigger.

## Required information and missing-proof control [SAL-D053]

Use `SALUS_REQUIRED_INFORMATION_AND_LENS_CROSSWALK.md` to tailor the Level 1 Member request after authorization and to track all applicable multidisciplinary lenses, twelve organizational-health domains, eight evidence dimensions, five mission areas and 32 core-capability applicability decisions. ## Template, change and release control

The registered Word templates are approved **structures and prompts**, not completed reports. Replace prompts with evidence-based member analysis or mark an item pending before issue. Never copy another member's findings, names, vulnerabilities or restricted details into a template. The analyst records the exact method/template version, source baseline, Charter conformance matrix, material contradictions, open release gates, reviewer and authorized recipients. Render and visually inspect every page; check fonts, blank page two, pagination, tables, accessibility and offered-format parity. Preserve one governed report identity, immutable issued versions, supersession, rollback and report-impact decisions. Material method or template changes require provenance, applicability, human approval, Decision Register and artifact updates, and impact review of active and issued reports.

## SAL-D054 discovery and scenario challenge

For SalINTEL™, apply the conditional sub-lens screen and source/uncertainty ledger in `SALUS_EXPANDED_LENS_DISCOVERY_METHOD_20260928.md`. Educate the Protected Member on each material concern and distinguish a planned capability from demonstrated incident/exercise performance. Trace dispatch, fire, EMS, HAZMAT, land use, animal operations, lifeline coupling, information integrity, volunteer safety, resource competition, community recovery and second-order effects when applicable. Investigate a contradiction before treating missing evidence as noncompliance. Person-level chat or case material stays restricted.

For SalTHIRA™, carry selected evidence and unknowns through the **existing six steps**, five mission areas and applicable core capabilities. Challenge the favored scenario with a compound failure, resource-constrained response, adverse treatment effect and plausible alternative explanation when each could change targets, gaps or priorities. Mark assumption, confidence and specialist trigger; do not invent a probability or claim an unmodeled surprise was identified. At Level 1 screen and rank; Level 2 validates selected decision-changing issues; Level 3 tests approved complex cases. Active Word templates and Member reports require separate SAL-D027/SAL-D043 review and release.

## SAL-D055 assumption-led recommendations and report expression

Apply `SALUS_ANALYTICAL_ASSUMPTIONS_TO_ACTION_STANDARD.md` to every material question and recommendation at the represented level. SalINTEL™ records the source-bound answer, contradiction, analytical working assumption, alternative explanation, confidence, validation test and decision effect; it educates the Member and recommends a specific proportionate immediate or next-level step. The missing-information table states the decision that each answer may change. In the SalINTEL™ executive summary and applicable sections, pair each priority recommendation's narrative with a verifiable action checklist and stable ID.

SalTHIRA™ carries the qualified assumption through all six steps and ranks how a true/false result would change the scenario, consequence, mission-area/core-capability target, gap and treatment. Its BLUF states the analyst's current-level judgment in narrative and checklist form, linked to the existing numbered tables; the checklist must not silently turn a proposed SalOPS™ option into an authorized task. Level 1 uses bounded assumptions to deliver useful qualified priorities and options; Levels 2 and 3 test and revise selected assumptions. Record no-action and feasible alternatives, affected groups, dependencies, professional/Member authority, proof and residual uncertainty. Maintain narrative/checklist parity in the controlled Word templates and any populated report after SAL-D027/SAL-D043 review.
