# Integrated Operational Legal and Financial Assessment Review

## Authority and application

SALUS-INTEGRATED-REVIEW-001 / SAL-D149. Directed by the Salus owner on 21 November 2026. Apply to every Salus assessment, at every level, including facility, personnel and organizational assessments, material reassessments, and all four report products. This exact implementation remains a candidate until governed promotion. The current owner direction authorizes its application to the current private assessment; it grants no external issue or runtime authority.

## Required integrated test

For every material finding and recommendation, examine operational feasibility, legal implications and financial requirements together. Identify the affected population, source and contrary evidence, assumed dependency, accountable decision role, receiving role, authority, available resources, funding or payment responsibility, fallback and completion proof. Test the interfaces between functions, including incompatible assumptions, unowned tasks, unfunded duties, unavailable partners, competing demands and records that do not establish actual performance. Record unknowns and their decision effect; missing information is not proof of failure.

Operational review must trace recognition, protective action, warning, command transfer, continuing care, recovery and mitigation as applicable. Examine routine conditions and degraded or simultaneous demand, including accessibility, children, visitors, staff, volunteers and contractors. A documented instruction, signed agreement, notification, purchase or completed discussion alone does not establish available capacity or successful delivery.

Legal review must identify the relevant jurisdiction, entity, activity and time period before suggesting a duty applies. Separate binding requirements, contractual duties, authority determinations, guidance, reported practice and Salus recommendations. Retain exact provenance, versions, contradictions and adverse evidence. Examine relevant authority, consent, custody, accessibility, privacy, record handling, contracts, insurance interfaces and retention without inventing a legal violation or immunity. Do not transfer a public-sector ruling or aviation requirement to another entity without applicability analysis. Risk management includes operational and financial exposure as well as legal exposure.

Financial review must identify initial and recurring cash needs, staff and volunteer capacity, training, maintenance, replacement, partner charges, disruption, recovery resources and residual exposure. Compare feasible alternatives, including use of existing resources and consequences of deferral; never describe inaction as cost-free. Identify the estimate basis, date, period, quantities, rates, assumptions, exclusions, funding owner and uncertainty. Volunteer time and donated resources may have capacity or opportunity costs even where no cash payment occurs. Avoid double-counting shared resources, loss categories or insurance recoveries. A purchase does not prove readiness; an agreement does not prove funding or availability.

Quantify cost-benefit, avoided loss or mitigation ROI only where defensible inputs support the chosen method. Distinguish cash flow, accounting treatment, cost-effectiveness, expected-loss reduction and investment return. State the baseline, counterfactual, useful life, discounting when relevant, uncertainty and sensitivity. Use ranges where supported, and label estimates separately from quotes, budgets, commitments and actuals. Where benefits or incident probabilities cannot responsibly be monetized, compare costs against observable capability or service outcomes and state that ROI is not established. Legal and life-safety obligations cannot be waived by an unfavorable ROI calculation.

## Joint decision and verification

Trace each material dependency to a named proposed or confirmed role and the evidence needed from that role. Do not assume that operations, counsel, finance or a partner has accepted a task. Reconcile conflicting decisions across those roles and record who can authorize action, spending, changes to scope and residual risk. When a dependency is unresolved, state a safe decision boundary, an available alternative if supported, and the specific proof required. This does not authorize the assistant to issue emergency instructions or delay immediate protective action pending administrative review.

Test relevant transitions against all five mission areas: Prevention, Protection, Response, Recovery and Mitigation. Preserve the six-step SalTHIRA method, priorities and stable finding IDs. Apply review depth proportionately without omitting a perspective; an explicit reasoned no-material-issue disposition is acceptable, a blank field is not.

Use existing records first. Record absent records and confirmations without making creation of a new plan, exercise, budget or legal opinion a condition for answering an evidence request. Any subsequent implementation, test or specialist engagement requires its own scope and authority.

## Four report responsibilities

SalINTEL establishes the source record, authority applicability, financial inputs, contradictions, currency and evidence limits. SalTHIRA evaluates scenarios, consequences, dependencies, capability gaps and uncertainty across the five mission areas. SalOPS compares feasible actions, costs and resource demands, ownership, authority, fallback and measurable proof. SalCAPA integrates the decision, rationale, alternatives, operational conditions, legal questions, financial implications, residual exposure and closure status. Use cross-references and concise summaries; do not repeat four complete analyses or add a duplicate recommendation register.

## Professional boundaries and assurance

These are analytical perspectives, not proof that an attorney, accountant, actuary, insurer or engineer performed a professional review. Identify when specialist judgment is needed, the specific question and its decision effect. Record actual reviewer role, scope, date and conclusions only when verified. Do not claim privilege, admissibility, regulatory acceptance, insurance coverage, accounting compliance or professional approval from this review alone.

The assistant owns routine integration, correction and verification. Retain finding-specific applicability, evidence and handoff dispositions in the restricted engagement record, using existing IDs. Verify each material finding and recommendation has all three perspectives or a reasoned disposition; test contrary evidence, uncertain costs and failed dependencies. Preserve source qualification, rights, Member isolation, eight substantive re-passes and the terminal editorial pass, approved typography, exact-file rendering and human release authority. Carry unresolved issues into all affected reports and SalCAPA's decision account. No new human attestation of assistant compliance is required.

## Implementation and learning

Record report/template impact and exact revised versions. Preserve predecessors and original Member documents. Member-neutral lessons may enter governed learning; case facts, personal data, source IDs, financial records and assessment binaries stay in their restricted boundary. A merged requirement does not establish universal implementation, qualified source use or deployed enforcement. Existing reader holds remain in force. Protected merges, deployments, disclosures, purchases and operational adoption retain separate authorization.
